## Canon near the PC (Vashri Crossing → the south road, the Silt Marches)
1. The drowned shrine-heart in the **south reed-channel** — Mahru's last home, now a bound vassal's seat; a latent lever for any future Mahru reckoning.
2. The **Akatha white rite-pavilion** travels with the column — Anuhya's god-binding circle, screaming-face jade beads, plum-blossom-and-altar-smoke.
3. The **Silt Marches' other gods** to be brought to heel — drowned and burning frontier gods the column marches to subjugate.
4. The **relief column**: a legion + Akatha rite-cadre + a **House Burano** contingent (Ravan's reach onto the campaign).
5. **Vashri Crossing** itself — granary-turned-fort at the ford; Prefect Sesrina holds it as the column moves on.
6. The **11 freed dead** — given rites at dawn (Akhil oversaw); their families and the survived cohort remember.
7. The grieving **cohort / field-infirmary** — Ophris's domain and his people; morale after the Night of Mahru.
