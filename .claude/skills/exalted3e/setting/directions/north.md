# North — Lunar Home Turf (PRIORITIZED)

Direction of the **Elemental Pole of Air**: tundra, ice, hardy iron-rich peoples, poor crops. The Realm's grip is thin and thinning, the Wyld and Underworld press close, and the **Bull of the North** (a Solar warlord) and the **Silver Pact** are active here — making it the default theatre for solo-Lunar play. Place cards below; Lunar **dominions** proper (Iscomay, Skandhar-Bhal, Touman, etc.) are in `lunar_dominions.md`; factions in `../04_factions.md`.

---

### The North — Direction · Elemental Pole of Air · Danger: moderate→lethal (winter/night)
Tone: bitter wind, pine-scrub giving way to endless tundra; gods you must feed, and a night that hunts.
Surface: three zones — **Near North** (between Inland & White Seas; culture, population, cruel winters, scattered Realm satrapies trading autonomy for protection) → **Far North** (beyond the White Sea; tribal, exotic resources, warm oases around volcanoes/hot springs, Wyld mutants) → the **Pole of Air** itself (infinite frozen depths past miles-high mountains). Religion is load-bearing: one failed harvest can erase a settlement; Northern gods keep a deadly grip on worshipers.
Dangers: Fair Folk "Winter Folk" who ride the unbroken snow to hunt mortals; renegade gods extorting worship; hungry ghosts in the mires; Wyld mutants (skinchangers, brine-giants).
Powers to know: Whitewall · Gethamane (underground city + Underways) · the Bull of the North's empire · the Haslanti League (airships) · the Icewalkers (totem-beast nomads) · Tusk (mammoth-pact) · Fortitude (prison-city) · Clovina (Mnemon tin satrapy).
Handles: by day Danger moderate (trouble odds Unlikely); after dark/in deep winter Danger lethal (odds Likely, foes act to win, +1 difficulty to survive exposure). Lunar PC is on home ground — Pact contacts, friendly barbarian cults, Wyld redoubts within reach.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; Faction:Realm (thin garrisons); NPC:The Bull of the North; the Lunar dominions in `lunar_dominions.md`.

### Whitewall — Place · North, midpoint of the Traveler's Road · Danger: low by day / lethal by night
Tone: pale stone walls graying with snow; lamplight, crowded stairs, gates that slam at dusk.
Surface: free city of ~500k (refugee-swollen, overcrowded, tense) ruled by the **Syndics**, three silver-and-ice spirit-lords whose magic makes the 15-yd walls inviolate to dead & Fair Folk. Gates shut at sundown — outside after dark, horrors hunt stragglers. Classes (**Quaternions**): Boyar > Tiun > Serf > Thrall; Visitors have no legal rights (money buys impunity). Exports ore + fine arms/armor/jewelry; brutal winters, short trade summers.
Rule: the Syndics — value the collective over any citizen; weekly **congregations** vote, a Syndic ratifies or vetoes.
Defense/dangers: walls + the **Nightwalkers' Treaty** — on the **Traveler's Road** (warm glassy highway, 50 mi each way) none may harm/enchant another (binding on dead & fae). Near the city horrors can't harm folk by day; at night they feed but **can't enter unless a citizen invites them**. Price: the Syndics hand **24 people/year** to the horrors. Hunter-captains raise monster-hunting corps for bounties.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The city is inviolate UNLESS a citizen invites a horror in — smuggled Underworld goods are the loophole. — reveal on: investigating the contraband trade, or a horror appearing inside the walls.
  - Boyar **Marlena Faria** smuggles Underworld goods (soulpearls, bonewood masks) from the **Bruised Grove** shadowland; a malevolent ghost could ride such an item to gain "invited" entry. — reveal on: tailing her caravans / a Bureaucracy or Larceny success.
  - The **Beggar Prince** (rag-clad almsgiver, refused a boyar title) may be a humility-god — or quietly raising an army from the poor. — reveal on: earning his trust, or a hostile faction's exposé.
Clocks: Refugee Pressure [2/6] — ticks each season of inflow; Anti-Refugee Faction [1/6] — ticks on incidents; Treaty Integrity [6/6] — drops on each violation (at 0: the night comes in).
Hooks: a deathknight ambassador attended this year's treaty-renewal summit (leaving roses at Immaculate temples); a hunter-captain (Alen) is aging and needs a successor; merchant-prince **Little Blue Heron** of Falcon's Watch negotiates to buy refugees as slaves; a ghost-possessed item appears inside the walls.
Handles: by day odds Unlikely; after dark odds Likely + foes act to win; hunter NPCs → CRB elite mortal templates; fae/undead → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`.
Links: Faction:Realm (tiny garrison, no satrap); Faction:Silver Pact (operates in the North); NPC:Marlena Faria; NPC:The Beggar Prince; Place:Irongate (mine-town); Place:Bruised Grove (shadowland).

### The Bull of the North's Empire — Place/Power · North, Malice Bay · Power: dominant but dying
Tone: a war-won realm holding its breath around a bedridden, dying god-king.
Surface: empire built ~10 yrs ago by **Yurgen Caul, the Bull of the North** (Icewalker reborn as a Dawn Caste Solar) via charisma + military genius. Spans Malice Bay shores, the River of Tears trade artery, sworn coastal kingdoms, and 1,000+ mi of nomad lip-service. Capital **Plenilune**. Laws: religious freedom, harsh penalties for violent crime, **collective punishment for rebellion**.
Rule: the Bull (now **dying** of an infected wound from the Battle of Futile Blood — resists even peerless sorcerous healing; never leaves his bed) + **the Bull's Circle** of Solars.
Leadership (Circle): **Samea** (Zenith sorceress, his mentor, consumed by curing him) · **Raneth of Plenilune** (Twilight viceroy — will let the empire burn to save Plenilune) · **Crimson Antler** (Dawn hunter — loyal to Icewalkers first; politically opposed to Raneth) · **Nalla Bloodaxe** & **Fear-Eater** (apparently slain at Futile Blood).
Defense/dangers: depleted armies; long desolate trade routes easy to sabotage; the empire's glue (conquest wealth, fear, the Bull's cult of personality, safety) weakens daily.
Truth (GM-only):
  - **Nalla Bloodaxe** (who once faked her death for a coup) and Fear-Eater only *apparently* died at Futile Blood — status is a GM lever. — reveal on: investigating Futile Blood survivors.
Clocks: Imperial Unravelling [3/6] — ticks each season the Bull stays sick; Icewalker Exodus [2/6] — ticks as tribes trickle home; Succession Split [—] — at the Bull's death, Raneth vs. Crimson Antler carve it up.
Hooks: subject cities revolt (Porcellana & Alnome in open rebellion; Bloomfell's coalition besieges Zemei); a cure for the Bull's wound is sought beyond Creation; the Silver Pact debates whether a dying Solar warlord is ally or obstacle.
Handles: Solars of the Circle → generate (Experienced–Legendary); imperial troops → CRB battle-group templates; if the Bull acts, treat as Legendary Dawn Caste.
Links: NPC:The Bull of the North; Place:Plenilune; Faction:Silver Pact; Faction:Realm (House Tepet's nemesis); the Icewalkers (below).

### Plenilune — Place · North, capital of the Bull's empire · Danger: moderate (seething)
Tone: a salt-rich city drawn tight as a wire, rich west bank glittering over a furious east-bank mob.
Surface: largest/richest of the five Saltspire Cities, on the magically backward-flowing **River of Tears** (carries the White Sea inland; manses desalinate it, but the power is nearly failing). Seat = **the Larkbright** island (the Plenilune Saltspire manse extends a prince's life by decades; the Pearlhallow palace). Salt is the great export (to Sijan, Rubylak, Whitewall via the Guild). West bank wealthy; east bank poor, populous, mob-justice, **Icewalker barracks**.
Rule: Prince **Ayn Celos** (Yurgen's figurehead under viceroy Raneth, quietly consolidating his own power) + tax-hungry barons.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Richest baron **Telliro Oskan** runs a broad anti-Bull conspiracy (barons, merchants, Saltspire League, Guild factors, priests) and bargains with **purple-robed Underworld missionaries** for a champion to beat Yurgen in battle. — reveal on: infiltrating the conspiracy / a Larceny or Socialize success.
Clocks: Oskan's Conspiracy [2/6] — ticks toward an uprising against prince and Bull.
Hooks: priest **Parcalla** openly preaches the Bull's prosperity is a lie (protected by his goddess + the poor); **Brasta of the Bleakwoods Elk** (Fire Aspect Icewalker commander) sees himself as the Bull's heir; bard **Jos Eryll** can lampoon anyone with impunity (a political weapon for hire).
Handles: barons/priests → CRB mortal templates; Icewalker troops → battle-group.
Links: Place:The Bull of the North's Empire; NPC:The Bull of the North; the Saltspire League (below).

### The Saltspire League & Malice Bay cities — Place cluster · North, Malice Bay · Power: fractured
Tone: salt-cities turning on a wounded conqueror, each with its own grudge.
Surface: the four southern Saltspire cities (their life-extending manses decayed; only Plenilune's works) allied against the Realm, Plenilune, AND the Bull. **Ordonel** (manse exploded 60 yrs ago, killed the royals; merchant council, assassination-ridden) · **Alnome & Porcellana** (intermarried houses, both in **open revolt** — Porcellana drove out the garrison) · **Astragal** (libertine, turned to the Icewalkers, muttering about welcoming the Realm or the Guild). Plus **Tarrack** (ex-raider shipwrights, now empowered to tax the river), **Bloomfell** (took in refugees, raising a **liberation coalition** now besieging **Zemei**).
Rule: varies per city (councils, ducal houses, rebel students).
Clocks: Revolt Cascade [3/6] — ticks each city that rises; the Bull is mustering force to crush Porcellana/Alnome.
Hooks: House Mnemon offers manse repairs for fealty (a Realm foothold); Bloomfell's hunting-mad prince may just want to replace the Bull's rule with her own; **Tepet survivors** ("Scions of Futile Blood") drift through — some still fighting (Talonlord Vergus Narivan joined Bloomfell), some hiding, some spying (Tepet Pryll's teahouse spy-net in Astragal).
Handles: city forces → battle-group templates; outcaste captains (e.g., Paran Death-Eye building her own petty empire) → CRB elite mortal.
Links: Place:Plenilune; Faction:Realm (House Mnemon, House Tepet); the Guild (`../04_factions.md`).

### The Icewalkers ("the Sworn") — Faction/People · North tundra · Power: dispersed nomad nations
Tone: drums, bone and antler, a hundred tribes following the herds of their sacred beast.
Surface: three nations — **Caribou/Reindeer**, **Elk**, **Mammoth** — of dozens of tribes, each bound by ancient pact to a patron animal and its **animal avatar** (a Gaia-empowered beast that sends talking-animal emissaries). Self-named "the Sworn." Tribe → bands (consensus, defer to glory-covered warriors/elders); war-leader in war, council of elders + shamans in peace. Every object multi-purpose; tribes ID each other by clothing/tools (outsiders never learn the distinctions → conflict).
Stance: strong ties with the **Haslanti** (anti-raiding treaties); mammoth tribes close to **Ryazan of Tusk**. Many bands sent warriors to the Bull and are now under-defended (elders + youths), suffering disproportionate raids.
Truth (GM-only):
  - **Kaj / "Lord Crowned by Black Antlers"** (Blackfur Elk outcaste, ex-Bull lieutenant who now deems Yurgen unfit) is uniting tribes the Bull never reached and may conquer a Haslanti city — or ally with Brasta to split the Bull's empire. — reveal on: scouting his warband / a Pact contact's tip.
Clocks: Tribes Drawn to War [3/6] — ticks as the Bull / Kaj pull more bands in; Herd & Tradition Strain [—].
Hooks: war-leader **Inger** (disabled tactical genius, rides mammoth Silma) needs a champion; a band begs a Lunar "living god" for protection from raiders; the Bull's death would scatter the tribes into a power-grab.
Handles: Icewalker warriors → CRB elite mortal / battle-group; shamans → CRB shaman template. A Lunar PC can readily become a tribe's living-god patron (a classic Lunar dominion seed).
Links: NPC:The Bull of the North; Place:The Haslanti League; Faction:Silver Pact.

### The Haslanti League — Faction/Power · North/Northeast, Fang Lakes & White Sea · Power: rising tech-oligarchy
Tone: airboats over frozen lakes; merchant-oligarchs and clan elders trading prophetic dreams and patents.
Surface: a loose League of city-states + clans profiting from the wreck of sorcerer-prince **Bagrash Köl's** flying empire (collapsed when the Eye of Autochthon turned on him). United at the **Shattered Tower Convocation**. "Haslan" = the ethnic clans; "Haslanti" = any member. Real power = **oligarchs** (merchant-traders) who rule cities in all but name, resented by clan/war leaders; League policy set at the **Haslanti Convocation** in the capital **Icehome**.
Tech (the draw): **airboats** (whale-oil/kerosene balloons; couriers faster than a trotting horse; some carry glider-commandos), iceships, repeating crossbows, alchemical medicine; **feathersteel** (ultralight, far-N, mined at lethal cost) and Windcreche steel (worth its weight in silver).
Culture: deeply spiritual AND innovative; prophetic dreams discussed openly; strong gift-giving ethos; no priests (shamans interpret dreams).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The **Grandmothers** are the League's spy/counter-espionage network (retired traders & strategists) — and they covertly keep the neighboring **Stragor Principalities** weak and feuding. — reveal on: a counterintelligence brush / Pact intel.
  - Oligarch **Pribizen of Tusk** (Diamond Hearth) illicitly sells relics & prototypes to the Guild-affiliated Unbroken Ice Caravaneers. — reveal on: tailing his caravans / a trade-network success.
Clocks: League Militarizes [2/6] — ticks as hawks (Birna Winter Flame) push selling tech to the Guild + southern conquest; Oligarchs vs. Clans [2/6].
Hooks: Diamond Hearth's northernmost feathersteel mine just went silent; Crystal's under-lake First Age ruin is eating scavengers; **Gerd Marrow-Eater** (an elder **Lunar** living in caves outside Icehome — uninterested in the Pact, knows Köl's works) is a riddling potential ally.
Handles: Haslanti soldiers → CRB mortal templates; airboat actions = a chase/mass-combat frame. Note Gerd as a non-Pact Lunar contact.
Links: Place:The Icewalkers (treaty allies); Faction:The Guild; Faction:Silver Pact (Gerd Marrow-Eater).

### Fortitude — Place · North, subterranean prison-city · Danger: high (no law)
Tone: torch-smoke and dark worship below cracked walls; everything is gang turf.
Surface: a city built in the tunnels of an old Tsarkuur prison-fortress; ruled by no one — dozens of gangs, three currently dominant in a tense truce (**God Killers** / Tuyaa the Hammer · **Storm Boys** / Amar · **Mother's Knives** / Naran). People = **Uznikane** ("prisoner"), descendants of imprisoned families who revolted; kill-or-be-killed ethic, no standard currency. Aboveground = ruined walls + Upper Market (caravans pay protection); deep mines worked by slaves.
Rule: gang truce, enforced by the three bosses; lower market = violence forbidden under truce.
Religion: worship **the dark**; primal deity = the **Buried God** (fatalistic; human sacrifice = sent to die in old tunnels). Sky burials. Belief: born cursed, atone via heroic deeds + an epic death.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Folk hero **Red Lilet** escaped the mines and means to rouse the enslaved miners to rebellion. — reveal on: investigating the mines / earning Uznikane trust.
Clocks: United Horde [3/6] — at full, the three gangs ride out together to pillage the neighbors; Mine Revolt [1/6] — ticks on Red Lilet's organizing.
Hooks: a gang boss hires outsiders against a rival; Silver Pact witch **Gray Rokin** spreads disease among neighbor satrapies (a Lunar-aligned pressure point); the **Lysand Wood** (sentient forest tapping Fortitude's tunnels) trades blood-fed prophecy.
Handles: gangers → CRB brigand/elite mortal; the horde → battle-group; no law means social/Larceny scenes resolve by faction favor, not authority.
Links: Place:Jagged Branch (satrapy, Lunar-harassed); Faction:Silver Pact (Gray Rokin); Faction:The Guild.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Fortitude.

### Clovina — Place · North, Mnemon tin satrapy · Danger: moderate (rebellion brewing)
Tone: gray-brick river towns over starving farm villages; hidden ancestor-shrines bleeding red.
Surface: a Realm satrapy under **House Mnemon** (Satrap **Mnemon Beril**), run through aristocratic **vojvadas** (three greater: Cosmir/tin, Radju/farmland, Stojca/lumber). Defined by tin (panned from the icy River of Tears) + the river trade. Most people are **sclavs** (enslaved laborers); the Lunar **Kanon Tas** devastated the land a generation ago before the Wyld Hunt killed her. Mnemon's lease demands restored profit while the house cuts funds, pulls troops, and raises tribute.
Rule: Mnemon via the vojvadas (played against each other); the Realm holds the lease.
Religion: pre-Realm ancestor worship (a *forgotten* ghost becomes a child-devouring monster; venerated ancestors ward against them) driven underground by the Immaculate Order; shrines now offer **blood instead of wine**.
Truth (GM-only):
  - **Nicol Dumutris** ("the Law That Pierces Life") was hanged for banditry 4 yrs ago — yet "rides again," recruiting vojvadas for a revolution to expel the Realm via restored ancestor worship (his agents impale one victim alive per raided village). — reveal on: investigating the raids / an ancestor-cult contact.
  - **Elena Cosmir**'s one uncrossable line is faith with her ancestors — she fears post-death judgment and believes only they can save her. — reveal on: a deep Socialize read / the ancestor-cult plot.
Clocks: Vojvada Debt [2/6] — ticks as Mnemon takes more (Guild loans rising); Banditry/Rebellion [2/6] — ticks as the garrison shrinks; Heresy Crackdown [1/6] — abbot Crying Truth pushes Beril to purge the ancestor cult.
Hooks: the **Utz hill clans** rally around prophet **Seer-in-Ochre** to "finish what Kanon Tas began"; smuggling fronts at Tears-of-Beauty's Black Sand Market; the Wyld-tainted **Bitter Leaf** estate (giant toxic crops, ghosts + demons bound to guard Tepet secrets).
Handles: vojvada guards → CRB mortal; Utz raiders → battle-group; the ancestor-cult/undead angle → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` ghosts.
Links: Faction:Realm (House Mnemon); Faction:Silver Pact (Kanon Tas's legacy); Place:Tears-of-Beauty.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Clovina.

### Ascension & Notch — Place pair · North, near the Pole of Air · Danger: low (Ascension) / high (Notch)
Tone: a timeless warm monastery-city of bells above a freezing, lawless mine-pit.
Surface: **Ascension** — several thousand on Horizon Plateau, kept warm year-round only by an ancient glyph ring-wall anchoring sorcery; ruled by the celibate, adopt-only **Caretakers** under an abbess; rich mines, thousand-year artisan traditions, a starmetal prayer-wheel that fascinates spirits. **Notch** — the lawless mining boomtown at the plateau's base (silver/tin, then ethereal iron & singing crystals guarded by **glassfolk** elementals); thousands of miners in bondage; real power split between an abbess-appointed governor and the **Prospector League**.
Rule: Ascension = the abbess; Notch = governor + sheriff + Prospector League.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Caretaker **Clemsen** (the governor's seemingly-naïve advisor) is the abbess's **spy** in Notch — and her leverage is smuggling foreign goods past the xenophobic warden Harzin. — reveal on: a Larceny/Investigation success.
Clocks: Foreign Tide [2/6] — Notch floods Ascension with goods, people, ideas (citizens lured away; monks split on absorbing vs. rejecting); Burning Wick Raids [1/6] — exiled prospector **Quill Ashmount** wars on both towns from fortified caves.
Hooks: glassfolk anger at illegal deep-mining (the original peace is forgotten); Crystal's frozen-lake ruin; the perilous Graveland Trail south (crime-boss Domra Redsleeve's Cauldron; Fair Folk queen Jadai of the Silver Nail who trades only for dreams).
Handles: prospectors/bruisers → CRB mortal; glassfolk & other elementals → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`; chain-elevators can snap in the cold (environmental hazard).
Links: Faction:The Guild (prospectors); the glassfolk (elementals); Place:Gethamane (underground-city neighbor, see below).

### Gethamane — Place · North, the underground city · Danger: moderate (deep dark)
Tone: a lamplit city sunk into the mountain, with stairways descending into the unknown Underways.
Surface: a famous fortress-city carved underground, a key Northern trade hub near the Pole of Air; defensible, never conquered. Beneath it stretch the **Underways** — endless ancient tunnels delving into the dark, partly explored, profitable and perilous (the city trades in what's brought up).
Rule: the city's own council/guard (an old, independent polity).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The deep Underways hold older, hungrier things than ore — and a Lunar (or worse) could use them as a hidden road beneath the North. — reveal on: a deep-tunnel expedition.
Hooks: an Underways expedition needs a strong arm; smugglers move goods beneath the Realm's notice; something has come *up* from the dark.
Handles: deep-dark foes → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (elementals, undead, beasts); navigation as exploration scenes.
Links: Place:Ascension/Notch (Northern trade); Faction:The Guild.
