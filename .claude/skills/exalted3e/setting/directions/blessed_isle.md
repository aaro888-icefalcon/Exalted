# Blessed Isle — the Realm's Heart (Pole of Earth)

The continent at Creation's dead center, founded on the **Imperial Mountain** (Pole of Earth) — the most geomantically stable, civilized, and lethally well-defended place in the world. Seat of the **Scarlet Empire** and home of the Dragon-Blooded **Dynasty**. The hardest place for a Lunar to operate (Anathema-hunting is densest here) and the prize the Silver Pact dreams of striking. Place cards below; the Realm-as-polity, the Great Houses, and the Immaculate Order are factions in `../04_factions.md`; the Scarlet Empress and key Dynasts are in `../05_named_npcs.md`.

### The Blessed Isle — Direction/Continent · Pole of Earth · Danger: high for Anathema (the Realm's core)
Tone: terraced jade-green provinces under an impossible mountain; ten thousand Dragon-Blooded who would burn an Anathema on sight.
Surface: the continent at Creation's center, ringed by the **Inland Sea**; founded on the miles-high **Imperial Mountain** (Pole of Earth). Greatest civilization in the world; densest population; the **Realm's** seat. Divided into ~hundreds of **prefectures** + three oversized **dominions** (Arjuf, Lord's Crossing, Numinous Rolling Wave). The Empress is sole landlord of all Isle land (leased, terminable at will).
Rule: nominally the absent **Scarlet Empress** via Regent **Tepet Fokuf** + the **Deliberative**; in practice the ten **Great Houses** carving it up (see `../04_factions.md`). State religion = the **Immaculate Order**.
Defense/dangers: the densest Dragon-Blooded population in Creation + the Imperial Legions (now partitioned among the Houses) + the Imperial Navy guarding the seas + the **All-Seeing Eye** (spy service) + the religious **Wyld Hunt**. For a Lunar this is the most hostile ground in the world — disguise (shapeshifting) is survival.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Empress holds the **Sword of Creation** (the First Age Realm Defense Grid) from the Imperial Manse — the superweapon that vaporized the Fair Folk Invasion and the Seven Tigers. With her gone, it sits dormant. — reveal on: deep First Age lore / the Imperial Manse.
Clocks: Succession War [3/8] — the Houses arm; a ~7-year clock runs before the Empress is judged to have abdicated; Wyld Hunt Decline [—] — legions pulled home leave the Threshold open but the Isle bristling.
Hooks: a Lunar infiltrates the Isle in stolen skin; a House recruits outsiders for a deniable strike on a rival; the Imperial City's underbelly hides everything.
Handles: Dragon-Blooded foes everywhere → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (DB) + CRB Experienced Dragon-Blood / Immaculate Monk templates; on the Isle, "is there a Dynast nearby?" odds are Likely. Anathema-detection is a constant risk.

### The Imperial City — Place · Blessed Isle, Scarlet Prefecture · Danger: high (the Realm's nerve center)
Tone: a vast unplanned capital grown around a humming First Age fortress, packed with petitioners and spies.
Surface: the Realm's capital (never officially named), grown organically around the **Imperial Manse** from a crowd of petitioners. Houses the core institutions: the **Imperial Palace**, the **Thousand Scales** ministries, the **Deliberative**, the All-Seeing Eye's old heart, and the Treasury (which once drew ~half the world's riches). City-god **Gri-Fel**.
Rule: contested — Regent Fokuf holds the throne notionally; the Deliberative legislates; the Houses maneuver; ministers freelance.
Defense/dangers: the **Legion of Silence** (5,000 mute, drug/sorcery-raised superhuman soldiers, 7+ ft, sworn from birth to the Empress) guards the Imperial Palace — the one army wholly independent of the Houses, currently quiescent (its reaction to any usurper unknown).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Imperial Manse holds the dormant **Sword of Creation** — and prisoners escaped it when the Empress vanished. — reveal on: the Manse / Eyre-of-the-Sleepwalker rumors.
Clocks: Power Vacuum [3/6] — every faction maneuvers for the throne; the Deliberative is now arguably the single greatest power in the Realm.
Hooks: the Capital's Underbelly (crime, black markets, hidden cults — the Empress secretly ran a heretical worship cult her whole reign); a magistrate-vs-ministries shadow war (lonely-road assassinations); the Mouth of Peace nears death and seeks to handpick a successor.
Handles: Dynasts/monks/agents → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` + CRB templates; the Legion of Silence → CRB statblock (Silent Legionnaire, elite, en masse a battle-group).
Links: Faction:The Realm; NPC:The Scarlet Empress; NPC:Regent Tepet Fokuf; Faction:All-Seeing Eye; Faction:Immaculate Order.

### The Imperial Mountain (Pole of Earth) — Place/Concept · Blessed Isle center · Danger: extreme (sacred / extraplanar)
Tone: a mountain so vast its peak touches Heaven; the still point of the turning world.
Surface: the **Elemental Pole of Earth**, rising miles high at the Isle's center; its foundations form the Blessed Isle, and Creation is most geomantically stable in its shadow. A site of supreme sacred and geomantic power; the Realm's name and legitimacy lean on it.
Rule: the Realm/Immaculate Order venerate it; few ascend far.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Pole anchors Creation's reality; its heights and roots reach toward Heaven and the deep earth — a place of First Age wonders and dangers far beyond mortal scale. — reveal on: a high-Essence pilgrimage / sorcerous working.
Hooks: a geomantic working or artifact-quest draws the PC mountainward; the Immaculate Mountain pilgrimage; First Age ruins near the Pole.
Handles: extreme-tier site; foes/wonders → generate at boss/legendary scale; environmental hazards (altitude, raw Essence).
Links: Concept:Creation (`../01_cosmology.md`); Faction:Immaculate Order.

### Note on the Realm as a *polity*
The Blessed Isle is the *place*; the **Scarlet Empire** as a political actor — the throne, the Dynasty, the ten Great Houses, the Magistracy, the All-Seeing Eye, the Imperial Legions/Navy, and the Immaculate Order — is statted as the campaign's central **faction board** in `../04_factions.md`, with its key figures (the Empress, Mnemon, V'neef, the Regent, Chejop Kejak, the Mouth of Peace) in `../05_named_npcs.md`. The Realm is the Silver Pact's prime enemy: wounded by the Empress's disappearance, pulling its legions home, its Wyld Hunt at its weakest ever — the strategic window the whole Lunar campaign exploits.
