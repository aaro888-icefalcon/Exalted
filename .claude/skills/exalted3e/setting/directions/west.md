# West — Pole of Water (oceans, islands, the deep)

Direction of the **Elemental Pole of Water**: thousands of miles of open ocean and a great chain of ~a hundred maritime city-states, largely untouched since the Great Contagion — the Realm's most tenuous Direction. Storm-gods, pirates, Fair Folk reavers, and the gates of the Underworld lie out here. Key cards below; Sunken Luthe (the Pact's Western HQ) is in `lunar_dominions.md`.

---

### The West — Direction · Elemental Pole of Water · Danger: moderate→high (sea / pirates)
Tone: salt wind and endless blue; island gods who demand sacrifice and pirates who take the rest.
Surface: open ocean dotted with a hundred-some maritime city-states; prizes = silver, coral, pearls, sea silk, spices, slaves. The Pole of Water is rumored beyond the farthest isle, near the **gates of the Underworld**. The Realm's grip is thinnest here.
Powers to know: **Wavecrest Archipelago** (Realm breadbasket) · the **Azurite Empire** (sea-lord republic) · the **Tya** (free sailor-women) · the **Lintha** (demon-blooded pirates, see `south.md`) · **Skullstone/Onyx** (Deathlord-ruled, see `../03_otherworlds.md`) · the floating city-ship **Denzik** · **Wu-Jian** (lawless island-port near the Isle).
Handles: Realm presence depends on the Imperial **Water Fleet** (House Peleps) — now sailing off for other wars, leaving the West underdefended. Storm-spirits, Fair Folk sea-reavers, and the Underworld's nearness are constant levers.
Links: Faction:Realm (House Peleps); Faction:Fair Folk; the Pact's **Sunken Luthe** (`lunar_dominions.md`).

### The Wavecrest Archipelago — Place/Satrapy · West, the "Breadbasket" · Danger: moderate (powderkeg)
Tone: lush volcanic islands of feasting and fear, where the mountains are gods that eat people.
Surface: a massive island chain, a **Realm satrapy** and "Breadbasket of the West" (legendary volcanic soil → exports breadfruit, maize, pork to fish-dependent neighbors); a cornerstone of House Peleps' power. Seems peaceful; tensions build toward bloodshed.
Rule: a republic of feuding royal families who elect the **Feathered One** (president-for-life, feather-cape) — whose ultimate duty is to be the final sacrifice to **Hamoji** (the chief volcano-god) if all else fails. House Peleps holds the lease.
Defense/dangers: the **volcano gods + sacrifice** system — volcanoes ARE gods; when prayer fails, hero-priestesses throw a live human in (drawn from prisons, so any real crime = prison to keep prisons stocked). Defense depends on the Realm's Water Fleet (now sailing away — the least-defended Wavecrest has been in a century); communities keep warrior-athletes but only small raiding craft.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Admiralty Board has decided to break the people's will — which means **breaking Hamoji and his siblings** (the gods) — and replaces familiar tax-collectors with cruel foreigners. — reveal on: a Realm-politics dig.
  - The royal families are secretly **converting trade ships to warships** and planning to raid neighbors, hidden from the satrap. — reveal on: a dockside Larceny/Investigation success.
Clocks: Religious Powderkeg [3/6] — reactionary **Ochre Heart Witnesses** stage attacks to goad Immaculate laity into the violence that sparks a revolt; Hamoji Restless [2/6] — volcanoes stir in the Time of Tumult.
Hooks: hero-priestess Kamach Achi (loudest anti-Immaculate voice) seeks duels with monks; Satrap Peleps Senit juggles rebellion + V'neef/Lunar sabotage + Admiralty pressure; Gullwing (westernmost isle) is the first line vs. Fair Folk raids from Creation's edge.
Handles: islander warriors → CRB elite mortal / battle-group; volcano-gods → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (gods, e.g. Storm Mother template upscaled); a Lunar can stoke this revolt against the Realm.
Links: Faction:Realm (House Peleps, House V'neef saboteurs); Faction:Immaculate Order; West overview.

### The Azurite Empire — Place/Power · West, Coral Archipelago · Danger: high (sea-lord republic)
Tone: armored turtle-ships and brutal justice; a republic that drowns its traitors for an immortal kraken.
Surface: an ascendant naval empire ruled from the city of **Azure**, explicitly a **bulwark against the expanding Realm** — islands between must choose Azure or House Peleps. Slavery props up everything (~1/3 enslaved). NOTE: **not Dragon-Blooded-ruled** — a republic of **sea lords** that *bars* DB from elected office (they serve only in the navy/as privateers).
Rule: the **First Sea Lord** (grand admiral + chief general + chief magistrate) + the Conclave of Sea Lords + three assemblies (commoners; military veterans who declare war; oldest/richest clans whose "recommendations" are edicts). Electorship hereditary but purchasable via tribute.
Defense/dangers: the largest native Western fleet (armored **turtle ships** with flame-spewing dragon-prows) + privateers. Justice = the brand: public flogging, face-tattooing, family guilt-by-association, and the **Kraken's Pool** (traitors thrown alive to an immortal kraken; also where citizens privately settle lethal vendettas by night).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The chief god, the **Ocean Father**, was demoted to the NW seas with no successor named; rivals **Siakal** (forbidden shark-goddess) and Zhuziao challenge him — and he may abandon Azure for a new empire if he thinks it'll break. — reveal on: an Occult/religion dig.
  - DB rear admiral **Kang Narae** (grandfather executed for sedition) is being courted by allies to back a power-grab if the First Sea Lord falters. — reveal on: navy intrigue.
Clocks: Leadership Wobble [2/6] — disillusioned First Sea Lord Gim Hanjul (both sons dead in battle) is reluctant to fight; the veterans' assembly murmurs of a new election; Siakal Cult [2/6] — the **Silver Prince**'s agents stir the underground Maw of Siakal to overthrow Azure's priest-magistrates.
Hooks: magistrate Sem Teong hunts the Siakal cult (cultists now threaten his family); the **Tya** integrate into the navy (Tya Tanari builds a political bloc, resented by highborn); the Last Prince's tomb may hold his vengeful sorcerer-spirit.
Handles: Azure marines/turtle-ships → battle-group + naval set-piece; sword-magistrates → CRB elite mortal; Siakal → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (god, boss).
Links: Faction:Realm (House Peleps, rival); Place:Skullstone (wary standoff); Place:The Tya; the Underworld (Silver Prince).

### The Tya — Faction/People · West (stateless; the Neck & beyond) · Power: scattered sailor-sisterhood
Tone: tattooed sailor-folk in communal lodges, free where their birth-lands would cage them.
Surface: not a place — a stateless cultural minority: laborers, sailors, tradesfolk, mercenaries. Originated as **women escaping sexist Western restrictions** (the Storm Mothers' jealousy + ships' women-bans); now always outsiders, slandered as witches/thieves. Concentrated around the Neck, scattered in ports as far as Nai Lei and Fajad. **Tya is its own gender** (intersection of gender/class/religion; outsiders use the address "Tya"; members keep a public name + a private true name).
Rule: members live in communal **lodges** (shared resources, democratic, led by an "Eldest"); visiting Tya welcomed at any lodge; lodges fund themselves via crafts.
Defense/dangers: regional standing varies (derided as witches in Auspicene lands; suspected craft-rivals in Randan; considered lucky in Utahi). Each lodge has a **compeer** — a bound patron spirit (usually one lacking worshipers: ill-tempered, strange, or forbidden) granting wards/blessings/minor mutations.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Banished Tya **Alumai** co-founded the **Great Mother's Lodge** with a Lintha sorcerer — raiding Makelo/Randan ships and sacrificing captives to the demon Lovota and to Kimbery. — reveal on: investigating Tya raiders / a heresy tip.
Clocks: Lodge Infighting [—] — the culture prizes authenticity and is prone to vicious splintering.
Hooks: the Three-Sided Coin lodge (esteemed bodyguards under scrutiny); Eventide Lodge (a Tya-harassing magistrate just vanished — suspicion falls on them); living-legend Eldest **Haholi** of Seahaven ("will sacrifice one for many"); a Lunar can find ready, discreet allies among the Tya.
Handles: Tya sailors → CRB mortal (skilled); compeers → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (minor/odd spirits).
Links: Place:The Azurite Empire (Tya in the navy); Place:The Lintha (the heretical lodge); West overview.
