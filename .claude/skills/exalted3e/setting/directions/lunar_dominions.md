# Lunar Dominions — the Silver Pact's Strongholds (PRIORITIZED)

The **dominions**: territories where Lunar Exalted rule as living gods / war-patrons of "barbarian" peoples, bleeding the Realm from the rim. These are the PC's most natural allies, models, rivals, and refuges — and a Lunar PC building a power base is, in effect, founding one of these. Spread across all Directions; collected here because they're a coherent Lunar layer. Each carries a **Territory** trio (its Boundary-Marking qualities: the master grants the supporting one as a bonus, denies/imposes the opposing as a penalty). Elders who rule them have NPC cards in `../05_named_npcs.md`.

### Iscomay, the Empire of the Bear — Place/Power · Northeast taiga · Power: expansionist empire (mobilizing)
Tone: bear-banners over a janissary empire outgrowing its founding prophet.
Surface: hundreds of thousands of people, many walled cities, the geomantically-warmed cornucopia **Oma Valley**. Founded 5 centuries ago by the Lunar culture-hero **True Voice**, who codified its law (the **Book of the Bear**), begat the dynasty, then left — and has now returned as a "prophet-saint" to turn the empire against the Realm, only to find it beyond her control. **The canonical fortification → open war dominion now mobilizing.**
Territory: capital **Karaqa** is sprawling, dilapidated, crime-ridden (True Voice's hold); she's failing to claim the new capital **High Sila**.
Rule: the Moon-Touched **House of Siladar** (falsely claim DB descent; child-emperor **Ajurai Siladar II**, naïve and court-manipulated). Defense = the **Legions of the Bear** (elite foreign-born **janissaries**, well-paid, 25-yr terms then land), the largest regional army — but overstretched by rebellion and high taxes. Scholar-priests = **khojas** (commune with a bear unharmed as rite of passage).
Truth (GM-only):
  - True Voice cannot bend the leading khojas' reading of the Book to her anti-Realm intent — the empire's own scripture resists its founder. — reveal on: court intrigue / a khoja's confidence.
Clocks: Mobilization vs. the Realm [3/8] — ticks as True Voice + hawks turn the empire outward; Internal Revolt [3/6] — Sulat province rebels, the nomadic Urma raid, Bey Isipho schemes to crown cousin Wawarai.
Hooks: a Pact mentor sends the PC to broker (or sabotage) Iscomay's war-entry; raksha queen **Meliash** (a rebel symbol) is rumored to remain near Spur; the janissary leadership grows restive.
Handles: janissaries → battle-group; khojas/courtiers → CRB mortal; True Voice → `../05_named_npcs.md` (elder Lunar pointer).
Links: NPC:True Voice; Faction:Silver Pact; Faction:Realm; North overview.

### Mahalanka & the Thousand Fangs Army — Place/Power · Eastern forest · Power: sorcerous empire (untouchable)
Tone: a decadent sorcery-city in First Age ruins, ruled by a cannibal devil-queen worshiped as a god.
Surface: a vast, sophisticated city (running water, sorcery-mills, warded crops) built amid the arcology-ruins of **Sperimin**, heart of the **Thousand Fangs Army Total Control Zone** — a grand multi-culture empire *even the Realm won't trespass*. Ruled by **Raksi, Queen of Fangs**, the Pact's greatest sorcerer (holds the *Book of Three Circles*).
Territory: prosperous, decadent, sorcerous.
Rule: Raksi (hands-off, delegates to the **Golden Fang Triad** — judge **Eskrin Fal**, finance-minister **Jakun Qobal**, spymaster-general **Feroz Kutari**), but delivers sudden horrific retribution; demands worship in person. Society ruled by her **apefolk** elite caste (made by surviving brutal mystic trials; can punish non-beastfolk at will).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Raksi *performs* monstrosity (cannibal banquets, vivisecting DB prisoners) deliberately, to unnerve rivals and test petitioners — a warm human side exists for those who earn it. — reveal on: surviving her court / earning her interest.
Clocks: Raksi vs. Ma-Ha-Suchi feud [—] — their old enmity still threatens to split the Pact.
Hooks: a petitioner must survive Raksi's trial/banquet for a boon or lore; adherent **Dark Eyes** banned the slave trade (kill slavers on sight), clashing with slaver clans; the Court of Thorns fae let her caravans pass.
Handles: Raksi → `../05_named_npcs.md` / `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (legendary, E7); apefolk → CRB elite mortal / battle-group.
Links: NPC:Raksi; NPC:Ma-Ha-Suchi (rival); Faction:Silver Pact; East overview.

### The Nameless Lair of Ma-Ha-Suchi — Place/Power · Southeast jungle ("the Tangle") · Power: beastfolk bastion
Tone: a vine-draped fortress in a deadly jungle, ruled by a bitter goat-and-wolf god-king and three jungle goddesses.
Surface: a First Age stone fortress by a lake in **the Tangle** (toxic plants, beasts, unstable ground) at the Scavenger Lands' southern edge — a bulwark vs. Realm expansion + a raiding base. Ruled by elder **Ma-Ha-Suchi**, "Hand of the Mothers."
Territory: the Nameless Lair is isolated, well-defended, terrifying; the **Threefold Womb** temple-demesne is uplifting, holy, arduous.
Rule: Ma-Ha-Suchi as equal to the **inan** (god-chosen chiefs) of the **Chante-Sa** — three tribes each patron-ed by one of the **Three Mothers** (Gap'Inan/Hungry, Chuwe'Inan/Lavish, Mahni'Inan/River). His beastfolk (cunning **goatfolk** advisors, mighty **wolf-folk** hunters) are made on the Path of the Mother's Hand. Funerary cannibalism (own dead only).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The **Blood Seasons** ritual (every 4th new moon) sets tribal policy; if no band wins, Ma-Ha-Suchi's beastfolk lead a **Season of Hunts** = an organized raid into Realm satrapies. — reveal on: witnessing a Blood Season.
Clocks: Inter-band Feud [2/6] — River Band inan Aan Jandar lets raids escalate; counselors plot to replace him.
Hooks: Ma-Ha-Suchi assigns the PC a favor (he negotiates, but on his terms); wolf-general Six Day Storm (barred from the Threefold Womb) seethes; Immaculate missionaries court the raided Vuloth.
Handles: Ma-Ha-Suchi → `../05_named_npcs.md` (legendary elder); beastfolk → CRB elite mortal / battle-group.
Links: NPC:Ma-Ha-Suchi; NPC:Raksi (estranged friend); Faction:Silver Pact; East overview.

### Sunken Luthe — Place · Western ocean depths · Power: hidden Pact war-HQ (West)
Tone: a drowned god-city in luminous twilight, on a war footing under a melancholy whale-god.
Surface: the First Age "City of a Thousand Gods," drowned in the Western deep (lit by algae, teetering over an abyss that swallows it yearly). The Pact's **hidden, unassailable HQ for Western operations**, ruled by the elder **Leviathan** (orca spirit-shape).
Territory: aquatic, fortified, martial.
Rule: the hereditary **Luthian Council** in name; Leviathan effectively decrees (imposed mandatory military training, expanded the army). Repopulated by **aquatic beastfolk** (squidfolk, whalefolk, etc.); sign-language lingua franca; currency = the abstract "deben."
Truth (GM-only):
  - A faerie court in the abyss fissure is **eating the temple district** (it craves the temples' echoes of worship). — reveal on: investigating the vanishing temples.
  - Leviathan's daughter **Blood-Dimmed Deep** (leads the Siaka Guard) privately pushes for **open war**, which Leviathan refuses — friction growing. — reveal on: a council intrigue.
Clocks: Western War Build-up [3/8] — ticks toward the Pact's planned invasion of the Blessed Isle.
Hooks: the black-jade map in the Sunken Hall tracks the Imperial Navy across the West; outpost Galbadan is an assassins'/poisoners' black market; DB fixer Gevan Batahul (under Leviathan's protection) has never heard of the Usurpation.
Handles: aquatic beastfolk → CRB mortal/battle-group; Leviathan → `../05_named_npcs.md` (legendary elder).
Links: NPC:Leviathan; Faction:Silver Pact; West overview; Faction:Realm (Imperial Navy).

### The Caul — Place/Theatre · island-continent (sacred, contested) · Power: open-war front
Tone: a living sibling-land where reality melds with dream, fought over shrine by shrine.
Surface: an island-continent **born of Gaia's dream of Luna** — sacred to BOTH Lunars and the Dragon-Blooded; it vanished after the Great Contagion and reappeared ~5 centuries ago. The Realm seized it as religious duty; the Lunars war ferociously to free their abused "sibling." The canonical **OPEN WAR** theatre (viable because of the Caul's remoteness), commanded by elder **Sha'a Oka, the Black Lion** — every Lunar here is part of his army.
Defense/dangers: off the pilgrimage road the land **physically shifts when unobserved** (you end up 100 mi off — only those who know the Caul navigate it); shadowlands cannot open here; unbound demons roam; raksha bordermarches exist.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The prize is the **five Shrine Cities**: holding all five lets one make the pilgrimage to **Feng-Yi**, which *guarantees a child will Exalt* (the Realm's whole reason to be here). Sha'a Oka holds every shrine **except Faxai** (the Realm's Earth-Shrine foothold). **Even the elders don't know what happens if Lunars hold all five and enter Feng-Yi.** — reveal on: campaigning for the shrines.
Clocks: Shrine War [4/5] — one shrine left (Faxai) for total Lunar control.
Hooks: Sha'a Oka recruits the PC into the Caul war; each shrine is a set-piece (Garianghis = dream-gateway; Houshou = burns nightly; Sekima = malleable past-life memory; Melilune = living reflections); the free city of **Kama** (garrison routed) is up for grabs.
Handles: beast-soldier clans (lionfolk, hawkfolk, boarfolk) → battle-group; Sha'a Oka → `../05_named_npcs.md` (legendary). Navigation = exploration/Survival scenes.
Links: NPC:Sha'a Oka; Faction:Silver Pact; Faction:Realm; the Caul also appears in `../04_factions.md` notes and the West.
Vault: Lunars: Fangs at the Gate › Chapter Three › The Caul; The Realm › Chapter Seven: The War for the Caul.

### Skandhar-Bhal — Place · Northern Skyrazor Mountains · Power: hidden monastic theocracy
Tone: a warm green valley atop killing peaks, peaceful — for now.
Surface: a hidden valley sheltering the **Moon-That-Fell** (a 10-ft silver moonstone, Luna's fallen heart, in a stupa-manse). A peaceful monastic theocracy under the tortoise-shaped Lunar **Vanamaithri Mirror-Soul** (who sickened of violence) — now preparing to return to the world *as a warrior*, taking the warrior-monks.
Territory: hidden, sacred, peaceful.
Rule: Vanamaithri + nine celestial guardian gods + monastic orders; near-moneyless, no formal law (crime = "unenlightened mind").
Truth (GM-only):
  - Earth-Aspect **DB** "Grandmother Aga" keeps the outer valley's peace and rivals Vanamaithri for popular loyalty (she doesn't realize how many would follow her). — reveal on: time among the valley folk.
Clocks: The Reopening [—] — Vanamaithri's return to war could shatter the valley's pacifism.
Hooks: the god Jade Eagle (bored troublemaker) worries about Vanamaithri's reawakened bloodlust; rural families make secret pacts with exiled "devil" gods; a pilgrim seeks the Moon-That-Fell.
Handles: warrior-monks → CRB monk template / battle-group; Vanamaithri → `../05_named_npcs.md` (legendary, E4).
Links: NPC:Vanamaithri Mirror-Soul; Faction:Silver Pact; North overview.

### The Touman Clans — Place/People · North of the White Sea (Carnelian) · Power: settling conqueror-nomads
Tone: loud, vivacious steppe nomads turned uneasy city-rulers, their ancestors literally in council.
Surface: Medoan refugees who became raiders, then **conquered the river city-state of Carnelian** ~20 yrs ago. ~a score of clans; cavalry is decisive. Their immortal champion is the carp-shaped Southern Lunar **Wake**, who refuses to rule or be worshiped (a "hero-aunt"). Ancestor-ghost worship runs deep (ghosts counsel and even hold office).
Rule: clans led by a **knez** (elected for life); a **voivode** from the royal Diamandri clan rules all (current: **Sangerel**, who wants empire and pushes war on Thetra).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Carnelian's deposed city-god, the **Queen of the First Vault**, runs an underground resistance + Immaculate following plotting against the Touman. — reveal on: investigating dissidents.
Clocks: Settle-or-Roam Schism [2/6] — rich urbanites want to stay; poor traditionalists want the steppe; Wake must soon push them to raid or split.
Hooks: Wake needs a champion to break the deadlock; slaver Guild factor Tearshape Love fans Sangerel's war to harvest refugee slaves.
Handles: Touman cavalry → battle-group; Wake → `../05_named_npcs.md`; ancestor-ghosts → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`.
Links: NPC:Wake; Faction:Silver Pact; Faction:The Guild; North overview.

### The Bronze Tide — Power/Fleet · far Southwest (Cinder Isles) · Power: refugee war-fleet
Tone: bird-prowed galleys of a dozen frightened nations, conquering to outrun a soul-eating fae empire.
Surface: NOT a fixed place — a massed war-fleet of refugee peninsular peoples fleeing the raksha **Spear-Empress Bhadri** (whose 12 dread companions devour souls and expand yearly). Led by the Changing Moon **Lukha Palash**; masters of psychological terror-warfare; "as much flight as conquest." Recently recruited to the Silver Pact (open-war ideology).
Truth (GM-only):
  - They conquer islanders only to keep ahead of Bhadri; some council members (Jural Three-Trees) secretly plan to drag Bhadri in chains to the Caul, doubting they can. — reveal on: a council confidence.
Clocks: Bhadri's Advance [—] — her soul-eaten territory creeps coastward; the Tide must keep moving.
Hooks: a doomed city in the Tide's path begs for help; conquering kin-islands tears the fleet morally; old rivalries (Mygdon vs. Yensei) simmer under Lukha.
Handles: Tide warriors → battle-group; Lukha → generate (Changing Moon); Bhadri & companions → legendary raksha (generate).

### The Eskari — People · savannas west of the Summer Mountains · Power: nomad confederation (militarizing)
Tone: ~seven camel-clans being secretly forged into anti-Realm raiders by a trickster "devil."
Surface: ~7 nomadic clans (~500 each), refugees of **Eskaridam** (a city razed by the Realm). Matrilineal; loose confederation under elected war-leader **gyula** (current: aggressive young **Zita**). The near-religious myth of "New Eskaridam" drives them.
Truth (GM-only):
  - **Smiling Zamisha** (a Changing Moon weaving herself into folklore as a cruel "devil") is the *secret author* of the whole militarization — she replaced elder authority with a war-leader and stoked the war via stolen faces and dream-visions. — reveal on: tracing the "Zamisha stories" to their source.
Clocks: War on the Varang [2/6] — Zita pushes ever-bigger raids, dreaming of New Eskaridam on Varangian land; clans disagree what/where it even is.
Hooks: matriarchs scheme to replace Zita; Tezelyke "fraud" Eszter is secretly a real **Exigent**; the Szonia clan negotiates peace with Prasad.
Handles: Eskari raiders → battle-group; Zamisha → `../05_named_npcs.md`/generate (Changing Moon).
Links: NPC:Smiling Zamisha; Faction:Silver Pact; South (Varang).
