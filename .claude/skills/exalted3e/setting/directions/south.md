# South — Pole of Fire (deserts, glass cities, fire)

Direction of the **Elemental Pole of Fire**: a coast of slave-built city-states under tyrants and priest-kings, drying inland to a vast sand sea, then the Pole's ultimate blaze. Water is precious as jade. The **Southwest** (An-Teng, the Lintha) is humid jungle/sea, subjugated like the West. Key cards below; the Eskari (Lunar) are in `lunar_dominions.md`.

---

### The South — Direction · Elemental Pole of Fire · Danger: moderate→high (heat / tyrants)
Tone: heat-shimmer over glass roads; slave-markets, star-priests, and gods who must be fed sacrifices.
Surface: coastal city-states (slave economies under tyrants/priest-kings) → dry plateaus → sand sea → the Pole of Fire. Water is scarce and precious. Trade runs the Jade Road and desert caravan routes; deep-desert nomads worship oasis-gods.
Powers to know: **Chiaroscuro** (glass metropolis, Delzahn) · **Gem** (underground gem-city) · **Harborhead** (warrior society, god Ahlat) · the **Varang** Confederacy · **Ember** (Nywera fire-city) · and SW: **An-Teng** (the Realm's garden satrapy), the **Lintha** (demon-blooded pirates), **Lathe** (Yozi-cult port).
Handles: Realm presence via satrapies (Chiaroscuro under Sesus; An-Teng; Varang) — and the garrisons are withdrawing. Heat/water are environmental levers; Wyld "black lions" and riddling desert fae roam the deep sands.
Links: Faction:Realm; Faction:The Guild; the Eskari (`lunar_dominions.md`).

### Chiaroscuro — Place · South coast, glass city · Danger: moderate (Realm satrapy, simmering)
Tone: imperishable dark-red glass roads glowing warm at night; a cosmopolitan crowd over First Age ruins.
Surface: the largest, richest Southern coastal metropolis — a First Age glass city, "jewel of the Delzahn." Signature: imperishable **cinnabar glass roads** (self-cleaning, weather-moderating) and crystal sea-walls (calm storms, guide ships); shattered glass towers (a handful habitable, reserved for nobility). Steam-cannon battlements face the sea.
Rule: the **tri-khatun** (eldest matrilineal heir; civil/legal authority + bureaucracy) + the **tri-khan** (her husband; military/diplomacy/Realm tribute) over feuding aristocratic families. A **Realm satrapy** with privileged status; **House Sesus** holds the lease and a legal monopoly on silk.
Defense/dangers: the lawless **Foreigners' Quarter** (sinkholes, volatile First Age weapons, hungry ghosts); the **Whispering Circles** (small shadowlands caged by salt-laying **Salt Wardens**, who are largely above the law).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Satrap **Sesus Alon Paras** is a senior **All-Seeing Eye** agent running three separate spy networks (Eye, House Sesus, herself). — reveal on: a counterintelligence brush.
  - Tri-khatun **Nasrin Dana**'s late mother left coded dispatches and a **hidden spy network** whose members Dana doesn't know. — reveal on: cracking the rosewood box / a Lore success.
Clocks: Realm Squeeze [2/6] — Sesus tribute rising as garrisons thin; Heresy Underground [1/6] — the Cult of the Illuminated (reveres Solars as "Shining Ones") spreads.
Hooks: glass is monopolized by the Faience & Glasswork Association (a Larceny/Bureaucracy plot); the lethal Salt Market (slaves + natron); Dana's dereth brother Sharif (heretic glassworker on the Association blacklist).
Handles: Delzahn guards → CRB elite mortal; ghosts/shadowland → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`; the salt-warded shadowlands quietly harbor discreet Celestial Exalted (a Lunar can hide here).
Links: Faction:Realm (House Sesus, the Eye); Faction:The Guild; the Cult of the Illuminated.

### Ember — Place · South, Summer Mountains (the Nywera) · Danger: moderate (refuge under threat)
Tone: a five-tiered fortress-city of red torch-pines, seers ruling in twinned pairs, watching the east for Prasad.
Surface: the final refuge of the **Nywera**, who fled Prasad a century ago following prophecy; built into the abandoned Shogunate citadel **Henmagan** on the Summer Mountains' western slopes. Sacred resource: **torch-pine groves** (hot-burning forge-wood, incense, firedust, and a pollen that grants oracular power but shatters the mind — only **twins** survive it, sharing the burden).
Rule: the **Twin Queens** (duumvirs chosen from gifted surviving twins). Gifted twins are the ruling class (priests, generals, seers). Districts run by reeves → high-reeves → the Queens' court.
Defense/dangers: five gated tiers up an escarpment (switchback stairs, windlass cargo elevators, archer towers); abandoned vaults hold hungry ghosts, bad air, cave-ins.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Nywera seers cracked the **command codes of Shogunate automatons** and lion-dragon obelisks (eye-beams, beast-command) — they present these to outsiders as "gods" and the codes as "secret prayers"; a few working automatons are held in reserve deep in Ember. — reveal on: a Lore/Investigation dig into the "gods."
  - Queen **Babirye** (Dragon-Blooded warrior) is secretly gathering resources for a journey to the afterlife "Cold House" to win immortality for her dying twin **Nakato** — distracted from defense as Prasad advances. — reveal on: a Socialize read / following her agents.
Clocks: Prasad's Advance [3/6] — the empire they fled is expanding again; "unwilling to be driven from their home again."
Hooks: warmaster Mishoka (whose twin was exiled) smuggles aid to Nywera rebels still under Prasad; a working automaton wakes; the torch-pine oracle is sought.
Handles: Nywera soldiers → CRB mortal/battle-group; automatons → CRB construct (Walking Statue/Man of Gold); ghosts → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`.
Links: Faction:Realm (Prasad, the Empire of Prasad — see Blessed Isle/SE); the Jade Road; South overview.

### The Varang City-States — Place/Confederacy · South, river valleys · Danger: moderate (Realm satrapy)
Tone: clockwork-precise city-states where the stars at your birth decide your whole life.
Surface: a confederacy of city-states obsessed with order, clocks, and the stars; a **Realm satrapy**. Defining system: **Social Spheres** — every citizen's rank/role fixed by natal horoscope (planet-based), instantly signaled by garments. Officially Immaculate, secretly star-god worshipers (hidden underground temples).
Rule: astrologically-selected princes + offices assigned by sortition (lotteries + horoscope exams) — but princely families rig births and charts to keep power. Capital **Yane** is first-among-equals + the satrap's seat.
Defense/dangers: native soldiers fight only ritual skirmishes; the **Realm garrison handles real defense** — and it's withdrawing (a key vulnerability).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Varangian astrology is "largely a well-meaning sham" — a codified mash run by mostly un-gifted savants; the wealthy bribe astrologers to doctor charts; a few real astrologers are drowned out. — reveal on: an Occult/Investigation expose.
  - The **Blue Seeress** (an incorruptible prophet-corpse entombed under a Urim clocktower) has begun speaking again: "fire and bronze, blood in Urim's streets, chaos in Heaven, and deaths of stars." — reveal on: a pilgrimage / hearing the prophecy.
Clocks: Garrison Withdrawal [2/6] — as the Realm leaves, princes scramble for alliances/marriages; militant **Kesil** is poised to dominate its neighbors.
Hooks: astrologer Mahazi Maala (corrupt, blackmailed, has written a chaos-causing posthumous confession); economy run by secretive "counting-temples" (e.g., the slaver Gauntlet); a doctored horoscope ruins (or makes) a life.
Handles: Varang janissaries → battle-group; counting-temple agents → CRB mortal. Social system = a built-in identity/intrigue lever.
Links: Faction:Realm; Place:Ember (Jade Road); the Eskari raid them (`lunar_dominions.md`).

### An-Teng — Place/Satrapy · Southwest, the Realm's "garden" · Danger: low (resorts) / rising (rebellion)
Tone: a gentle pleasure-garden where Dynasts vacation — over a seething hatred of them.
Surface: the Realm's pleasure-garden satrapy: renowned as gentle and peaceful, where Dynasts holiday on Tengese hospitality; underneath, centuries of hatred (crushing taxes, free-loading Dynasts, an abusive garrison above the law). Three provinces under three Realm-installed **Princes** (Shore/Middle/High Lands), each autonomous, each collecting heavy tribute.
Rule: the three Princes; the satrap is **Ragara Soras Jor**.
Defense/dangers: **no standing army** (only aristocratic house guards + the Realm garrison); tradition is guerrilla war; princely elite troops are artifact-flavored (Shore's shadow-puppeteer assassins; Middle's elephant-riding ghost generals; High's animal-commanding masks).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The **High Queen** title (whom the Princes once answered to) was claimed by the Scarlet Empress — so succession is now an open question. — reveal on: a Lore/court success.
  - The **Seven-Stranded Vine** demon cult (descendants of the old royal family, worship She Who Lives in Her Name) wields rumor/sabotage/assassination for an independent An-Teng with themselves on top. — reveal on: investigating the cult / a betrayal.
  - The former capital **City of Dead Flowers** is a fog-shrouded shadowland; a **deathknight** reportedly now resides in the Palace of the Lotus. — reveal on: approaching the dead city.
Clocks: Rebellion [3/6] — with the Empress gone + legions withdrawn, the country may erupt; the Ghost Leopards (Robin-Hood guerrillas), the Golden Lord's Kingdom (monarchist rebels), and the Firedust Regiment (provocateurs) all push.
Hooks: smuggler Emerald Cliff funds mountain rebels; the **Lintha** covertly ally with An-Teng (dock at Shore ports to trade, hand over captured smugglers, raid Realm merchants); garrison commander Ledaal Shuri secretly spies on House Ragara.
Handles: house guards → CRB mortal; rebel bands → battle-group; the demon cult / shadowland → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`.
Links: Faction:Realm (House Ragara); Place:The Lintha; the Underworld (`../03_otherworlds.md`).

### The Lintha — Faction/People · Southwest seas (Bluehaven) · Danger: high (demon-blooded pirates)
Tone: the terror of Western shipping — a floating city of lashed ships, blood-purity cultists, and monster-drawn galleys.
Surface: demon-blooded pirates claiming descent from the demon prince **Kimbery** ("the Great Mother"); once ruled a vast Western realm, now rebuilt via intermarriage; the terror of shipping from An-Teng to the Coral Archipelago. Home = **Bluehaven**, a floating city drifting in a sargasso gyre around their dying island-sister **Ng Oroo** (nearly unreachable; foreigners confined to the Floating Market).
Rule: family titles (cousin → ... → grandparent → the **Lei Kiangi** at the apex + the Septian Covenant), divided into **septs** (Gajui richest, Haquen showy, Ng Hut run the cult). Real power = the **Cult of Dukantha** (sorcerers who bind the demons/krakens propelling the **sailless ships**; high priest always an Ng Hut grandparent).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The "blood purity" doctrine (truebloods vs. half-bloods, sacred cannibalism, eugenics, castration) is nonsense — the Lintha were **always human**; "pureblood strength" is unattainable. — reveal on: deep Lintha lore / a heretic's confidence.
  - **Dukantha** (a Dragon-Blooded "Chosen of the Great Mother") returns from Hell each Calibration for the Feast and has promised to come crush anyone who threatens Bluehaven if called. — reveal on: provoking Bluehaven / witnessing the Feast.
Clocks: Succession Tension [2/6] — cautious Lei Kiangi Wari Fan (refuses to exploit Realm chaos) vs. rival Sarangkai whispering for bolder leadership; Ng Oroo Dies [—] — the island sinks lower yearly (the water chokepoint they control).
Hooks: a Lintha raid takes captives/cargo; the Semivirist + apocalyptic Oroite heresies; Lintha crime rackets in distant ports (Wu-Jian, scattered colonies).
Handles: Lintha reavers → CRB Lintha/elite mortal; sailless ships (monster-drawn) → naval set-piece; bound demons → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`.
Links: Faction:Malfeas/demons (Kimbery); Place:An-Teng (secret ally); West overview.
