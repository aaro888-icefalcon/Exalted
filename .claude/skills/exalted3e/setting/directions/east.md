# East — Pole of Wood (Scavenger Lands, River Province, Far East)

Direction of the **Elemental Pole of Wood**: verdant, vital, three harvests in a lean year. Home to the free city-states of the **Scavenger Lands** along the Yanaze, the great trade-city **Nexus**, the military power **Lookshy** (faction → `../04_factions.md`), and Lunar dominions in the deep wilds. Twice the East threw back the Realm. Key cards below; Lunar dominions (Mahalanka, the Nameless Lair) are in `lunar_dominions.md`.

---

### The East — Direction · Elemental Pole of Wood · Danger: low→moderate
Tone: green light through primeval canopy; river-cities at war with their neighbors and rich with First Age loot.
Surface: the most fecund Direction. The **Scavenger Lands** = a sprawl of fractious principalities along the **Yanaze River**, cradle of civilization, riddled with lootable ruins, endlessly at war among themselves — twice repelling the Realm under **Lookshy**. The **River Province** (Sijan to Lookshy to Great Forks) is its rich core. Beyond lie the **Hundred Kingdoms**, then the **Far East** Pole-of-Wood wilds (Lunar domains, the serpentfolk **Ixcoatli/Ixchel**).
Powers to know: **Lookshy** (Shogunate-heir military) · **Nexus** (greatest trade city) · **Great Forks** (city of gods) · **Sijan** (necropolis) · **Thorns** (fallen to a Deathlord — see `../03_otherworlds.md`) · the Hundred Kingdoms · Realm satrapy **Greyfalls** (NE, base for Wyld Hunts).
Handles: Realm presence patchy (satrapies + Greyfalls); the free cities resolve by faction politics, not imperial law. Lunar dominions and the Pole of Wood are within reach.
Links: Faction:Lookshy; Faction:The Guild; the Lunar dominions in `lunar_dominions.md`.

### Nexus — Place · East, confluence of the Yellow/Grey/Yanaze · Danger: high (lawless wealth)
Tone: a million souls trading everything, watched by a silver-masked thing that punishes the worst.
Surface: the greatest trade city in Creation (~1 million); spiritual home of the **Guild** (Directorate sits across the Big Market). No government, no laws, no peacekeepers — only the **Council of Entities'** edicts. Justice is hired (mercenaries/bounty hunters by the docks).
Rule: the **Council of Entities** — ~nine idiosyncratic Councilors (titled by caste-names) who maintain the status quo; self-electing, stable; power level scalable (magnates to Exalted).
Law: **the Dogma** (six unbreakable laws — none obstruct trade, bring an army in, do wanton violence, falsely claim Council sanction, harbor a Council fugitive, or levy taxes save the Council) + the **Civilities** (lesser rules any Councilor can change at will; newest wins). Enforced by **the Emissary** (masked supernatural enforcer; gruesome public punishments).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Emissary's nature is GM's choice (First Age automaton? Dragon King sorcerer?); by default strong enough to beat an experienced Dragon-Blood one-on-one. — reveal on: provoking it / deep investigation.
  - Scattered through the city are the **tombs of six Solar Anathema** who died in old Hollow — deadly defenses; the Council forbids opening them; some entrepreneurs repurpose them (a Solar's imperishable tomb as a forge). — reveal on: a Lore/Investigation dig.
Clocks: Guild vs. Council [2/6] — the Guild would seize the city but fears the Emissary.
Hooks: Rom Daoh's anti-slavery "Liberating Ones" cult gains ground (Coffleblock slave-market is legal here); Water Opal's crime ring expands via a deal with a Councilor; Firewander's sewer-bordermarch (grotesque fae below) is beyond even Council reach.
Handles: by default Danger high (any crime possible, no law); foes → CRB mortal/elite; the Emissary → generate (boss). Social scenes resolve by Council favor + the Dogma.
Links: Faction:The Guild; NPC:The Emissary; Place:the Solar tombs.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Nexus.

### The Hundred Kingdoms — Place/Region · East, eastern Scavenger Lands · Danger: moderate (perpetual war)
Tone: dozens of petty crowns rising and falling with each harvest, death, and feud.
Surface: dozens of ever-shifting petty kingdoms, republics, principalities; borders/rulers change with every death or bad harvest; lightly populated, varied terrain, ruins everywhere. Origin: 500 yrs ago water-wars broke the League of Many Rivers; refugees flooded in carrying ancestral grudges that still drive the feuds.
Rule: anyone with a claim — Dragon-Blooded-descent, folk hero, spirit, ancestor, a god's Chosen; champions get thrust into rule.
Defense/dangers: **military weakness is the defining fact** — most states are too small for armies (house guards + farmers from the fields), so the region is a perpetual target; locals fight guerrilla, using terrain to bleed invaders.
Clocks: Outside Pressure [2/6] — Lookshy (Nasaru Redoubt), Greyfalls (eastern border, controls the Golden Road), and Vaneha (preparing reconquest) all press in.
Hooks: **Rake** (Queen Lapis; her husband Datei is her blackmail-and-frame hatchet-man; three heirs scheme); **Theris** (republic trapped in a predatory mercenary contract with Nexus's Sisters of the Turning Wheel); "one village's hero is another's monster" (a Wyld Hunt vs. a local champion; a Lunar-protected town).
Handles: state forces → battle-group / CRB mortal; a perfect sandbox for a Lunar carving out a protectorate.
Links: Faction:Lookshy; Faction:Realm (Greyfalls); Place:Vaneha.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › The Hundred Kingdoms.

### Halta — Place/Nation · Northeast, the great redwood forest · Danger: moderate (fire / the floor)
Tone: cities in the boughs, bridges spanning miles of canopy, the forest floor a forbidden hunting-ground.
Surface: a treetop nation in giant redwoods between two Silver River tributaries; cities in the boughs linked by aerial walkways; isolated, largely sheltered. Intelligent animal companions are a legal class with rights.
Rule: a loose alliance of city-states ruled jointly from **Chanta** by the **Ferulae** (one delegate per tree-city, 5-yr term); larger cities elect a prince + council.
Defense/dangers: **the Pact** — Haltans own the trees, the **Fair Folk own the floor**; price = each city sacrifices 3 people/year (sent down to be hunted); fail to pay = lose protection. Fire is the existential threat (lightning, Linowan sabotage, the fae fire-court).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Chief fae-negotiator priest **Wilander Alrunas** is bewitched — having an affair with the faerie merchant Silver Silk on the forest floor. — reveal on: tailing him / a Socialize or Occult success.
Clocks: Groundwalker Cult [2/6] — a Fair-Folk-worshiping faction pushes to renegotiate the Pact and return to the floor; Prince Aune Ilavarin secretly grants them audiences.
Hooks: the war with the **Linowan** flares (Halta trades Linowan war-prisoners to the fae to cut its own tithe); the **Oracle Tree** at Chanta grants prophetic visions; a city falls behind on its tribute.
Handles: Haltan rangers → CRB elite mortal (canopy fighters); Fair Folk → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`. Fire = environmental catastrophe lever.
Links: Place:The Linowan (enemy); Faction:Fair Folk; East overview.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Republic of Halta.

### The Linowan — Place/Nation · Northeast, Silver River wetlands · Danger: moderate (raiders)
Tone: stilt-island villages of autumn-haired masters, spear in hand, river-goddess at their back.
Surface: river-dwellers on the Silver River's floodplain in hundreds of **crannog** (stilt-island) settlements; feared raiders and sought mercenaries; prize excellence in every craft. Friendly to fair traders; raid those who skimp on gifts, cheat, or (worst) take slaves on Linowan land.
Rule: a meritocratic **mask system** — master a skill, get tested at **Rubylak**, become a noble with a spirit-blessed **animal-mask** (real magical boons); greatest masters = royalty; the **High Queen** reigns for life as sacred wife to river-goddess Yanae.
Defense/dangers: guerrilla raiders; capital **Rubylak** is a fortified holy island that bars all foreigners (everyone masked/hooded).
Truth (GM-only):
  - High Queen **Arkasi** is courting Dynasts (Cynis Falen, Ragara Banoba) to crush Halta now the Empress is gone; Realm "ambassador" **Cynis Legat** freelances for House Cynis; forest-witch **Amerine** covertly spies on the Realm and sabotages the Immaculate faith. — reveal on: court intrigue / a spy-network success.
Clocks: War with Halta [3/6] — centuries old; Linowan raid up-river for precious redwood and vengeance; the Empress's absence tempts a decisive push.
Hooks: a Linowan noble needs a champion; the mask-ritual (a 2-dot artifact) is sought; the Halta war could pull in House Cynis and the Guild.
Handles: Linowan warriors → CRB elite mortal / battle-group; masks → minor artifacts.
Links: Place:Halta (enemy); Faction:Realm (House Cynis); Faction:Fair Folk.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › The Linowan.

### Wolf's Paw — Place · East, north of the Golden Road · Danger: moderate (the floor / the wolves)
Tone: talking wolves lounging on silk in a half-ruined city while humans live in fear.
Surface: an isolated, overgrown valley city conquered by intelligent **talking wolves** from the nearby **Uskwood** bordermarch; humans are servants (meat is for wolves). Wolves must whelp and hunt inside the Uskwood to stay intelligent (predators grow smarter eating other Uskwood-born beasts).
Rule: ~a dozen aristocratic wolf clans jockey over carved-up territory; they appoint a **human council** (one human per clan) to handle human affairs and keep problems from wolf fangs.
Truth (GM-only):
  - A secret human cult to the poison-god **Auled Evening-Beauty** plots against the wolves — **Belah**, a 20-year household servant of elder wolf Elizar, is a planted cult assassin. — reveal on: investigating the household / an Occult tip.
Clocks: Wolf Succession [2/6] — young wolf **Nathian** builds an independent following, resentful of the nobles; orthodox Immaculate human converts murmur about "the rightful order."
Hooks: a trusted human is invited to run with the pack on a raid; the Uskwood's prey-farms need tending; nearby Thresh tolerates rogue talking wolves as a form of rebellion.
Handles: wolves → CRB great-cat/wolf templates upscaled (intelligent); Uskwood beasts → `../../statblocks/00_index.md`. (No named Lunar patron — Wyld/bordermarch wolves; a thematic Lunar tie-in, not a dominion.)
Links: Faction:Realm (Greyfalls agent Rosewood Dohera watches the Uskwood); the Wyld (`../03_otherworlds.md`).
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Wolf's Paw.

### Champoor, the Nighted City — Place · East/Dreaming Sea, fallen Simharajala · Danger: high (eternal night)
Tone: a city in perpetual twilight, run from the shadows by a court of secret-keeping gods.
Surface: an eternally dark city (no true dawn since the Pact) on the western Dreaming Sea; former capital of the fallen Simharajala empire; nominally ruled by an emperor + caste system, actually run from the shadows. Besieged by Prasad, it accepted the dragon-goddess **Tenepeshu** and her **Court of Secrets**, who annihilated the invaders in 3 days of darkness — and the city has lived in twilight, worshiping the Court, ever since.
Rule: weak Emperor **Champara Sanjhar Ishao** over five castes (Sanjhar > Kadhar > Silhar > Vashar > Rorhar), each with its own ward; the **Kadhar (warrior caste) Collapse** left most of the city outside the top wards anarchic.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Court runs **the Game of Secrets** — gods dig up mortals' secrets to force them into cults (lower wards) and ruin rivals' cults by exposing scandals (upper wards). It drives all crime and cult politics. — reveal on: getting blackmailed / an Investigation into a cult.
  - Emperor's chief advisor **Mehra Kadhar Sarala** is being sounded out by conspirators for a coup (and plans to reconquer the rebel Tanjipal islands). — reveal on: court intrigue.
Clocks: Coup Pressure [2/6]; Prasad's Squeeze [2/6] — expansionist Prasad still wants the city and chokes its makko-incense supply.
Hooks: the Silver Wheel thief-cult; the Garden of Twilight's Pearls (courtesan house whose overseer secretly blood-sacrifices slavers); the Brimstone Fleet pirates hunt Champoor ships.
Handles: cultists/mercs → CRB mortal; Court gods → `../../statblocks/00_index.md` (gods/spirits). Secrets are the city's combat — gather, trade, weaponize.
Links: Faction:Realm (Prasad); the Dreaming Sea; Faction:The Guild.
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › Champoor, the Nighted City.
