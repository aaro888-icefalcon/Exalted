# 04 — Factions (the Starting Faction Board)

The powers that move Creation. Each uses the **faction stat block**:
- **Magnitude** — raw size/might (1 local … 5 world-spanning).
- **Cohesion** — how unified (fractured ↔ disciplined); low = it fights itself.
- **Trouble** — the pressure currently fracturing or driving it.
- **Features** — what it's good at / its assets.
- **Problems** — its weaknesses / what bleeds it.
- **Interest** — what it wants from the PC / how it reacts to a Lunar.
- **Reach** — where it can act.
- **Goal** — its agenda.

Then **Track (−3…+3)** = the PC's standing, plus Surface / Truth(secrets) / Clocks / Hooks / Leadership / Links / Vault. These double as the **starting faction board** — set Tracks at session zero and tick the clocks as play proceeds.

> The campaign's master tension: the **Realm** (wounded, Empress gone) vs. the **Silver Pact** (the PC's kin, striking while the iron's cold), with the **Deathlords**, **Guild**, **Lookshy**, **Immaculate Order**, **Fair Folk**, and **Bureau of Destiny** as the other players.

### The Silver Pact — Faction · everywhere Lunars roam (no fixed seat) · the PC's kin
Magnitude: 3 (few but mighty Celestials) · Cohesion: low (no hierarchy; cells, mentors, feuds) · Trouble: schism over strategy + whether to ally with returned Solars.
Features: the only society of Lunar Exalted; mentorship + cub-rescue; Wyld-bordering redoubts; spy webs; centuries of lore; shapeshifting infiltration; sorcery.
Problems: leaderless and faction-ridden; thinly spread; the Wyld Hunt picks off the untrained; elder grudges (Raksi vs. Ma-Ha-Suchi) paralyze councils.
Interest: a Lunar PC is **kin by default** (Track starts +1); the Pact wants the PC mentored, fighting the Realm, and answering favor-debts.
Reach: the Threshold and deep wilds; thin on the Blessed Isle.
Goal: tear down the Realm (the usurping Shogunate's heir) and raise the next Lunar generation.
Track to PC: **+1** (kin).
Surface: led by **Shahan-ya** (elder mentors) who find/train new "cubs," fix their caste with moonsilver tattoos, and shelter them; bitterly opposed to the Realm; runs on a **favor economy** (bargain, don't command) and **councils**.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Pact is split on the returned Solars — some seek the old bonds, some want them controlled or killed. — reveal on: attending a council / a mentor's confidence.
  - Three war-approaches run in parallel and their leaders feud: **Attrition** (dominant; bleed the Realm) / **Fortification** (build dominions, e.g. Iscomay) / **Open War** (Sha'a Oka, the Caul). — reveal on: involvement in Pact strategy.
  - The Pact never forgot the **Sidereals** (the Bronze Faction architected the Usurpation + Wyld Hunt) though Creation has; some Abyssals carry slain Solar mates' souls. — reveal on: deep lore / a betrayal.
Clocks: War on the Realm [3/8] — ticks on Realm weakness / Lunar victories; Cub Survival [—] — per rescued/lost new Lunar; Solar Question [1/6] — ticks as returned Solars force the Pact's hand.
Hooks: a mentor assigns the PC to retrieve a newly-Exalted cub before the Wyld Hunt; a shahan-ya calls in a blood-debt; a factional dispute over allying with a returned Solar.
Leadership (Shahan-yas): Ma-Ha-Suchi · Raksi · Leviathan · Sha'a Oka · Lilith · Golden-Leaf Liseli · Rukhsara · Tayan Silver-Crowned (+ more) → `05_named_npcs.md`.
Links: Faction:Realm (enemy); Faction:Immaculate Order/Wyld Hunt (enemy); the Lunar dominions (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`); Concept:Lunar/Chimerism (`01_cosmology.md`).

### The Realm (the Scarlet Empire) — Faction · the Blessed Isle + satrapies · the prime enemy
Magnitude: 5 (world-spanning) · Cohesion: dropping fast (Empress gone; Houses arming for civil war) · Trouble: succession crisis; ~7-year clock to a declared abdication.
Features: ~10,000 Dragon-Blooded; the Imperial Legions (40 at the disappearance, now **partitioned among the Houses**); the Imperial Navy; the All-Seeing Eye (spies); the Immaculate Order (state faith + Wyld Hunt); the world's wealth.
Problems: no Empress = no keystone (no veto, no paymaster, no spymaster); most Houses deep in debt; legions purged of loyalists and demoralized (~10,000 deserted); the Wyld Hunt at its lowest ebb ever (garrisons pulled home).
Interest: a Lunar is **Anathema** — kill on sight where the Hunt can reach (Track starts deeply negative). Individual Houses/agents may deal with a deniable outsider in stolen skin.
Reach: total on the Blessed Isle; contracting across the Threshold.
Goal: hold together / install a new Empress; survive the interregnum without civil war.
Track to PC: **−3** (Anathema) by default.
Surface: the Dragon-Blooded empire ruling Creation from the Isle; nominally led by Regent **Tepet Fokuf** + the **Deliberative**; ten **Great Houses** (below) carve up legions and satrapies.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Empress's vizier at the founding was **Chejop Kejak** (Bronze Faction Sidereal); the Realm exists to suppress returning Solars/Lunars. — reveal on: deep lore / the Bronze Faction.
  - The **All-Seeing Eye** has been infiltrated since inception by Bronze Faction Sidereals AND, lately, by the disgraced House **Iselsi** funneling intel to its elders. — reveal on: a counterintelligence brush.
Clocks: Succession War [3/8] — ticks toward open civil war; Wyld Hunt Decline [—] — Threshold grip slips; Empress Returns? [0/?] — a wildcard the GM may arm.
Hooks: a House recruits a deniable strike on a rival; a magistrate (beloved by commoners, losing a shadow-war with the ministries) crosses the PC's path; a Wyld Hunt is dispatched after the PC.
Leadership: Regent Tepet Fokuf; the Deliberative (Master Ragara Julinei); heirs **Mnemon** & **V'neef**; the absent **Scarlet Empress** → `05_named_npcs.md`.
Links: Faction:Great Houses; Faction:Immaculate Order; Faction:All-Seeing Eye; Place:Blessed Isle (`directions/blessed_isle.md`).
Vault: The Realm › Chapters One–Three; Core › Chapter Two › The Realm.

### The Great Houses — Faction cluster · the Blessed Isle · the Realm's competing dynasties
Magnitude: 4 (each a power; together = the Realm) · Cohesion: low (they're rivals) · Trouble: the throne is empty and everyone wants it.
Features/Problems/play per House (legions in parentheses):
- **Cathak (8 — largest army)** — premier military house; aggressive, growing pious; a **kingmaker** (offered to back a viable claimant → showered with bribes).
- **Sesus (7)** — wealthy, cruel reputation; keenest anti-Guild ops + a vast spy net; pushes dark-horse claimant **Sesus Raenyah**.
- **Mnemon (5)** — pious public-works house; led by heir **Mnemon**; bankrolled by the Immaculate Order; a leading throne contender (Old Dragons lean to her).
- **Peleps (4 + the Imperial Navy)** — honorable, runs the Navy; reeling from losing the Merchant Fleet to V'neef; clashes with V'neef in the West.
- **Cynis (3)** — hedonists/physicians; **Imperial slave-trade monopoly** + underworld; lax on heresy.
- **Ledaal (3)** — the anti-Anathema house; wages a ruthless "Shadow Crusade" (demons, witches, fae, Anathema); raised the alarm on the Solars.
- **Ragara (3 + the Realm's bank)** — predatory moneylenders; **dread a debt-jubilee from a new Empress**, so call in debts in hard coin (antagonizing everyone).
- **Nellens (2)** — weak-blooded "black sheep," but strong with commoners; dominates the bureaucracy.
- **V'neef (2 + the Merchant Fleet)** — newest house (~14 yrs); matriarch **V'neef** a key heir; created to check Peleps.
- **Tepet (1)** — martial house **militarily destroyed** by the Bull of the North at Futile Blood (sabotaged from home by the Empress); rebuilding by plundering rivals.
- **Iselsi (0)** — the **fallen** house: struck from the rolls for a failed assassination plot; now a clandestine spy network.
Interest: any House might use a deniable outsider against a rival; all hunt Anathema in public.
Goal: put their candidate (or themselves) on the Scarlet Throne.
Track to PC: **−2** default (less hostile than the Order; pragmatic in private).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Several **All-Seeing Eye** handlers are secret **Iselsi** working to suborn the whole Eye. — reveal on: a spy-network success.
Clocks: shares the Realm's Succession War [3/8]; House Debt Crisis [2/6] — ticks as Ragara squeezes.
Hooks: a House offers patronage for a strike on a rival; House Tepet's survivors haunt the North (`directions/north.md`); House Mnemon offers manse-repairs for fealty in the Saltspire League.
Links: Faction:The Realm; Faction:The Guild (Sesus/Cynis rivals); NPC:Mnemon, V'neef.
Vault: The Realm › Chapter One › The Great Houses; Core › The Great Houses.

### The Immaculate Order — Faction · the Blessed Isle + every satrapy · the Realm's faith & Wyld Hunt
Magnitude: 4 · Cohesion: high (disciplined hierarchy) · Trouble: the **Mouth of Peace** nears death (an interregnum that could trigger civil war); the Solars' return revives the Gold Faction.
Features: the state religion (gives the DB a divine mandate); the largest source of education/historical control; a confession/intelligence network; the **Wyld Hunt's** religious arm; the **five Immaculate Dragons** (Sextes Jylis/wood, Pasiap/earth, Mela/air, Hesiesh/fire, Daana'd/water) as exemplars.
Problems: built on a lie (below); complicit in revolts it both suppresses and leads; dependent on Realm stability.
Interest: brands **Solars and Lunars as Anathema** (always) — the PC's doctrinal nemesis; dispatches Hunts to overwhelm a Lunar "before she becomes another Bull of the North or Ma-Ha-Suchi."
Reach: anywhere the Realm proselytizes (monks travel vast distances for a Hunt).
Goal: maintain the Perfected Hierarchy + the Dragon-Blooded mandate; exterminate Anathema.
Track to PC: **−3** (Anathema).
Surface: doctrine = the **Perfected Hierarchy** (serve up, protect down, reincarnate upward); mortals forbidden to worship the Dragons directly; all worship channeled via the **Prayer Calendar** (a lever over local gods).
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Order was founded during the DB **Shogunate** (not by incarnated Dragons); the Dragons' deeds are **allegories**; Anathema are simply Exalted — a deliberately **constructed religion** part-authored by Sidereals (some texts fabricated by the Bronze Faction). — reveal on: rising in the monkhood / deep lore.
  - Inside the Order is "a second, secret order": the **Bronze Faction** of Sidereals, posing as wise monks, who **steer the Wyld Hunt** and now scramble to avert Realm civil war. Led by **Chejop Kejak** (posing as the Mouth of Peace's assistant; will die of old age soon). — reveal on: the wisest monks' confidence.
Clocks: Mouth of Peace's Death [3/6] — ticks toward an interregnum; Gold vs. Bronze [2/6] — ticks as Gold Faction re-emerges to back Solars; Wyld Hunt on the PC [—].
Hooks: a Wyld Hunt is sent after the PC; an Immaculate inquisitor investigates a "heresy" the PC is tangled in; the Mouth of Peace seeks to handpick a successor.
Leadership: the **Mouth of Peace**; **Chejop Kejak** (Bronze Faction) → `05_named_npcs.md`.
Links: Faction:The Realm; Faction:Bureau of Destiny (Sidereals); Concept:The Usurpation (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: The Realm › Chapter Four: The Immaculate Order.

### Lookshy — Faction · East / River Province · the Shogunate-heir war-machine
Magnitude: 3 · Cohesion: high (disciplined military gentes) · Trouble: Lunar mercenaries (the Green Rose) bleed it in the River Province; Realm instability shifts the board.
Features: the pre-eminent Eastern **military** power; heir to the Dragon-Blooded **Shogunate**; hoards First Age weapons (warstriders, implosion bows); the elite **Seventh Legion**; an excellent General Staff + intelligence.
Problems: surrounded by ambitious neighbors; a small Dragon-Blooded population vs. the Realm; targeted by Lunar attrition.
Interest: another Shogunate-heir → shares the Realm's wrath toward Lunars, but **independent of the Realm** (a potential lever/rival, not the Empire itself).
Reach: the River Province + the western Hundred Kingdoms (Nasaru Redoubt).
Goal: defend its hegemony in the Scavenger Lands; survive between the Realm and the free cities.
Track to PC: **−2** (Lunar-hostile, but not the Wyld Hunt).
Surface: a militaristic city-state ruled by Dragon-Blooded **gentes**, defending the free Scavenger Lands as the region's strongest army.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The mercenary **Company of the Green Rose** harrying Lookshy is secretly led by the Lunar **Blackpetal Mothwing** ("Helm Beetle") to bleed Lookshy out of the River Province; the General Staff suspects but can't prove it. — reveal on: a Pact contact / espionage.
Clocks: River-Province War [2/6] — ticks with the Green Rose's campaign; Mask of Winters Threat [—] — the Deathlord eyes the River Province (`03_otherworlds.md`).
Hooks: Lookshy hires (or hunts) outsiders; the Green Rose recruits the PC against it; Lookshy and a Deathlord may become uneasy mutual problems.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact (Green Rose); Faction:Deathlords (Mask of Winters); East (`directions/east.md`); Faction:The Guild.
Vault: Core › The River Province (Lookshy); Lunars › Other Dominions (the Green Rose).

### The Guild — Faction · everywhere there is trade (seat: Nexus) · the merchant leviathan
Magnitude: 4 · Cohesion: medium (a profit-driven directorate, not a state) · Trouble: would seize Nexus but fears the Emissary; the Realm's instability disrupts and tempts it.
Features: the continent-spanning trade combine; controls caravans, the slave trade, mercenaries, money-lending, and rare goods; deep pockets buy access everywhere.
Problems: no army of its own (hires mercenaries); reviled (slavers); checked by Nexus's Council + the Emissary; antagonized by anti-Guild Houses (Sesus).
Interest: amoral and transactional — will **deal with anyone, including a Lunar**, where it profits; the Silver Pact uses it as a tool, not an ally (supports it where it undermines the Realm, attacks it where it serves the Realm).
Reach: every market in Creation; thickest in the East, North, and along the trade roads.
Goal: profit and the expansion of trade above all loyalties.
Track to PC: **0** (neutral/transactional).
Surface: the great merchant combine; its Directorate sits across the Big Market from the Nexus Council; most caravans route through it.
Truth (GM-only):
  - Guild factors are riddled with double/triple agents (e.g., Ma-Ha-Suchi's man Ranotis feeds false intel to factor Miral, now a triple agent for Greyfalls' satrap). — reveal on: an intelligence dig.
Clocks: Guild vs. Nexus Council [2/6] — ticks toward a power grab; War Profiteering [—] — the Guild battens on the Realm's chaos.
Hooks: the Guild hires the PC (caravan guard, recovery, sabotage); a slaver-factor is a target (cf. Nexus's Liberating Ones); the Guild backs a faction the PC opposes.
Links: Place:Nexus (`directions/east.md`); Faction:The Realm (frenemy); Faction:Silver Pact (a tool); the slave trade (everywhere).
Vault: Core › The Guild; Across the Eight Directions › Nexus (Guild Influence).

### The Deathlords — Faction · the Underworld + shadowlands · undead end-bringers
Magnitude: 4 (each a power; collectively dominate the Underworld) · Cohesion: very low (they perpetually war among themselves) · Trouble: rivalry + the secret death-wish that drives them.
Features: ancient **undead sorcerer-kings**; legions of the dead + bone war-machines; the **Abyssal Exalted** ("deathknights") as their knights; necromancy; chalcedony citadels; behemoths (Juggernaut).
Problems: they fight each other (the dead are NOT a unified engine); bound to dead masters; few in number (more than five, fewer than a score).
Interest: would corrupt or destroy the living; some Abyssals carry slain **Solar mates' souls** (a fraught Lunar connection). Hostile, but exploitable against the Realm.
Reach: the Underworld; into Creation through shadowlands (Thorns, Skullstone, the North).
Goal: serve their dead masters and, ultimately, **end all existence**.
Track to PC: **−2** (hostile; situationally a shared-enemy ally vs. the Realm).
Surface: dark lords of the Underworld; no two alike; their deathknights ride out from bleak citadels.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Deathlords are the **ghosts of First Age Solars**, sworn to the **Neverborn** (dead Primordials rotting in the Labyrinth) whom they feed souls — aiming to drag all of existence into oblivion. — reveal on: deep Underworld lore / an Abyssal's confidence.
Clocks: Mask of Winters' Expansion [2/8] — toward the River Province; the Lover's Northern reach [—]; the Silver Prince's Western scheme [2/6] (stirring Azure's Siakal cult).
Hooks: a Deathlord (or deathknight) becomes a shared enemy or a tempting ally; a smuggled Underworld good threatens a city's wards (Whitewall); an Abyssal carries a face the PC knows.
Leadership (named): **Mask of Winters** (took Thorns; eyes the River Province) · **The Lover Clad in the Raiment of Tears** (the North; binds the passion-dead as the Loveless) · **Bodhisattva Anointed by Dark Water / "the Silver Prince"** (Skullstone; canny trader-admiral) → `05_named_npcs.md`.
Links: Place:The Underworld, Thorns, Skullstone (`03_otherworlds.md`); the Abyssal Exalted (`01_cosmology.md`); Concept:The Usurpation.
Vault: Core › The Deathlords; Core › The Fall of Thorns.

### The Fair Folk (raksha) — Faction · the Wyld & bordermarches · chaos courts
Magnitude: 3 (diffuse; armies on a crusade) · Cohesion: low (warring courts of mask-wearing nobles) · Trouble: a new predator (the **hannya**) hunts them; the world's fixity oppresses them.
Features: soul/dream-eating immortals; gossamer-armies + lesser fae (hobgoblins, reavers) + tamed behemoths; pacts that bind even the dead (Whitewall); bordermarch dominions.
Problems: weaken without a Pole's substance; soulless and self-warring; bound by their own bargains (a lever).
Interest: covet the PC's **heroic soul/dreams**; may raid, bargain, or be bargained with (some Pact Lunars ally with fae nobles — controversially).
Reach: Creation's rim in every Direction (Winter Folk in the North, isle-raksha in the West, jungle-fae in the SE).
Goal: feed on Creation's souls/dreams; unmake its oppressive certainty.
Track to PC: **−1** (predatory, but bargain-able).
Surface: beautiful, soulless chaos-beings who feed on souls and dreams; see Place card in `03_otherworlds.md`.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The **Bronze Tide** flees the soul-eating raksha **Spear-Empress Bhadri** + her 12 companions, whose territory expands yearly (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`). — reveal on: Western/SW campaigning.
Clocks: Wyld Tide [—] — the rim ebbs/surges with the moon; a court's scheme (Frost-Bites-into-Envy wants to freeze the North's seasons forever) [2/6].
Hooks: a fae noble offers a (treacherous) bargain; a raid threatens a town the PC protects; a Pact dispute over allying with the fae.
Links: Place:The Wyld (`03_otherworlds.md`); Place:Halta, Whitewall; the Bronze Tide.

### The Bureau of Destiny (the Sidereals) — Faction · Yu-Shan (hidden) · the puppeteers of fate
Magnitude: 3 (100 only, but fate amplifies them) · Cohesion: split (**Bronze** vs. **Gold** factions) · Trouble: the Solars' return has riven them; the Realm they prop up is fracturing.
Features: the **100 Sidereal Exalted**; manipulate the **Loom of Fate**; Sidereal Martial Arts (legendary secret styles); astrology; control of the **Immaculate Order** (Bronze) and a heretical Solar-supporting cult (Gold, the Cult of the Illuminated); the Heavenly bureaucracy.
Problems: erased from memory (most of Creation forgot they exist); few; their pawns "run amok" as the Realm cracks; internal schism.
Interest: the architects of the Lunars' ancient ruin (Bronze) — the deepest enemy. But the **Gold Faction** seeks out Solars (and might treat with Lunars), and the Pact has made wary overtures despite deep distrust.
Reach: anywhere fate runs (via hidden gates); deepest inside the Order and the Realm's destiny.
Goal: Bronze — hold the ancient prophecy, suppress Solars/Lunars, keep the Realm together; Gold — guide the returned Solars to avert catastrophe.
Track to PC: **−2** (Bronze; the old enemy) / **0–+1** (a Gold contact, situationally).
Surface: officially they "don't exist"; manuscripts mentioning them vanish; they appear when destiny trembles, claim to act for Heaven, and disappear.
Truth (GM-only):
  - The Sidereals **murdered and exiled the Solars in the Usurpation** (a prophecy said only this saved Creation) and bound their souls in jade — the wound behind the Lunars' rage. — reveal on: deep lore / a Pact elder.
  - **Chejop Kejak** leads the Bronze majority (running the Order); the **Gold Faction** broke away to back the Solars. — reveal on: the Bronze/Gold split surfacing.
Clocks: Gold vs. Bronze [2/6]; the prophecy's grip [—] — ticks as the returned Solars/Lunars defy it.
Hooks: a Sidereal nudges events around the PC (the world "conspires"); a Gold-Faction agent makes contact via the Cult of the Illuminated; the PC uncovers a fate-working.
Leadership: **Chejop Kejak** (Bronze) → `05_named_npcs.md`; Gold-Faction agents (generate / `../statblocks/00_index.md` AotR Sidereals).
Links: Faction:Immaculate Order (Bronze runs it); Faction:Silver Pact (old enemy / wary overtures); Place:Yu-Shan (`03_otherworlds.md`); Concept:Fate (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: Core › The Sidereal Exalted (Bronze and Gold); The Realm › The Bronze Faction.

### Malfeas / the demon-cults — Faction · Hell + summoners across Creation · the imprisoned creators
Magnitude: 4 (the Yozis are titanic, but imprisoned) · Cohesion: n/a (the Yozis rage; demons obey oaths) · Trouble: eternal imprisonment; they crave escape and worship.
Features: the **Yozis** (overthrown Primordials) + their enslaved **demons** (summonable by sorcery; First/Second/Third Circle); demon-cults in Creation (the **Lintha** worship Kimbery; Yozi cults at Lathe, in An-Teng's Seven-Stranded Vine).
Problems: the Yozis can't leave Hell; demons enter only via summoning or torn rifts; bound by oath.
Interest: demons serve a binding sorcerer; cults pursue power/escape. A wildcard the PC may summon, fight, or stumble into.
Reach: Malfeas; anywhere a sorcerer summons or a cult festers.
Goal: (Yozis) escape and revenge on Creation/Heaven; (demons) survive, gain worship, escape.
Track to PC: **0** (transactional via summoning) / **−2** (active demon-cults).
Surface: the demon-city prison of the Yozis under a green sun; see Place card in `03_otherworlds.md`.
Truth (GM-only):
  - **Gaia** pleaded mercy for her imprisoned siblings — which the demon princes will never forgive; demon omens harm bystanders. — reveal on: deep occult lore.
Clocks: a demon-cult's scheme (e.g., the Lintha's Dukantha promise; a summoned demon slipping its binding) [2/6].
Hooks: a sorcerer (PC or NPC) summons a demon; a demon-cult menaces a community; a bound demon offers a Faustian bargain.

## Setting the board (session-zero quick-use)
1. Place **Tracks**: Silver Pact +1; Realm/Immaculate Order −3 (Anathema); Great Houses/Lookshy/Deathlords/Bureau(Bronze) −2; Fair Folk −1; Guild/Malfeas 0. Adjust to the PC's history.
2. Arm 2–3 **clocks** to start (e.g., Realm Succession War [3/8], Pact War on the Realm [3/8], plus one local — the Bull's Imperial Unravelling, a Wyld Hunt on the PC).
3. Each scene end: tick clocks PC action or time moved; shift a Track on a notable favor/betrayal; follow Links to update connected cards.
