# 01 — Cosmology (Creation, Essence, the Chosen, the Curse, the Age)

The conceptual spine. Concept cards: what it is · how it shows in play · what the GM rolls · limits. For places see `directions/`; for the otherworlds see `03_otherworlds.md`.

---

### Creation — Concept · the world itself · the stage
What it is: a flat world floating on the chaos-sea of the **Wyld**; Second Age of Man. Center = the **Blessed Isle** (Realm's seat), ringed by the **Inland Sea**, then the **Threshold** (all outer lands), then the Wyld at the rim. Five **Elemental Poles** anchor it: **Earth** (the Imperial Mountain, dead center, most geomantically stable), **Air** (N), **Wood** (E), **Fire** (S), **Water** (W).
In play: orientation + lethality dial. Toward the center = lawful, fixed, Realm-controlled. Toward the rim = thinner reality, Wyld bleed, Lunar/barbarian turf, less Realm reach. Distance from the Pole of Earth ≈ how stable reality is.
GM rolls: travel times in weeks; "is the Realm present here?" odds drop sharply with distance from the Isle.
Limits: edges are a tattered interstitial zone, not a clean wall — bordermarches, shadowlands, and satrapies all blur the map.
Vault: Core › Chapter Two: The Age of Sorrows (intro); Core › The Realm (overview).

### Essence — Concept · the fuel of all magic · always-on
What it is: the fundamental force composing all things; ebbs/flows in life, land, storms, fate. Mortals can't command it; the Exalted direct it into miracles (innate caste powers, learned **Charms**, and **sorcery** that rewrites Creation's laws). Its flavor changes with source — elemental Essence is primal, the dead's is cold, demonic is alien.
In play: the resource economy (motes); spent on Charms, recovered slowly from ambient Essence. An Exalt drained to empty is "little more than mortal for a time."
GM rolls: per the `ex_*`/`dice.py` engine (mote pools, Charm activation, sorcery).
Limits: limited resource in a mortal frame; demesnes/hearthstones/manses are the levers that pool and amplify it (places of power; see Handles on place cards).
Vault: Core › Chapter One: The Exalted (Essence, The Tools of the Chosen).

### The Exalted — Concept · god-chosen champions · power tiers at a glance
What it is: mortal heroes uplifted by the gods to wield Essence ("the Second Breath"). **Terrestrial** (Dragon-Blooded) = weakest individually, most numerous. **Celestial** (Solar, Lunar, Sidereal) = greatest, rare. Plus the new/odd: Abyssal, Liminal, Exigent. Long-lived (DB 250–300 yrs; Solar/Lunar 2,000–3,000; Sidereal 3,500–5,000), self-healing, youthful until their last years; most die by violence, not age.

| Type | Patron | Tier read | Niche |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solar** ("Lawgivers"/"Anathema") | Unconquered Sun | apex | mightiest all-rounders; 5 castes (Dawn/Zenith/Twilight/Night/Eclipse); return drives the plot |
| **Lunar** ("Anathema") | Luna | apex | shapeshifters (heart's blood); 3 castes by moon phase (Full/Changing/No Moon); **the PC's kind** |
| **Sidereal** | the 5 Maidens | hidden | 100 only; fate-manipulators, secret martial arts; erased from memory; rule from the shadows |
| **Dragon-Blooded** | the Elemental Dragons | common-but-strong | the Realm's rulers; 5 aspects (Air/Earth/Fire/Water/Wood); inherited, numerous |
| **Abyssal** ("deathknights") | the Deathlords/Neverborn | apex-dark | death-mirrors of Solars; necromancy; first seen at Thorns; **new, unrecorded** |
| **Liminal** | Underworld power + living hands | varies | patchwork undead-born ghost-hunters; no organization |
| **Exigent** | a desperate god | wildly varies | one-of-a-kind divine champions; unpredictable powers |

In play: a foe's type sets the fight's lethality and the right statblock (see `../statblocks/00_index.md`). For the PC: a **Lunar** — see `../lunar/` for the rules layer.
GM rolls: combat/social per engine; foe selection by tier.
Limits: only Solars, Lunars, DBs are "common"; the rest are rare set pieces. **Anathema** (Solar/Lunar) are hunted on sight in Realm lands (see `04_factions.md` › Immaculate Order, Wyld Hunt).
Vault: Core › Chapter One: The Exalted (The Roll of the Chosen); Core › Ch8 Antagonists › The Exalted.

### Luna & the Lunar Exalted — Concept · the PC's nature · apex predator-heroes
What it is: Luna's Chosen — "Creation's divine apex predators," made to hunt the gods' enemies; creatures of **transgression and boundaries** (hero/monster, civilization/wild, mortal/divine). Three Second-Age castes by moon phase: **Full Moon** (warriors), **Changing Moon** (tricksters/infiltrators), **No Moon** (witches/sorcerers). Every Lunar has a **spirit shape** (a true-self animal) and shapeshifts by drinking **heart's blood** (steal the form of any man or beast). **Tells** = transgressive/divine traits marking the Chosen; **moonsilver tattoos** (from the Silver Pact) lock the caste and armor against the Wyld. Luna of Many Faces personally attends every Exaltation.
In play: shapeshifting is the signature weapon — *anyone might be a disguised Lunar.* Essence fever channels into **rage** (acts on anger already in the heart). The PC's home turf is the **North** (`directions/north.md`) and Lunar dominions; default kin = the **Silver Pact**.
GM rolls: shapeshifting/heart's blood per `../lunar/02_shapeshifting.md`; the Great Curse (below).
Limits: untattooed/untrained young Lunars are unstable and Wyld-Hunt-vulnerable; chimerism (Wyld dissolution of self) is the long shadow.
Vault: Core › The Lunar Exalted; Lunars: Fangs at the Gate › Chapter One: The Lunar Exalted.

### The Great Curse — Concept · the flaw in every Exalt · hidden engine
What it is: a curse woven into Exaltation by the dying Primordials. For Lunars it surfaces as **Limit** building toward a **Limit Break** — a forced, distorted outburst of the Lunar's own rage/nature when the Limit Trigger fires. (Solars suffer it differently; all Celestials carry it.)
In play: Limit accrues from honoring/violating Intimacies under pressure; at full, the GM (or oracle) takes the wheel for a scene — the PC acts on the Curse, not free will.
GM rolls: Limit gain per the Curse rules; the Break is mandatory narrative, optionally shaped by an oracle question.
Limits: it's a periodic loss-of-agency beat, not a constant; it makes a Lunar dangerous to allies, not just enemies.
Vault: Lunars: Fangs at the Gate › Chapter Five › The Great Curse.
Truth (GM-only): the Curse is the Neverborn's death-curse, the same wound that twisted the Solars in the Usurpation — reveal on: deep occult research, a Sidereal's confidence, or a Limit Break's revelation.

### Fate & the Loom — Concept · destiny as a system · usually offscreen
What it is: the future is partly woven on the **Loom of Fate** in Yu-Shan, tended by the **Bureau of Destiny** under the Five Maidens; the **Sidereals** read and re-write it. Mortals glimpse it via astrology. Some beings (the Exalted, the Wyld, the dead) sit askew to fate.
In play: explains omens, prophecy, and why Sidereals always seem to have the world on their side. A Fate Question's odds can be nudged by fate-aware forces.
GM rolls: oracle/Fate Questions; astrology as a thaumaturgy.
Limits: the Wyld and the Underworld lie outside fate's weave; Lunars are only loosely bound.
Vault: Core › Heaven; Core › The Sidereal Exalted (Masters of Fate).

### The Spirit World — Concept · gods/elementals/demons · the unseen majority
What it is: Creation teems with divinities, all hungry for **worship** (it grants them power). **Gods** = immaterial overseers of a portfolio (a place/thing/idea) + cult; by Heaven's law they can't intercede directly in Creation, though many now defy this. **Elementals** = naturally material spirits born of natural energies; ancient ones become **lesser elemental dragons** (often **censors** enforcing celestial law). **Demons** = enslaved souls/children of the imprisoned **Yozis**, bound by oath, summonable by sorcery. **God-Blooded** = their mortal-divine children.
In play: spirits run cults, grant boons, levy banes, sire agents, and can Exalt **Exigents**. Materialize/dematerialize is the key combat wrinkle. The Lunar PC navigates a Threshold where local gods are players, not scenery.
GM rolls: spirit foes from `../statblocks/00_index.md` (gods/elementals/demons); worship as a social/economic lever.
Limits: immaterial gods can't physically act unless materialized; the Creation-Ruling Mandate belongs to the Exalted, not the gods.
Vault: Core › The Spirits of the World (Gods, Elementals, Demons, Other Divinities).

### The Age of Sorrows / Time of Tumult — Concept · the NOW · the campaign clock
What it is: the present era. ~**RY 768**; the **Scarlet Empress vanished ~5 years ago** (during Calibration, RY 763), removing the keystone that held the Realm together. Cascade: Great Houses arm for a succession war; legions pulled home off the Threshold; **Wyld Hunt at its lowest ebb ever**; rogue gods skim worship; prisoners escaped the Imperial Manse; **Thorns fell** to the Deathlord Mask of Winters (RY 764); the **Solars have returned**. It is, for the Silver Pact, the long-awaited moment of Realm weakness.
In play: the master clock. Realm reach is contracting → Lunar/Threshold opportunity is expanding. Every faction is in motion (see `04_factions.md`); the Empress may or may not return.
GM rolls: the standing faction clocks on `04_factions.md`; "is the Realm here?" odds.
Limits: it is a *window*, not a collapse — the Realm is wounded, not dead, and could re-consolidate under a new Empress (a debt-jubilee dread that drives House Ragara).
Vault: Core › Chapter Two: The Age of Sorrows (Measuring Time; A Report on the Disappearance of the Scarlet Empress); The Realm › An Empty Throne.

### The Usurpation & the lost First Age — Concept · the wound behind everything · deep backstory
What it is: in the First Age the Solars ruled a golden, sorcerous Creation with Lunars as their bonded seconds/spouses. The **Sidereals' Bronze Faction** prophesied that only by murdering and exiling the Solars would Creation survive; they rallied the **Dragon-Blooded** to slaughter them (the Usurpation) and bound Solar souls in a jade prison. The DB **Shogunate** rose; the **Great Contagion** + **Fair Folk Invasion** then shattered it; the Scarlet Empress seized a First Age superweapon and built the **Realm** from the ruins.
In play: the source of the Lunars' rage and vendetta (they failed to protect their slain Solar mates), the Immaculate Order's lie, and the returned Solars' explosive significance.
GM rolls: lore reveals gated as secrets (Surface vs. Truth on faction cards).
Limits: most of Creation has *forgotten* the Sidereals and the truth of the First Age — it's GM-held backstory, surfaced only when earned.
Vault: Core › The Sidereal Exalted (Bronze and Gold); Lunars: Fangs at the Gate › Chapter Two › History of the Silver Pact.
