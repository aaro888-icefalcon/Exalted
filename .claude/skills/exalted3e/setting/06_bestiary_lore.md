# 06 — Bestiary Lore (spirits · demons · behemoths · the dead · Wyld things)

Lore cards for the things the PC fights, binds, or bargains with — the *flavor + behavior + ban/lure*, with every entry pointing to its statblock in `../statblocks/00_index.md`. Threat card = What it is · Behavior/MO · Ban/weakness · Lure/sign · Lore one-liner · Stat pointer. Categories follow `01_cosmology.md`'s spirit world + `03_otherworlds.md`'s otherworlds. Full creature rosters are in HDNP + Core Ch8; the highlights below are the campaign-useful ones.

> Universal levers: **gods/elementals/demons crave worship**; **the dead fear salt and crave living blood**; **immaterial spirits must materialize to act physically**; **hungry ghosts burn in sunlight**; **demons are bound by the Yozis' oaths (summonable/bindable)**.

## Spirits — Gods & Elementals

### Elementals (overview) — Threat · naturally material spirits · tier minor→boss
What it is: spirits coalesced from natural energies (storm, fire, quake, current); **naturally material** (unlike gods); ageless but mortal; ancient ones become **lesser elemental dragons** (often **censors** enforcing celestial law). More sympathetic to mortals than gods are.
Behavior/MO: tied to their element/locale; can pose as gods or monsters to mortals; serve courts, guard places, or run wild.
Ban/weakness: bound by celestial law (censors enforce it); element-specific vulnerabilities; can be bargained with via worship/respect.
Lure/sign: intense elemental phenomena (fire-blackened living trees, singing crystals, localized storms).
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → HDNP Elementals (Cloud Person, Storm Serpent, Steel Eater, Charnavrix/Wood Dragon, etc.); CRB (Garda Bird, Tidemare, Vaktri); AotR (Wong Bongerok E7 Censor, Xemadea, Unrelenting Aton).
Vault: Core › Elementals; Hundred Devils › Chapter Three › Elementals.

### Gods (overview) — Threat/Patron · portfolio-holders · tier minor→boss
What it is: immaterial overseers of a place/thing/idea + a cult; hungry for worship; by Heaven's law cannot intercede directly in Creation (many now defy this).
Behavior/MO: grant boons for worship, levy banes/curses for disrespect, sire God-Blooded agents, scheme in the celestial bureaucracy; renegade gods extort worship (rife in the Time of Tumult).
Ban/weakness: must **materialize** to act physically; censors/celestial law constrain them; cutting their worship starves them.
Lure/sign: shrines, the **Prayer Calendar** (Immaculate-channeled), local cults, omens.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → AotR Gods (Siakal E6 war/shark-god; Ninegala forge-goddess; Resplendent Twin; Yetunda Clatterbones; Afondha); CRB (Storm Mother, Disease/Plague God, Field Guardian).
Vault: Core › Gods; Adversaries of the Righteous › Chapter Three › Gods and Spirits.

### Notable named spirits (pointers)
- **Siakal** — Western god of battle, slaughter, and sharks; bloodthirsty, forbidden cult (the Maw of Siakal) festers in Azure. — boss. (AotR)
- **Wong Bongerok, Censor of the South** — a lesser elemental dragon enforcing celestial law; a top-end legal/combat threat. — boss (E7). (AotR)

Vault: Adversaries of the Righteous › Gods and Spirits; Across the Eight Directions (place sections).

## Demons (Malfeas)

### Demons (overview) — Threat/Summon · enslaved children of the Yozis · tier standard→legendary
What it is: souls and children of the imprisoned **Yozis**, bound by their masters' oaths; counted (grudgingly) among divinity; crave worship and escape. **First Circle** = simple servitors; **Second/Third Circle** = mighty, alien-minded, expressing the Yozis' urges.
Behavior/MO: enter Creation chiefly via **sorcerous summoning** (oaths make them bindable) — they build manses, guard, serve as steeds/concubines; spread inauspicious omens (a hazard to bystanders); folklore warns of summoners overcome by their slaves.
Ban/weakness: bindable/banishable by sorcery; the binding's terms are the lever; the Yozis themselves can't leave Hell.
Lure/sign: summoning circles, demon-cults (the Lintha worship **Kimbery**; Yozi cults at Lathe / An-Teng's Seven-Stranded Vine), bad omens.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → HDNP demons by circle (1st: Anuhle, Oldrasek, Tomescu; 2nd boss: Audegar, Rimvidas, Stanewald, Makarios; 3rd legendary: **Sibri** E9); CRB (1st: Agata, Erymanthus, Neomah; 2nd boss: Alveua, Mara, Octavian, Sigereth).
Vault: Core › Demons and Demon Princes; Hundred Devils › Chapter Three › Demons.

### Notable named demons (pointers)
- **Sibri, the Rampart of Serpents** — Third Circle; a legendary capstone foe (E9). (HDNP)
- **Mara** — Second Circle seductress; classic tempter/corruptor. (CRB)
- **Octavian, the Living Tower (Quarter-Prince)** — Second Circle siege-monster. (CRB)
- **Neomah, the Maker of Flesh** / **Erymanthus, the Blood-Ape** / **Agata, the Beauteous Wasp** — common First Circle summons (flesh-crafter / war-ape / winged messenger). (CRB)
Vault: Core › Demons; Hundred Devils › Demons.

## The Dead (Underworld)

### Ghosts (overview) — Threat · lingering souls · tier minor→elite
What it is: souls that failed to reincarnate, chained by passion/dark magic/the boundary; from mindless **echoes** to scheming personalities. **Hungry ghosts** = the feral lower soul (po).
Behavior/MO: solid and active by **night**, fading by day; haunt deaths/atrocities; the ancestor cult both protects and provokes them; armies bring funerists to burn enemy dead.
Ban/weakness: **salt repels, living blood intoxicates**; sunlight destroys hungry ghosts and suppresses most; proper funerary rites lay them.
Lure/sign: shadowlands, gravesites, mires, unburned/unhonored dead; leeched colors and savage storms.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → HDNP › The Dead (Ankou, Haunt, Keregost, the Wreck of the Ruby Wren); AotR ghosts (Telamedes, Tepet Goran, Dorashenje, Jovena Oreia); CRB (hungry ghost, war ghost, nephwrack E5).
Vault: Core › The Restless Dead; Hundred Devils › Chapter Two › The Dead.

### Undead constructs & the Restless (pointers)
- **Zombies / Skeletons** — animated corpses + necro-machines; the Abyssals' & Deathlords' rank-and-file (battle-group). (CRB / HDNP)
- **Barrow Hound, Bloodworm, The Drowned, Scavenger, Rantai** — assorted hungry/animal dead (graveyard, drowned, scavenger menaces). (HDNP)
- **The Forlorn Manor / Haunt** — place-bound haunts (a building as a foe). — elite. (HDNP)
- **Juggernaut** — the city-sized behemoth-corpse Mask of Winters rides (behemoth lore below; Place lore at Thorns). 
Vault: Core › The Dead; Hundred Devils › The Dead.

## The Wyld & the Fair Folk

### Fair Folk / raksha (overview) — Threat/Bargain · soul-eating chaos-beings · tier minor→legendary
What it is: beautiful, soulless immortals born beyond the world; feed on Creation-born **souls and dreams** (heroes' best); wear **gossamer forms** and stolen Pole-substance; passions/ethics are masks.
Behavior/MO: raid the rim (Winter Folk in the North, isle-raksha in the West, jungle blowgun-fae in the SE); make binding pacts (Whitewall's treaty, Halta's floor-pact); rule bordermarches; tame behemoths; make lesser fae.
Ban/weakness: weaker in deep Creation (need a Pole's substance); **bound by their own bargains** (a lever); cold iron and salt feature in folklore wards (region-specific); the new **hannya** hunts them.
Lure/sign: impossible beauty, dream-grace, robes of woven poetry, sunset eyes; bordermarch shimmer.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → CRB (Fair Folk Cataphract, Lorelei, Silverwight, Buck-Ogre); HDNP Creatures of the Wyld (Kian-Ji, Mirrorkin, Siren Tower, etc.); AotR (Iolanthe, Lady Midnight, Fivefold Masks and Lies).
Vault: Core › The Fair Folk; Hundred Devils › Chapter Four › Creatures of the Wyld.

### Wyld beasts & mutants (pointers)
- **Cockatrice, Unicorn, Lava Moth, Singer of the Deep** — Wyld-touched animals (petrifying gaze, holy horn, fire-moth, deep-sea siren). (HDNP)
- **Manticore** — classic Wyld predator. (CRB)
- **Visage Blight, Parasitic Shadow, Haloed Husk, Napaxu** — gossamer horrors and soul-warpers. (HDNP)
- Wyld-touched mortals/skinchangers haunt the rim (the North's brine-giants; the Uskwood's intelligent wolves — Place lore in `directions/east.md`).
Vault: Hundred Devils › Creatures of the Wyld; Core › Creatures of the Wyld.

## Behemoths

### Behemoths (overview) — Threat/Landmark · discarded prototypes of life · tier boss→legendary
What it is: near-indestructible, often **fortress-sized** monsters; half-formed prototypes cast off by the Primordials (and into the Wyld by First Age Solars); incomprehensible motives. Loosely includes behemoth-ghosts, buried city-automata, and cannibal cast-out gods.
Behavior/MO: prowl/devour at a scale where humanity is often beneath notice; a few are man-sized but still hugely dangerous; the Wyld and fae dreamforges birth new ones (raksha tame most easily).
Ban/weakness: usually unbeatable head-on — **redirect, don't confront**; some have specific ritual vulnerabilities; raksha-tamed ones answer to their tamer.
Lure/sign: continent-scale destruction, a moving mountain, a city-sized corpse.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → HDNP (Gajam-Un the Living Manse, Mahicara the Volcanic Earthwalker, Thousand-Forged Dragon, Sky Titan, Grelidaka, Mata-Yadh); Lunars (Thunder Lizard).
Vault: Core › Behemoths; Hundred Devils › Strange Beasts / Animals.

### Notable behemoths & strange beasts (pointers)
- **Juggernaut** — city-sized corpse-behemoth; throne of the Deathlord Mask of Winters (Place lore at Thorns, `03_otherworlds.md`). — legendary; generate.
- **Gajam-Un, the Living Manse** — a manse that is also a beast. — elite. (HDNP)
- **Thousand-Forged Dragon** — a constructed behemoth-dragon. — elite. (HDNP)
- **Strange Beasts of the Hundred Devils** — Dream Hawk, Gravehound, Jungle Stalker, Moonsilver Shadow, Tiger's Eye, Twistroot & Mother Tree, etc.: enchanted/Essence-touched animals for any biome. — mostly standard. (HDNP › Strange Beasts)
Vault: Hundred Devils › Chapter One › Strange Beasts; Core › Behemoths.
