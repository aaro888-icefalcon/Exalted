## Silver Pact — Lunar elders (Shahan-yas)

### Ma-Ha-Suchi — NPC · Shahan-ya · Power: Legendary (First Age elder Lunar)
Tone: a beautiful goat-and-serpent-horned elder dripping contempt, ringed by his beastman children.
Surface: ancient, influential Pact elder; god-king of the **Nameless Lair** (SE jungle); sires armies of beastfolk; openly scorns the young and the Realm.
Wants: a **unified Lunar Realm** inheriting the Old Realm; vindication for old humiliations; loyal beastman legions.
Leverage: appeals to Lunar supremacy + shared grudge move him; he despises weakness and flattery; he negotiates, but on his terms.
Voice: courtly, cruel, never raises his voice.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → Ma-Ha-Suchi (legendary). Loyalties: his vision of the Pact; his beastfolk.
Truth (GM-only): his **feud with Raksi** (once his closest friend, who rejected his unified-Realm vision) nearly split the Pact; they don't speak. — reveal on: Pact strategy / a council.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; NPC:Raksi (estranged); Place:The Nameless Lair (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`).
Vault: Lunars › Notable Shahan-yas › Ma-Ha-Suchi; Lunars › Heroes and Monsters › Ma-Ha-Suchi.

### Raksi, Queen of Fangs — NPC · Shahan-ya · Power: Legendary (E7 sorcerer-elder)
Tone: genteel and bestial by turns — a cannibal banquet served with perfect manners.
Surface: the Pact's **greatest sorcerer** (holds the *Book of Three Circles*); devil-empress of **Mahalanka**, worshiped as a goddess.
Wants: replace the Realm with many independent Lunar **autarch-princes** (autarchy, not Ma-Ha-Suchi's single Realm); be left to her sorcery.
Leverage: she *performs* monstrosity to test petitioners — survive her court/banquet and earn her warm human side; she respects power and nerve.
Voice: cultured, purring, suddenly violent.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → Raksi (legendary, E7). Loyalties: her own power; her apefolk; (estranged) the Pact.
Truth (GM-only): a warm, human self exists beneath the cannibal-queen act — shown only to those who truly earn it. — reveal on: surviving her trial / sustained trust.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; NPC:Ma-Ha-Suchi (estranged); Place:Mahalanka.
Vault: Lunars › Notable Shahan-yas › Raksi; Lunars › Heroes and Monsters › Raksi.

### Leviathan — NPC · Shahan-ya · Power: Legendary (First Age elder; orca)
Tone: a vast, laconic sea-god of wounded pride, surfacing from a thousand years of grief.
Surface: First Age elder, once master of the seas; broke after failing to save his Solar mate in the Usurpation; rules **Sunken Luthe**, now the Pact's Western war-HQ.
Wants: grind the Realm down until the Pact can **invade the Blessed Isle** and besiege the Imperial City.
Leverage: shared grief over slain Solar mates moves him; he's bad at politics and impatient with the inexperienced.
Voice: slow, deep, few words.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → Leviathan-scale (generate, legendary). Loyalties: the Pact's Western war; the drowned city.
Truth (GM-only): his daughter **Blood-Dimmed Deep** secretly pushes for open war he refuses; he protects a DB fixer (Gevan Batahul) who's never heard of the Usurpation. — reveal on: a Luthe council.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; Place:Sunken Luthe (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`); NPC:Sha'a Oka (snubs him).
Vault: Lunars › Notable Shahan-yas › Leviathan.

### Sha'a Oka, the Black Lion — NPC · Shahan-ya · Power: Legendary (open-war commander)
Tone: indomitable charisma over a haunted distance — a war-king who keeps coming back from death.
Surface: supreme commander of all Pact forces on **the Caul**; the chief proponent of **open war**; "His Divine Lunar Presence." Returns from many deaths (lets the immortality rumor stand).
Wants: unify the Caul as a staging ground for a **direct, decisive assault on the Blessed Isle**.
Leverage: glory and a winnable war draw recruits; he respects boldness; he is estranged and performs obscure rites alone.
Voice: commanding, magnetic, oddly distant.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → generate (legendary lionfolk war-form). Loyalties: the Caul; the dream of striking the Isle.
Truth (GM-only): he alone returned from the Caul's centuries-long disappearance — the experience changed him; he snubs Leviathan and is opposed by the attritionists. — reveal on: the Caul war / his confidence.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; Place:The Caul (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`); NPC:Leviathan.
Vault: Lunars › Notable Shahan-yas › Sha'a Oka.

### Lilith — NPC · former Pact paragon · Power: Legendary (E6; legendary martial artist)
Tone: a quiet, wandering legend who walked away from both the Pact and her human life.
Surface: a prominent early-Pact member and **legendary martial artist**; left the Pact and her humanity behind; many still count her among the Pact's greats.
Wants: (GM's table) — her own path; old bonds; mastery. Leverage: appeals to honor and the lost First Age.
Voice: spare, weathered, unhurried.
Stat pointer: `../statblocks/00_index.md` → Lilith (legendary, E6). Loyalties: herself; (loosely) the Pact's legacy.
Truth (GM-only): her full agenda is open canvas — a wandering elder who can mentor, test, or judge the PC. — reveal on: earning her notice.
Links: Faction:Silver Pact; the Lunar Exalted (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: Lunars › Chapter Two (cross-ref); Lunars › Heroes and Monsters › Lilith.

## The Bull's Circle (Solars of the North)

### The Bull of the North (Yurgen Caul) — NPC · Solar warlord · Power: Legendary (Dawn Caste), now dying
Tone: a charismatic conqueror-king fading on a sickbed he will not leave.
Surface: Icewalker reborn as a Dawn Caste Solar ~10 yrs ago; built a Northern empire by charisma + military genius; **dying** of an infected wound from the Battle of Futile Blood (resists even peerless healing); bedridden in Plenilune.
Wants: to recover, crush his rebels, and resume conquest; (failing that) his empire's survival.
Leverage: appeals to the war against the Realm and to Icewalker kinship; the question of his succession moves everyone around him.
Voice: rare now — when he speaks, commanding even from the bed.
Stat pointer: generate (Legendary Dawn Caste; if he acts, a capstone). Loyalties: the war on the Realm; (neglected) the Icewalkers.
Truth (GM-only): two of his Circle (Nalla Bloodaxe, who once faked her death; Fear-Eater) only *apparently* died at Futile Blood. — reveal on: investigating Futile Blood.
Links: Place:The Bull of the North's Empire (`directions/north.md`); Faction:Silver Pact (the Pact debates him); Faction:Realm (Tepet's nemesis).
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › The Bull of the North's Empire.

### The Bull's Circle — NPC cluster · Solars · Power: Elite–Legendary
- **Samea** (Zenith sorceress, Blackwater Mammoth) — his mentor, consumed by curing him; loves the Icewalkers.
- **Raneth of Plenilune** (Twilight viceroy/inquisitor) — will let the empire burn to save Plenilune; low regard for Icewalkers and his Circlemates.
- **Crimson Antler** (Dawn hunter/diplomat, Birchbark Reindeer) — loyal to the Icewalkers first; **politically opposed to Raneth**; will leave rather than subordinate the tribes.
- **Nalla Bloodaxe** & **Fear-Eater** — apparently slain at Futile Blood (see the Bull's Truth).
Stat pointer: generate (Solar, Elite–Legendary). Truth (GM-only): at the Bull's death, **Raneth vs. Crimson Antler** split the empire. — reveal on: the succession crisis.
Links: NPC:The Bull of the North; Place:Plenilune (`directions/north.md`).
Vault: Across the Eight Directions › The Bull's Circle.

## The Realm

### The Scarlet Empress — NPC · founder-autarch · Power: Legendary (absent), the keystone
Tone: the most powerful Dragon-Blood alive — and a five-year absence that hollowed out an empire.
Surface: founded the Realm ~760 yrs ago by seizing the **Sword of Creation** (First Age superweapon) to destroy the Fair Folk Invasion + the Seven Tigers; ruled by pitting every faction against each other (the "Thousand Mazy Paths"); **vanished RY 763** (~5 yrs ago).
Wants: (unknown) — her return is the campaign's great wildcard.
Leverage: her name still commands legitimacy; the Sword sits dormant without her.
Voice: (if encountered) imperial, ancient, unreadable.
Stat pointer: generate (Legendary DB sorcerer-autarch; she has even learned Solar-only secrets). Loyalties: the Realm; her children.
Truth (GM-only): her vizier at the founding was **Chejop Kejak** (Bronze Faction); she secretly ran a vast heretical worship cult her whole reign. — reveal on: deep lore / the Imperial Manse.
Links: Faction:The Realm; Place:The Imperial City (`directions/blessed_isle.md`); NPC:Chejop Kejak.
Vault: The Realm › Chapter One (Birth of an Empire); Core › A Report on the Disappearance of the Scarlet Empress.

### Key Dynasts & the Regent — NPC cluster · the Realm · Power: Elite (DB) / political
- **Regent Tepet Fokuf** — the "witless regent" elected as a disposable canary; holds the Empress's veto but never dares use it; lets the Deliberative rule.
- **Mnemon** — the Empress's eldest *Dynastic* child; pious architect-house matriarch; a **leading throne contender** (Old Dragons lean to her); a mighty sorceress (~400 yrs old).
- **V'neef** — matriarch of the youngest Great House (~14 yrs old, ~60 herself); holds the Merchant Fleet; a key contender (Yellow Banners lean to her); created to check Peleps.
- **Cathak Cainan** — leads the premier military house (8 legions); a **kingmaker** courted by bribes.
- **Sesus Raenyah** — dark-horse throne candidate pushed by a coalition.
Stat pointer: generate (DB, Elite) or `../statblocks/00_index.md` (AotR Dragon-Blooded). Links: Faction:The Realm, Great Houses (`04_factions.md`).
Vault: The Realm › An Empty Throne / The Great Houses.

## The Deathlords

### Mask of Winters — NPC · Deathlord · Power: Legendary (undead sorcerer-king)
Tone: a patient, theatrical lord of the dead astride a city-sized corpse.
Surface: took the city of **Thorns** (RY 764) by siege atop the resurrected behemoth **Juggernaut**; raised his citadel on its rotting back; now the dominant Deathlord in the East.
Wants: expand — his gaze is now on the **River Province** (Lookshy, the free cities); ultimately, oblivion.
Leverage: a shared enemy (the Realm/Lookshy) makes him a momentary ally; his deathknights/dead serve him.
Voice: courtly, unhurried, faintly mocking.
Stat pointer: generate (Legendary Deathlord); his Abyssals → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (AotR Abyssals). Regent: blind deathknight **Red Iron Rebuke**.
Truth (GM-only): like all Deathlords, the ghost of a First Age Solar, sworn to the Neverborn. — reveal on: deep Underworld lore.
Links: Place:Thorns (`03_otherworlds.md`); Faction:Deathlords (`04_factions.md`); Faction:Lookshy (threatened).
Vault: Core › The Fall of Thorns; Core › The Deathlords.

### Other Deathlords (pointers) — NPC cluster · the Underworld · Power: Legendary
- **The Lover Clad in the Raiment of Tears** — claims the North's shadowlands; binds those who die with great passion as the cannibalistic **Loveless** (a Northern menace near the PC's home turf).
- **Bodhisattva Anointed by Dark Water / "the Silver Prince"** — eternal monarch of the **Skullstone Archipelago** (West); a canny trader-admiral who ensnares neighbors in debt then annexes them; his agents stir Azure's Siakal cult.
Stat pointer: generate (Legendary Deathlord). Links: Place:Skullstone, the North shadowlands (`03_otherworlds.md`, `directions/`); Faction:Deathlords.
Vault: Core › The Deathlords.

## The Bureau of Destiny

### Chejop Kejak — NPC · Bronze Faction leader (Sidereal) · Power: Legendary (fate-weaver), aging
Tone: a wise, unremarkable old monk whose hand is on every thread of the Realm's fate.
Surface: leader of the **Bronze Faction** of Sidereals; the Empress's founding vizier; runs the **Immaculate Order** from the shadows while posing as the Mouth of Peace's assistant; **will die of old age soon**.
Wants: hold the ancient prophecy — suppress Solars/Lunars, keep the Realm together, keep the Wyld Hunt effective; now scrambles to avert Realm civil war.
Leverage: he acts only through proxies and fate; near-impossible to reach directly; his coming death is a lever.
Voice: mild, patient, grandfatherly.
Stat pointer: generate (Legendary Sidereal) or `../statblocks/00_index.md` (AotR Sidereals). Loyalties: his prophecy; Creation's survival as he sees it.
Truth (GM-only): the Bronze Faction **architected the Usurpation** (murdered/exiled the Solars) and the centuries-long Wyld Hunt — the wound behind the Lunars' rage. — reveal on: deep lore / a Pact elder. The **Gold Faction** has now broken away to back the Solars.
Links: Faction:Bureau of Destiny, Immaculate Order (`04_factions.md`); NPC:The Scarlet Empress; Concept:The Usurpation (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: The Realm › The Bronze Faction; Core › The Sidereal Exalted (Bronze and Gold).

### The Mouth of Peace — NPC · public voice of the Immaculate Order · Power: political (mortal/DB)
Tone: the beloved, ageless face of the faith, quietly dreading the chaos her death will bring.
Surface: the Order's revered public voice, taught to be a reincarnation of the last; the **current Mouth is near natural death** and fears her death's interregnum will trigger civil war.
Wants: to handpick her own successor (bypassing the normal Sidereal-guided process) to avert disaster.
Leverage: enormous moral authority; she once struggled against Chejop Kejak's control.
Voice: serene, compassionate, weary.
Stat pointer: generate (influential mortal/DB; social, not combat). Loyalties: the faith; the Realm's peace.
Truth (GM-only): she is a piece on Kejak's board, half-aware of it. — reveal on: court/Order intrigue.
Links: Faction:Immaculate Order; NPC:Chejop Kejak; Faction:The Realm.
Vault: The Realm › The Mouth of Peace.
