# 03 — The Otherworlds (Wyld · Underworld · Yu-Shan · Malfeas)

The realms beyond Creation's shell. Each is both a place the PC can enter and a force that bleeds into Creation. Concept/Place hybrid cards. Bestiary creatures for each (raksha, the dead, demons) point to `06_bestiary_lore.md` + `../statblocks/00_index.md`.

---

### The Wyld — Place/Concept · beyond Creation's rim · Danger: high→annihilating (by depth)
Tone: a beautiful, impossible chaos where landscapes melt and the self comes apart.
Surface: raw primal chaos — "endless potential of the unformed" — beneath, within, and surrounding Creation; the world is a thin shell over it. It seeps in wherever minds/dreams are stripped away (reality is strongest where sentient life flourishes). Gradient by distance from the Pole of Earth: stable core → reality thins outward → **bordermarches** (the fae-marked edge) → **middlemarches** → **deep Wyld** (raw madness), ebbing/surging with the moon.
In play: the danger dial of the rim. Effects = **shaping/mutation** of flesh and mind; addictive euphoria/loss of self; impossible landscapes; **time runs unreliably** (a day in the Wyld can be 20 years in Creation). Wyld-touched people are feared as mutants.
GM rolls: shaping/mutation contests; Wyld-survival (Resolve/Integrity); the moon's phase shifts how far the Wyld reaches.
Limits: **only Lunars venture beyond the borders without elaborate preparation** — moonsilver tattoos armor a Lunar against the Wyld, making it the PC's refuge where the Realm cannot follow. Mortals and most Exalts mutate or dissolve.
Links: Concept:Lunar Exalted (`01_cosmology.md`); Faction:Fair Folk (`04_factions.md`); the Bronze Tide flees a Wyld empire (`directions/lunar_dominions.md`); Concept:Chimerism (`../lunar/`).
Vault: Core › The Wyld (The Face of the Wyld).

### The Fair Folk (raksha) — Faction/Concept · the Wyld & bordermarches · Danger: high (soul-predators)
Tone: beautiful, soulless things in robes of woven poetry, who love you and would eat your dreams.
Surface: chaos-beings born beyond the world — ravenous, beautiful, immortal, **soulless**; resemble humans but unmistakably not. They both **hate** Creation (its fixity oppresses them) and **love** it (it fills their emptiness). They **feed on Creation-born souls and dreams** (heroes' souls best — drawing them to the Exalted). They wear **gossamer forms** and steal substance from the nearest Elemental Pole; ethics/passions are masks they don. They make lesser fae (hobgoblins, reavers, howlers) and tame behemoths.
In play: the rim's signature menace; they raid (the North's "Winter Folk," the West's cannibal isle-raksha, the SE jungle blowgun-fae), make pacts (Whitewall's Nightwalkers' Treaty, Halta's floor-pact), and rule bordermarches. A Lunar may bargain with, fight, or be hunted by them.
GM rolls: raksha foes → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (Fair Folk; CRB Cataphract/Lorelei, HDNP Wyld creatures); soul/dream theft as a social-magic attack.
Limits: weaker in deep Creation (need a Pole's substance to persist); some Pact Lunars (controversially) ally with fae nobles (Klesamra Lotus-Seed; the Bronze Tide's enemy Bhadri).
Links: Place:The Wyld; faction card in `04_factions.md`; Place:Halta, Whitewall.
Vault: Core › The Fair Folk; bestiary lore in `06_bestiary_lore.md`.

### The Underworld — Place/Concept · the land of the dead (beside Creation) · Danger: high (worse at night)
Tone: a sunless mirror-world of black rivers and the envious dead, close beside the living.
Surface: a mirror-world of the dead — a confluence of void-oceans and **rivers of darkness** carrying souls/memories toward reincarnation or oblivion (**Lethe**); studded with islet-afterlives. Sunless (strange stars turned by the prayers of the dead). It sits **close beside Creation** in the Second Age. The ancient dead envy living blood and breath.
In play: reachable via **shadowlands** (below); the Lunar may enter for lore, allies, or to strike Deathlord power. Universal levers: **the dead find salt repellent and living blood intoxicating.**
GM rolls: dead foes → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (ghosts/undead); navigation as exploration; funerary rites matter (improperly burned dead rise as hungry ghosts).
Limits: perilous even for ghosts, far worse for the living; lies outside fate's weave.
Links: Concept:Shadowlands (below); Faction:Deathlords (`04_factions.md`); the Liminal Exalted (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: Core › The Grave and Beyond.

### Ghosts & the Restless Dead — Concept · everywhere the dead linger · Danger: minor→elite
Tone: the past refusing to rest — from a looping echo to a scheming dead lord.
Surface: reincarnation is imperfect; some souls linger, chained by passion, dark magic, or being caught on the boundary — from mindless **repetitive echoes** to full personalities with plans. **Hungry ghosts** = the lower soul (po) gone feral; they must **sleep in their corpse by day or be destroyed by sunlight**. Beyond the Realm, the **ancestor cult** flourishes (families worship their dead for protection; slighted ghosts turn vicious).
In play: the bread-and-butter Underworld foe + a constant Threshold presence (Clovina's ancestor-monsters, the North's mire-ghosts, Sijan's respected dead). Armies bring **funerists** to burn enemy dead.
GM rolls: ghost foes → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (HDNP › The Dead; AotR ghosts; CRB hungry ghost/nephwrack); salt/blood/sunlight as levers.
Limits: most ghosts fade in daylight (solid and active by night); hungry ghosts are sun-vulnerable.
Links: Place:The Underworld; Concept:Shadowlands; Place:Clovina, Sijan.
Vault: Core › The Restless Dead / The Ancestor Cult; bestiary in `06_bestiary_lore.md`.

### Shadowlands — Concept/Place · scars where Creation & the Underworld overlap · Danger: high (lethal at night)
Tone: a place of leeched colors and savage storms where the dead walk after dark.
Surface: places where **Creation and the Underworld overlap** — formed where too many died too fast or atrocity scarred the land. Marked by leeched/violent colors, savage storms, listless animals, sickly pale people, warped narrow buildings, bitter crops. **Border rule (key):** cross by **day** → into the land of the living; cross by **night** → into the Underworld. Ghosts are solid and active by night here; most fade at sunrise.
In play: the doorway between worlds; smuggling routes (Whitewall's Underworld-goods loophole runs through the Bruised Grove shadowland), Deathlord beachheads (Thorns), and hiding spots. A Lunar can use one as a back door into the Underworld.
GM rolls: night = Underworld rules + active dead; day = Creation; salt wards (Salt Wardens cage Chiaroscuro's Whispering Circles).
Limits: a shadowland is a *seam*, not the whole Underworld; sunlight still suppresses most dead.
Links: Place:The Underworld; Faction:Deathlords; Place:Thorns, Whitewall (Bruised Grove), Chiaroscuro, An-Teng (City of Dead Flowers).
Vault: Core › Shadowlands.

### Thorns — Place · East/River Province, fallen city · Danger: high (new shadowland)
Tone: a once-rich trade city living among the dead on the rotting back of a city-sized corpse.
Surface: formerly the Realm's largest Scavenger-Lands outpost; **conquered RY 764 by the Deathlord Mask of Winters**, who rode the resurrected First Age behemoth **Juggernaut** (city-sized corpse) and raised his citadel on its back. He chose **siege over assault** (rats ate the food, plague tainted the air, cursed pigs spawned skeletons) until the starving people opened their gates and begged him in. Now a fast-growing **shadowland** and the capital of his nascent empire (~10,000 living among the dead).
Rule: Mask of Winters; regent = the blind deathknight **Red Iron Rebuke** (poses as the last Autocrat's lost elder brother).
Truth (GM-only):
  - Mask of Winters' gaze is now on the **River Province** (Lookshy, the free cities) — Thorns is a launch-point, not an endpoint. — reveal on: scouting his forces / a Pact intel tip.
Clocks: Mask's Expansion [2/8] — ticks toward a move on the River Province.
Hooks: refugees from Thorns carry rumors and grudges; a deathknight emissary ranges out (cf. Whitewall's summit); the Juggernaut itself is a behemoth-scale threat/landmark.
Handles: deathknights → generate (Abyssal, elite/boss) or `../statblocks/00_index.md` (AotR Abyssals); the dead → `../statblocks/00_index.md`; Juggernaut → behemoth, legendary.
Links: Faction:Deathlords (Mask of Winters); Concept:Shadowlands; Faction:Lookshy (threatened); the Abyssal Exalted (`01_cosmology.md`).
Vault: Core › The Fall of Thorns; Core › The Threshold › The River Province.

### Yu-Shan (Heaven) — Place/Concept · beyond the world, in a quicksilver sea · Danger: variable (gods' city)
Tone: a continent-sized city of bureaucracy, divine slums, and gods playing dice with destiny.
Surface: the great celestial island — **a city the size of a continent**, floating beyond the world in an endless sea of quicksilver; home to ~ten million gods; once the home of the world's creators. Center = the **Jade Pleasure Dome**, where the **Incarnae** (the seven greatest gods) play the **Games of Divinity**. The rest is a vast **bureaucracy** (scribes file reports, committees schedule mortal destinies, **dragon censors** police the law — or take bribes). Houses the **Bureau of Destiny** + the **Loom of Fate**.
In play: the source of celestial law (gods can't intercede in Creation directly), the Sidereals' home base, and the engine of fate. In the Second Age, **millions of gods lost their posts and live in divine slums** (unemployed, scheming, criminal — potential allies/rivals).
GM rolls: divine bureaucracy as a social/intrigue arena; censors → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (elemental dragons); fate via the oracle.
Limits: hard for the un-divine to reach/move in; the Incarnae are absorbed in the Games and rarely act.
Links: Concept:The Spirit World, Fate (`01_cosmology.md`); Faction:Bureau of Destiny (`04_factions.md`); the Sidereal Exalted.
Vault: Core › Heaven; Core › Gods (the Incarnae).

### Malfeas (the Demon City / Hell) — Place/Concept · outside Creation · Danger: extreme (the Yozis' prison)
Tone: an endless city of vitriol and brass under a mad green sun, ringed by an infinite silver desert.
Surface: the **Demon City** — an endless city of brass lit by a **mad green sun**, bordered by an **infinite silver desert**: the prison **outside Creation** where the **Yozis** (the overthrown creator-Primordials) rage in inviolate imprisonment. **Demons** are the souls and children of the Yozis, enslaved eternally by the oaths the Yozis swore. **Circles:** **First** (simple servitors) · **Second/Third** (mightier, expressing the Yozis' urges, alien beyond human understanding).
In play: the wellspring of summonable demons (the Yozis' oaths make them bindable by **sorcery** — they build manses, guard, serve); demon-cults (the **Lintha** worship Kimbery; Yozi cults at Lathe). Demons spread inauspicious omens (a hazard to bystanders).
GM rolls: demon foes → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (HDNP/CRB demons by circle; Sibri E9 is the capstone); summoning per the sorcery rules.
Limits: the Yozis themselves cannot leave; demons enter Creation chiefly via summoning (or torn rifts); they crave worship and escape.
Links: Concept:The Spirit World, sorcery (`01_cosmology.md`, `../rules/05_sorcery_and_workings.md`); Faction:Malfeas/demons (`04_factions.md`); Place:The Lintha.
Vault: Core › Demons and Demon Princes; bestiary in `06_bestiary_lore.md`.

### Behemoths — Concept · scattered (Wyld, ruins, deep places) · Danger: boss→legendary
Tone: fortress-sized monsters that make no sense, built and discarded by the world's makers.
Surface: near-indestructible, often **fortress-sized** immortal monsters — half-formed prototypes of life created and discarded by the Primordials, frequently cast into the Wyld by First Age Solars. Motives are incomprehensible (living mountains, thousand-headed worms, ship-eating piscine titans). The term loosely covers ghosts of slain behemoths, buried city-sized automata, and cannibal gods cast from Heaven. A few are man-sized but still hugely dangerous.
In play: a scale-breaking landmark/threat (Mask of Winters' **Juggernaut**; HDNP/Lunars behemoth statblocks). The clever **redirect** rather than fight head-on; many operate at a scale where humanity is beneath notice.
GM rolls: behemoth foes → `../statblocks/00_index.md` (Gajam-Un, Mahicara, Thousand-Forged Dragon, Sky Titan, Thunder Lizard, Grelidaka, Mata-Yadh); often a hazard/objective, not a brawl.
Limits: the Wyld and fae dreamforges birth new ones; raksha tame most easily (animal-or-less intelligence).
Links: Place:The Wyld; Place:Thorns (Juggernaut); bestiary in `06_bestiary_lore.md`.
Vault: Core › Behemoths.
