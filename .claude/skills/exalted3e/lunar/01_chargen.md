# Solo-Lunar Character Creation — GM Walkthrough

LLM reference. RAW Exalted 3e (*Fangs at the Gate*, Ch4; cross-ref Core). Citations: `Book › Heading`.
GM runs these steps with the player, one at a time, producing a filled sheet. Defaults below = a Lunar Exalted **under a year** (standard solo start). For an experienced Lunar, see "Grizzled Veterans" at end.
See also: `lunar/03_traits.md` (castes/anima/Curse), `lunar/02_shapeshifting.md` (spirit shape/Tell/forms), `charms/`.

---

## Step 1 — Concept & Caste `Fangs › Step 1: Concept and Caste`
- Talk out **concept**: origin, personality, skills, heroic archetype. Note Silver Pact membership and any shahan-ya. One-line summary.
- **Pick Caste** (the moon's phases) — or begin **Casteless**:
  - **Full Moon** (Str/Dex/Sta) — warriors, survivors, warlords.
  - **Changing Moon** (Cha/Man/App) — tricksters, seducers, spies, leaders.
  - **No Moon** (Per/Int/Wits) — witches, savants, shamans, crafters.
  - **Casteless** — no Caste Attributes; better at unfavored Attributes; can use any Caste's anima power (cycling). Full caste detail + anima powers: `lunar/03_traits.md`.
- Note the Caste's anima powers now (player should know them before spending).

## Step 2 — Spirit Shape & Tell `Fangs › Step 2: Spirit Shape and Tell`
- Choose **spirit shape** (an animal embodying her truest self — meaningful, often matching the Caste's themes). It's an innate form.
- Choose a **Tell**: a distinctive marker present in every form she takes (animal feature, scar, scent, voice, eerie shadow…). Detection rules: `lunar/02_shapeshifting.md › The Tell`.

## Step 3 — Attributes `Fangs › Step 3: Attributes` / `STEP 3: ATTRIBUTES`
- Select **2 Caste Attributes** from the Caste's list (none if Casteless).
- Select **2 Favored Attributes** (not the same as Caste). Casteless: 2 Favored, no Caste.
- Every Attribute starts at **1 dot**. Then rank the three categories — **Physical / Social / Mental** — as primary/secondary/tertiary and distribute on top of the base dots:
  - **Primary: 9 dots · Secondary: 7 dots · Tertiary: 5 dots** (none above 5).
- **Placement rule:** at least one Caste-or-Favored Attribute must fall in the **primary** category, and at least one in the **secondary** category. (Casteless: only one Favored need be primary; none required in secondary.)

## Step 4 — Abilities `Fangs › Step 4: Abilities` / `STEP 4: ABILITIES`
- Divide **28 dots** among Abilities. Each starts at 0; **none above 3 without bonus points** (and never above 5).
- Assign **4 specialties** (need ≥1 dot in the Ability). Lunars do **not** pick "Caste/Favored Abilities" — favoring is by Attribute (Charms key off Attributes; see `lunar/03_traits.md`).

## Step 5 — Merits `Fangs › Step 5: Merits` / `STEP 5: MERITS`
- Choose **10 dots of Merits**. Lunar-specific shapeshifting Merits (optional, set starting forms):
  - **Heart's Blood (•–•••, Story):** packages of starting **animal** forms (more/stronger per dot). `Fangs › New Merits`
  - **Stolen Faces (•–•••••, Story):** starting **human** shapes — 5 commoners *or* 1 high-status human per dot.
  - Common: **Mentors** (shahan-ya, usually •••), **Retainers** (Moon-Touched, ••). `Fangs › EXISTING MERITS`
- Without these, she still starts with her own human shape + spirit shape; gain others via sacred hunts (`lunar/02_shapeshifting.md`).

## Step 6 — Charms `Fangs › Step 6: Charms` / `STEP 6: CHARMS`
- Choose **15 Charms**. Most require a minimum rating in their **Attribute** tree — if she doesn't qualify, raise that Attribute with bonus points. `lunar/03_traits.md › nine Attributes`.
- May swap in **Martial Arts Charms** or **Evocations** for Lunar Charms. Taking **Terrestrial Circle Sorcery** as a starting Charm also lets her learn spells in place of Charms.
- **Excellencies are not bought as Charms** — they're unlocked automatically (Caste/Favored at 3+, or any Attribute with ≥1 Charm; otherwise rating 5 or 2 Charms). `Fangs › Excellencies`. Full Excellency rules: `lunar/03_traits.md`.
- Pick from `charms/` (note **Hybrid Body Transformation** is the gateway to the war form — see `lunar/02_shapeshifting.md`).

## Step 7 — Intimacies & Limit Trigger `Fangs › Step 7: Intimacies and Limit Trigger`
- Choose **≥4 Intimacies**: at least **one Defining**, **one Major**, **one positive Tie**, **one negative Tie** (overlaps allowed). No maximum. Intensities: Minor / Major / Defining (valued 2/3/4 for Charms). `Core › Intimacies`
- Many Lunars take Intimacies of rage and Ties to Luna, the Silver Pact, their shahan-ya, and their dominion.
- Choose a **Limit trigger** — a stressful/frustrating condition (usually rage-related) that feeds the Great Curse. Sample list + how Limit/Limit Break/Monstrous Urges work: `lunar/03_traits.md › The Great Curse`.

## Step 8 — Bonus Points `Fangs › Step 8: Bonus Points` / `BONUS POINT COSTS`
- Spend **15 BP** any time during chargen. Costs:

| Trait | BP cost |
|---|---|
| Caste/Favored Attribute | 3 / dot |
| Non-Caste/non-Favored Attribute | 4 / dot |
| Ability | 2 / dot |
| Specialty | 1 |
| Merit | 1 / dot |
| Caste/Favored Charm | 4 |
| Non-Caste/non-Favored Charm | 5 (4 if Casteless) |
| Spell (Int Caste/Favored) | 4 |
| Spell (Int non-Caste/Favored) | 5 |
| Evocation | 4 |
| Animal latent ability | 3 |
| Willpower | 2 / dot |
- **Most efficient:** Caste/Favored Attributes, and raising Abilities above 3. **Least efficient:** Charms/Evocations/spells. `Fangs › Step 8`

## Step 9 — Finishing Touches `Fangs › Step 9: Finishing Touches` / `STEP 9: FINISHING TOUCHES`
- **Essence 1.**
- **Personal motes = 15 + Essence = 16.** **Peripheral motes = 34 + (Essence × 4) = 38.** `Fangs › Step 9`
- **Willpower 5** (raisable with BP at 2/dot).
- **Health levels:** −0, −1, −1, −2, −2, −4, Incapacitated (7 total; more via Ox-Body Technique). `Fangs › GRIZZLED VETERANS`
- **Join Battle = (Wits + Awareness)** — record it (in play: successes + 3 = starting Initiative). `Core › combat`
- **Anima:** starts at **Dim** (invisible). Note Caste Mark shape + anima powers. `lunar/03_traits.md › Anima Banner`
- Confirm **spirit shape + starting forms** and **Tell** are recorded. `lunar/02_shapeshifting.md`

## Grizzled Veterans (experienced-Lunar variant) `Fangs › GRIZZLED VETERANS`
Use instead of the defaults above: **Essence 2** · **13 Merit dots** · **20 Charms** · **18 BP**.
Recompute motes from Essence 2: Personal = 17, Peripheral = 42. Health, Willpower, Intimacy minimums, Attribute/Ability allocations unchanged.

## Character Sheet — Fields to Fill
- **Name · Concept · Caste** (or Casteless) **· Silver Pact / shahan-ya**
- **Spirit shape · Tell · starting forms** (from Heart's Blood / Stolen Faces)
- **Caste Attributes (2) · Favored Attributes (2)**
- **Attributes** (9, with primary/secondary/tertiary noted)
- **Abilities** (with dots) **· Specialties (4)**
- **Merits** (10 dots)
- **Charms (15)** **· unlocked Excellencies** **· Sorcery/spells if any**
- **Intimacies** (≥4: ≥1 Defining, ≥1 Major, ≥1 +Tie, ≥1 −Tie) **· Limit trigger · Limit = 0**
- **Essence 1 · Personal 16 · Peripheral 38 · Willpower 5**
- **Health: −0 / −1×2 / −2×2 / −4 / Incap · Join Battle (Wits+Awareness) · Initiative**
- **Anima level: Dim · Caste Mark shape · Caste anima powers · Solar Bond (yes/no/ST)**
