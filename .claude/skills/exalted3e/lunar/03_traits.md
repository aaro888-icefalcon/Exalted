## How Lunar Charms Are Organized: the NINE Attributes
Lunar Charms are grouped by **Attribute** (not Ability, unlike Solars). The nine trees:
**Strength, Dexterity, Stamina · Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance · Perception, Intelligence, Wits.**
Most Charms require a minimum rating in their tree's Attribute. `Fangs › Chapter Six: Charms` (tree headings).
- **Universal** Charms (e.g. Hybrid Body Transformation) count as Favored for all Lunars. `Fangs › Universal`
- **Protean** keyword: Charm gains power while shapeshifted into an animal/human form (true human shape doesn't count). `Fangs › New Keywords`
- **Archetype** keyword: a Lunar with a fitting spirit shape may learn the Charm under a *different* Attribute (alt minimums/prereqs, possible Caste/Favored discount). `Fangs › New Keywords`

### Excellencies (the baseline Charm)
- Add dice to an (Attribute + Ability) roll: **1m / die**. Raise a static value (Evasion/Parry/Resolve/Guile): **2m / +1**. `Fangs › Excellencies`
- Cap: +(Attribute) dice, or +(Attribute/2 rd down) to a static value; auto-successes count double vs. the cap.
- **Stunt to raise the cap:** describe a second, fitting Attribute → cap becomes (action Attribute + second Attribute). ST approves the stunt. `Fangs › Excellencies`
- **Strength Excellency** also adds Str-withering damage dice (before soak); **Stamina Excellency** also adds soak. 1m each, max (Attribute/2 rd down); declare before rolling. `Fangs › Excellencies`
- **Unlocking:** auto for any Caste/Favored Attribute at 3+ **or** for which you know ≥1 Charm. Any other Attribute: unlock by rating 5 **or** knowing 2 Charms. `Fangs › Excellencies`

## The Five Phases / Three Castes (+ Casteless)
Lunars dissolved the original five castes; modern Lunars are **three Castes** (the moon's phases) plus **Casteless** (every phase / the wanderer). Caste is fixed by moonsilver tattoos; all Lunars begin Casteless at Exaltation. `Fangs › Chapter One: The Lunar Exalted`; `Fangs › CASTES`

| Caste | Phase / archetype | Caste Attributes | Assoc. | Derogatory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Full Moon** | Warriors, athletes, survivors, warlords | Strength, Dexterity, Stamina | summer / fire / white / Maiden of Battles | the Frenzied |
| **Changing Moon** | Tricksters, thieves, seducers, leaders | Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance | spring / air / purple / Maiden of Serenity | the Face-Stealers |
| **No Moon** | Witches, savants, healers, shamans, crafters | Perception, Intelligence, Wits | winter / water / blue / Maiden of Secrets | the Ogres |
| **Casteless** | Undecided / all phases | None (better at unfavored Attributes) | autumn / wood / gray / Maiden of Journeys | the Moon-Mad |
`Fangs › Full Moon Caste`, `Changing Moon Caste`, `No Moon Caste`, `Casteless`

Caste Mark shapes: Full Moon = filled silver disk; Changing Moon = silver crescent; No Moon = empty silver circle; Casteless = shifts with the moon's phase. `Fangs › (each caste's Anima Banner)`

## Anima Banner — Levels & Effects
Spend Peripheral Essence to raise the banner: **every 5 Peripheral motes spent in one instant raises it one level.** `Fangs › Anima Banner`

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Dim** | Invisible (default when not spending Essence). |
| **Glowing** | Outlines body; Caste Mark appears (shines through cover); **Tell & tattoos obvious**; −3 to her Stealth/disguise. |
| **Burning** | Argent aura; objects it touches warp/dampen; Caste Mark subsumed (returns at dramatic moments); **Stealth impossible**. |
| **Bonfire / Iconic** | Pillar of light visible for miles; iconic display (spirit shape, mirrors, blades, etc.); lights short range; **Stealth impossible**; unlocks "free at bonfire" anima powers. |
Recede: 15 min bonfire→burning or burning→glowing; 30 min glowing→dim. `Fangs › ANIMA LEVEL / EFFECTS`

**1-mote anima tricks (any Lunar):** show/hide Caste Mark or Tell at will; discern moon phase & exact time. `Fangs › Anima Effects`

### Caste Anima Powers `Fangs › Anima Effects`
- **Full Moon** — 5m: +(Essence, max 5) non-Charm dice on movement & feats of strength, +(Essence or 3) natural soak until next turn (**free at bonfire**). Always: +(highest Physical Attr /2 rd up) Resolve vs. fear. Once/day on a decisive attack that resets Initiative: 10m+1wp to roll Join Battle adding +(highest Physical Attr) dice.
- **Changing Moon** — 5m: +(Essence/2 rd up) dice on an influence roll; audience must hear her out (leaving early costs 1wp) — cost is **Mute, waived at bonfire**. At dim: +1 Guile, +(Essence/2 rd up) Stealth/disguise. Once/day: 3m+1wp to ignore targets' negative Ties toward her on an influence roll.
- **No Moon** — 5m: shadowy penumbra, −2 to vision-based rolls against her (incl. most attacks) until next turn (**free at bonfire**). 3m: sense a nearby place of power + (highest Mental Attr) successes navigating to it. Once/day at bonfire: expend anima fully to gain 1wp, **or** +(Essence + highest Mental Attr) sorcerous motes to a spell, **or** a free full Excellency on a Mental roll (not Join Battle).
- **Casteless** — at dim, shifting into **human** shapes costs 2m less. May once/day use **any** Caste's once-per-day power, but must cycle through all three before reusing one (or session resets). `Fangs › Anima Effects (Casteless)`

## The Great Curse — Lunar Limit
Tracked by **Limit (0–10)**; pure roleplaying until 10, then **Limit Break → a Monstrous Urge**. `Fangs › The Great Curse`

**Gaining Limit (each once per scene unless noted):**
- Act against a **Major** Intimacy → roll **1 die**, gain Limit = successes.
- Act against a **Defining** Intimacy → roll **2 dice** (1 die if already rolled vs. Major this scene).
- **Limit trigger** met → roll **3 dice**.
(Weakening an Intimacy, or spending WP to resist influence it supports, counts as acting against it.) `Fangs › The Great Curse`

**Losing Limit:** −1 on accomplishing a legendary social goal; resets to 0 at a Limit Break's end. `Fangs › The Great Curse`

### Limit Triggers (Lunar — usually rage-flavored) `Fangs › Limit Triggers`
Pick ONE at chargen. Samples: destroys/sacrifices something valuable to her · is insulted or deliberately frustrated · a near-certain goal hits a big obstacle · gives advice and is ignored · others' immorality hinders her · someone she wants to notice her rejects/humiliates/ignores her · forced to endure deprivation/hardship by an adversary or for a goal.

### Limit Break → Monstrous Urges `Fangs › Monstrous Urges`
At Limit 10 the ST imposes a **Monstrous Urge** (now or at a dramatic moment). All Urges: count as a temporary **Defining Intimacy**; if it lets her treat influence as unacceptable she **must**; last **one scene or one session** (may spill over), ending early if a stated condition is met; on end, **Willpower resets to permanent rating and Limit resets to 0.** Samples:
- **Avaricious Hoard-Beast Craving** — must obsessively amass a chosen object, violently defend/retrieve it. (Session; ends if she harms a positive-Tie target over it.)
- **Bellowing Tyrant Proclamation** — all must acknowledge her superiority; forces submission. (Session; ends on a significant defeat from trying.)
- **Enraged Behemoth Rampage** — attacks any physical barrier/unpleasant stimulus until it stops (can intimidate rather than harm positive-Tie targets).
- **Laughing Devil Spirit** — trickster-mentor sowing chaos to shatter others' false security.

## Lunar Trait Notes
- **Attribute-centric:** Lunars are defined by Attributes (their Charms key off them); Castes are Attribute-themed (Physical / Social / Mental). `Fangs › CASTES`
- **Essence Fever:** all Lunars' Essence-fever pushes toward **rage / acting on anger** (it doesn't create rage, only impels existing anger). `Fangs › Essence Fever`
- **Health levels (all PCs):** −0, −1, −1, −2, −2, −4, Incapacitated (7 total); add more via Ox-Body Technique. `Fangs › GRIZZLED VETERANS`
- **Join Battle** = (Wits + Awareness) successes **+ 3** = starting Initiative. `Core › (combat)`
- **Willpower** starts equal to permanent rating; refills at story start, on Limit Break, and on goal/stunt awards. `Core › (Willpower)`
- **The Solar Bond:** many Lunars share a reincarnating bond with a Solar (sometimes now an Abyssal/Infernal); decided at chargen or left to the ST. Mechanics live with shapeshifting/relationships, not caste. `Fangs › The Solar Bond`
