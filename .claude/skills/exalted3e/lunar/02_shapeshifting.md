# Lunar Shapeshifting Subsystem

LLM reference + GM adjudication. RAW Exalted 3e (*Fangs at the Gate*). Citations: `Book › Heading`.
See also: `lunar/03_traits.md` (anima, castes), `lunar/01_chargen.md`, `charms/`.

## The Spirit Shape
- Emerges at Exaltation: an **animal form that is as much her true self as her human form**. The Lunar does **not** choose it — it always holds deep personal/cultural meaning. `Fangs › The Spirit Shape`
- Her two innate forms at start: **own true human shape + spirit shape**, plus any forms from the Heart's Blood / Stolen Faces Merits. `Fangs › Sacred Hunts`
- The spirit shape is the one form she **cannot** turn into a beast-spirit (no greater embodiment exists). `Many-Faced Strangers › Heart's Blood` (beast-spirit Charm).

## Basic Shapeshifting Action
- Enter a known animal/human shape: **miscellaneous action**, commit **4m** for the duration. Can flurry (not with attack actions). Revert to true human form reflexively by releasing the commitment. `Fangs › Animal Shapes`
- Some Charms refine this (cheaper, reflexive, indefinite, etc.). `Fangs › Animal Shapes`
- **Discounts:** Casteless at dim → human shifts cost 2m less; any Lunar at bonfire → animal shifts cost 2m less (not Towering Beast Form / Emerald Grasshopper Form). `Fangs › Anima Effects`, `Animal Shapes`

## Heart's Blood: Claiming New Forms (the Sacred Hunt)
To gain a form not already known, perform a **sacred hunt**: `Fangs › Sacred Hunts`
1. **Declare intent** to take a specific quarry's shape (cannot be declared mid-combat; no minimum hunt time).
2. **Hunt and kill the prey yourself** — directly, or via your traps/poison. Others may assist, but the Lunar must land the kill.
3. **Drink its heart's blood** (blood from its heart, or nearest analogue) → she claims the form.
- Claiming a **human** shape: she must form a **Tie** toward him if she lacks one (need not keep it to retain the form, but it's never casual). `Fangs › Sacred Hunts`
- **ST may skip combat / use downtime** for prey that offers no meaningful resistance. `Fangs › Sacred Hunts`
- **Hunters Hunted:** kill a fellow Lunar (or other human/animal shapeshifter) → claim **all** their human & animal shapes. Take their shape **without** killing (via Charm) → only their current shape. `Fangs › HUNTERS HUNTED`
- Some Charms grant **alternative sacred hunts** (e.g. take a lover's shape via an act of physical intimacy instead of killing; claim ghosts' shapes), all still requiring a declaration. `Fangs › Heart's Blood` (Ch6).

**Starting forms via Merits** (chargen): **Heart's Blood (•–•••)** = packages of animal forms; **Stolen Faces (•–•••••)** = human shapes (5 commoners *or* 1 high-status human per dot). `Fangs › New Merits`

## Using Animal Forms — What Changes vs. Carries Over
`Fangs › Animal Shapes`
**Carries over (unchanged):** Essence, Willpower, base Initiative, health levels, Attributes, Abilities, specialties, Resolve, Guile.
**Changes / uses the animal's:**
- **Natural weapons** (animal's attack pool + damage; same pool for withering & decisive). Dice above her Brawl pool = Charm dice; if her Brawl pool is higher she uses it and gains +1 withering Accuracy.
- **Evasion, Parry, natural soak, Hardness** — may use the animal's; any amount over her own base = Charm bonus (doesn't count against Stamina-Excellency soak cap).
- **Feats of strength** the animal can do (capped at the lower of her/its Strength; assume Str 1 if unlisted).
- **Innate special abilities & Merits** of the animal (added dice/successes = Charm bonus).
- **Latent abilities:** unlock mundane (not magical) ones for 6 xp / 3 BP each; once unlocked for one form, unlocked for all capable forms. (distract/disarm/unhorse gambits need no unlock.)
- **Communication** limited by anatomy — most animals can't speak; use body language.
- She **loses intrinsic mutations** (keeps magic-granted ones and Tell-linked ones).
- Once/day may use **any one Caste's** once-per-day anima power (cycle all three before reuse).
- **Legendary Size** animals need Towering Beast Form; **Minuscule** need Emerald Grasshopper Form (can still *claim*/choose as spirit shape without them, just can't transform).

### Human Forms `Fangs › Human Shapes`
- Indistinguishable from the person **unless her Tell is spotted** or anti-disguise magic (e.g. Eye of the Unconquered Sun) is used.
- **Traits don't change**: a strongman's body still runs on *her* Strength; a beauty's face is wasted without *her* Appearance. No prey's knowledge, memory, or magic.
- Loses intrinsic mutations except Tell-linked ones.

### Mutant Forms (beastfolk, Wyld mutants) `Fangs › MUTANT FORMS`
- Gains the form's Supernatural mutations but must **commit extra motes = total dot value of those Supernatural mutations** (not applied to mutations added after she claimed the form).

## War Form — Deadly Beastman Transformation
Two-step combat transformation. `Fangs › Hybrid Body Transformation`, `Deadly Beastman Transformation`
1. **Hybrid Body Transformation** (Universal; 4m; Simple; Indefinite, Essence 1): blends human + spirit shape, granting **6 dots of spirit-shape mutations** (chosen at purchase). Not an "animal form" for Protean. Repurchase (Essence) times for +4 dots each. Prereq for the war form.
2. **Deadly Beastman Transformation** (1m, 1wp; Simple; one scene; Strength 3, Essence 1; Withering-only): HBT must be active (may activate DBT reflexively when activating HBT). Grants:
   - +(Strength) raw withering damage; +(Strength) dice on feats of strength & move actions; +(Strength) natural soak; treats Strength as +(Essence) higher for *attempting* feats.
   - **Drawbacks:** −1 Defense (cannot be negated); loses artifact resonance / becomes dissonant.
- **Upgrades:** Terrifying Ogre Alteration (+4 spirit-shape mutation dots while in war form, Str 5); Claws of the Silver Moon (Essence claws/artifact weapon traits, repurchase to add Evocations); many DBT-keyed Charms reflexively fire when she enters war form. `Fangs › Terrifying Ogre Alteration / Claws of the Silver Moon`
- Higher-Essence Charms let DBT trigger from animal shapes, heal in war form, boost Initiative Breaks, add a Martial Arts Form, etc. `Fangs › (Strength/Stamina trees)`, `Many-Faced Strangers › Heart's Blood`

## The Tell (the supernatural marker in EVERY form)
- A **distinctive mark present in any shape** she takes: an animal feature, scar, scent, voice, or subtle supernatural display (e.g. an independent shadow). If inapplicable to a form, it shifts to a fitting equivalent but stays identifiable. `Fangs › The Tell`
- **Spotting it:** nontrivial characters roll **difficulty 7 (Perception + Awareness)**. +3 dice if they've detected *this Lunar's* Tell before. Anti-disguise magic applies. `Fangs › The Tell`
  - Success = notice the Tell (or, if seen before in another form, ID her as the same individual). **Failure = can't reroll while she stays in that shape**, unless she acts out of character (then a fresh roll with +1 to +3 dice; bigger discrepancy / read-intentions corroboration = bigger bonus).
- **Never fully impossible to detect** unless she knows **Subtle Silver Declaration**. At **glowing anima the Tell becomes obvious** to all. `Fangs › The Tell`; `lunar/03_traits.md › Anima Banner`
- A physical-feature Tell may be taken as a **mutation**: it's noticed normally, but linking it across her forms still needs the roll above. `Fangs › The Tell`

## Knacks / Gifts
- **No separate "Knack" subsystem in 3e** (that was 2e). Lunar shapeshifting upgrades are ordinary **Charms** in the nine Attribute trees — Protean/Archetype keywords, mutation Charms (Hybrid Body Transformation, Beast-Form Empowerment, Chimera-Soul Expression), and war-form Charms. `Fangs › New Keywords`, `Chapter Six: Charms`
- *Many-Faced Strangers* adds **Gift**-named Charms (e.g. Otherworldly Wisdom Gift) — still standard Attribute Charms, not a distinct mechanic. `Many-Faced Strangers › Otherworldly Wisdom Gift`

## Chimerism — RAW Correction + GM Adjudication
**Important:** In Ex3 Lunars, **chimerism is NOT a corruption/degeneration mechanic.** The 1e/2e "Lunar warps into an insane Chimera" rule does **not exist** in *Fangs at the Gate*. `Fangs › Chapter Five/Six` (no such rule present).
- "Chimera" here is **benign/aesthetic**: **Chimera-Soul Expression** (Charm) permanently fuses a second animal into the spirit shape (6 mutation dots; both count as spirit shape for Archetype Charms). `Fangs › Chimera-Soul Expression`
- The **corruption/loss-of-self pressure is the Great Curse**, not chimerism: rising **Limit → Limit Break → Monstrous Urge** (temporary Defining Intimacy of monstrous behavior). Adjudicate that via `lunar/03_traits.md › The Great Curse`.
- **GM notes:** if the player invokes "chimera" flavor, treat it as cosmetic/Charm-driven monstrousness, not a stat penalty. Drive the "monster vs. hero" tension through Limit, Intimacies, and Tell-exposure consequences — never an unrequested transformation into an uncontrollable creature.

## GM Adjudication Quick Notes `Fangs › Shapeshifting Complications`, `WHAT ARE ANIMALS?`, `SHAPESHIFTING AND THE ORICHALCUM RULE`
- **Animals only** — no spirits/magical beasts. If unlisted, ST decides; **intrinsic supernatural powers disqualify** a creature. Rule of thumb: could it exist (or have existed) in the real world? Then it's an animal.
- **Equipment:** items unusable in the new form vanish **Elsewhere** and return on a compatible shape. Moonsilver armor reshapes to fit any form (if she wishes); most other artifacts banish.
- **Martial Arts incompatible with animal forms** (can't attack/parry/use MA Charms; active MA Charms end on entering an animal shape).
- **No healing on shift:** damage, crippling, disease, poison, shaping effects persist across forms.
- **Space:** need room to transform; if confined, flurry shifting with a feat of demolition or you can't shift.
- **Guile/disguise:** read intentions/profile may flag out-of-character behavior without piercing the disguise; witnesses may attribute it to trauma/secrets rather than "shapeshifter." `Fangs › SHAPESHIFTING AND GUILE`
- **Orichalcum Rule:** ST may freely alter/ignore any animal+Charm combo that's unfun or overpowered. `Fangs › SHAPESHIFTING AND THE ORICHALCUM RULE`
