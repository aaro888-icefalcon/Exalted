# Abyssal — Antagonist Charm Pool

> Mirror-Solar deathknights: decisive killers who feed on Initiative and death, pile on wound/crippling penalties, drain motes/life, deny healing, and wear grave-cold perfect defenses.
> Anima/Essence trick: dark Caste Marks bleed darkness and at burning+ the chill withers/rots everything nearby; at bonfire they radiate terror (Heart-Stopping Mien). Their "resonance" is the Great Curse as Bleak Expiation — defying death spawns Blights of rot — while anima powers raise zombies, bind ghosts, and let them drink blood for motes.
