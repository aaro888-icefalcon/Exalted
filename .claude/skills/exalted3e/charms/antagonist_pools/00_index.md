# Antagonist Charm Pools — Index

Small, curated Charm pools to stat NON-PLAYER rivals/foes fast (one per non-Lunar Exalt type, plus spirits). Each file groups ~18–24 encounter-relevant Charms by role (Offense / Defense / Mobility / Social-Mental / Signature), one line apiece, cited `Book › Charm`. These are for NPCs you throw at a Lunar player — pick a handful, don't load the catalog. (Lunars themselves: see `lunar/`.)

Citation keys: Core = Core Rulebook · WFHW = What Fire Has Wrought · CFC = Charting Fate's Course · StG = Sworn to the Grave · FbtMG = Forged by the Machine God · OotA = Out of the Ashes · HDNP = Hundred Devils Night Parade · AotR = Adversaries of the Righteous.

- **solar** (`solar.md`) — Generalist demigod: peerless accuracy, perfect defenses, one-exchange decisive kills, dominant influence. Field as a Lunar's mirror-match or the Solar Deliberative rival who out-aces her at everything.
- **dragonblooded** (`dragonblooded.md`) — Team attrition with cheap Excellencies and elemental Auras; weak solo, deadly in a pack. Field as a Wyld Hunt or Realm legion sent to run the Lunar down by numbers and environmental damage.
- **sidereal** (`sidereal.md`) — Fate-cheater: cuts difficulties, dodges by prophecy, curses destinies, hides in false fates. Field as the hidden hand orchestrating the hunt — she rarely fights, she arranges the Lunar's doom.
- **abyssal** (`abyssal.md`) — Death-fueled decisive killer: wound-stacking, life-drain, denied healing, terror, grave-cold perfects. Field as the dark-mirror nemesis racing the Lunar to a kill, raising her own dead.
- **alchemical** (`alchemical.md`) — Modular war-machine: integrated cannons/blades/plating, tanky and reconfigurable, cold Clarity. Field as an Autochthonian incursion or a relentless construct-hunter the Lunar can't out-shapeshift.
- **exigent** (`exigent.md`) — Bespoke god-chosen one-off; monstrous inside its narrow domain, ordinary outside it. Field as a local champion-deity's Chosen defending home turf — pick a type and lean on its single trick.
- **spirits** (`spirits.md`) — Gods/elementals/demons/ghosts: fight materialized, flee dematerialized; bless, curse, possess. Field as patrons, summoned servitors, or terrain bosses — the backbone list for statting any divine foe fast.
