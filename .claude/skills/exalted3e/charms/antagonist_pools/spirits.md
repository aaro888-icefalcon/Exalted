# Spirits — Antagonist Charm Pool

> Most spirits fight MATERIALIZED (pay motes for flesh) and flee DEMATERIALIZED when crashed; gods/Censors/Circle demons hit like Exalts, lesser elementals and ghosts are mooks. They bargain, bless, and curse more than they brawl.
> Anima/Essence trick: their "anima" is divine manifestation; nearly all keep a sanctum and can answer prayers from afar. Universal Charms below recur across most statblocks — costs scale up for greater gods/demons.
