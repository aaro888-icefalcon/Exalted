# Dragon-Blooded — Antagonist Charm Pool

> Weaker than Celestials solo but lethal as a team: cheap Excellencies, attrition, elemental damage, layered defenses, and Charms that reward being in the matching Aura.
> Anima/Essence trick: each Aspect flies an elemental ANIMA banner and at bonfire sheds anima flux (elemental hazard to nearby non-DBs). Spending an elemental Charm enters that element's AURA (scene-long; ends if you use an off-element Charm) — Auras empower matching Charms, and many Signature Charms EXPEND the Aura for a big payoff.
