# Solar — Antagonist Charm Pool

> Solars fight like demigods: peerless accuracy, perfect defenses, decisive blows that end a fight in one exchange. They pay motes for nearly everything, so a rival's anima climbs as she spends — at **bonfire** iconic tricks unlock (reflexive extra attacks) and her glory cows foes; the Limit/Great Curse is flavor, the combat note is mote economy.
