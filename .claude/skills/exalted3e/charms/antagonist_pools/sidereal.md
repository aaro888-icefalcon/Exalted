# Sidereal — Antagonist Charm Pool

> Sidereals don't out-muscle foes — they cheat the rules of fate: cut difficulties, force rerolls, dodge by prophecy, curse threads of destiny, "arrange a coincidence," and edit fate so "you cannot do that to me." They avoid fights they can't win and stack probability instead.
> Anima/Essence trick: Maiden caste-marks and an astrological anima that can blaze their Caste constellation; they wear resplendent destinies (false fates), and at high anima become arcane fate / invisible to fate (enemies of fate).
