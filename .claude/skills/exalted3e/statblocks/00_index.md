# Statblocks — Ready-Foe Index

Tagged index of every **ready-to-run** statblock in the vault (has Essence + COMBAT/Defense/Soak/Health), so the GM can drop a foe into a scene without building one. Lore-only entities live in `setting/06_bestiary_lore.md` and the place/faction cards.

**Format:** `Name — type — tier — Book › Heading`
**type:** mortal · spirit · god · elemental · demon · ghost · undead · Fae (raksha) · Exalt · beast · behemoth · construct · battle-group
**tier (solo-GM read):** minor (mook, E1) · standard (E1–2 named) · elite (E3–5) · boss (E6+ / Second Circle demon) · legendary (elder Exalt / Third Circle demon / great god)

**Books:** AotR = Adversaries of the Righteous · HDNP = Hundred Devils Night Parade · CRB = Core Rulebook (Ch8 Antagonists) · Lunars = Lunars: Fangs at the Gate (Ch9). All also hold generic Quick-Character templates (CRB Ch8 › Quick Characters; CRB Ch8 › The Exalted) for fast mooks/foils.

> Tier is inferred from Essence rating + described might; the vault stat line is authoritative. Sort within a book is by chapter, then alphabetical.

## Coverage notes

- **~257 ready statblocks** total: AotR ~72 · HDNP ~99 · CRB Ch8 ~59 (+ generic templates) · Lunars Ch9 27.
- CRB mortal/animal/dead entries are **slash-named templates** (e.g., "Brigand / Conscript / Militia"); reskin freely.
- The Lunars "Red Howling Fiend" and "Ten Tentacles Ascendant" near the file's back are **Kickstarter backer credits, not statblocks** — excluded.
- Heavier antagonist supplements (Abyssals, Dragon-Blooded, Sidereals, Exigents, Alchemicals) sit in the vault but are **out of scope** for this index; mine them directly if needed.
