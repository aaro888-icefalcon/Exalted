# Chaos Tendency — Creation (Age of Sorrows)   (hook: chaos)

The engine's Chaos Factor and Exalted's **Turmoil** are the **same 1–9 number**. Call it
either; store it once in `campaign-state.md`.

- **start:** 5
- **volatility:** normal        # −1 if the PC was mostly in control of the last scene, +1 if it was chaotic
- **floor:** an active **Wyld Hunt on the PC**, an open **warfront**, or a deep **Wyld zone** holds Chaos ≥ 4
  (Creation does not calm while a god-killer or the Hunt is at your heel)
- **flavor:** standard          # full Mythic chaos — Exalts are demigods, but the Age of Sorrows is anything but stable

Higher Turmoil = the world acts on the Lunar more (more Altered/Interrupt scenes, more Random
Events, the Fate Chart swings toward the dramatic). It does **not** mean "more lethal" — lethality
is set by the fiction and the genre lens (`interpretation.md`), never by Chaos alone.
