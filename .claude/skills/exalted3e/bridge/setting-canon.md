# Setting Canon — Creation (the Age of Sorrows)   (ground truth; overrides recollection)

Consult canon before inventing. The full world lives in the `exalted3e` skill; this file is the
index to it. Reveal a card's **Truth** only when its in-fiction trigger fires (player ≠ PC
knowledge). When all sources are silent, a Fate Question decides and the answer is written here or
to the campaign's own `setting-canon.md` to stay consistent.

## Premise / tone
- **The Age of Sorrows.** Creation is vast, old, and dangerous. The Solar Exalted have returned and
  the Realm's Empress has vanished, so every old order is cracking. The PC is a **Lunar Exalt** — a
  shapeshifter and culture-hero hunted as *Anathema*, standing at the margins of empire.
- Default register: **Creation-as-Threat** (the world is a credible danger) over **Creation-as-Cost**
  (power exacts a price). See `interpretation.md` for the agenda this implies.

## The master tension (faction board)
The **Realm** (wounded, the Empress gone) vs. the **Silver Pact** (the PC's Lunar kin, striking
while the iron is cold), with the **Immaculate Order / Wyld Hunt**, the **Great Houses**, the
**Guild**, **Lookshy**, the **Deathlords**, the **Fair Folk**, the **Sidereals (Bureau of Destiny)**,
and **Malfeas/demon-cults** as the other players. Default PC Tracks and starting clocks are in
`setting/04_factions.md` → "Setting the board."

## Per-campaign canon
Each campaign keeps its **own** `setting-canon.md` in its folder for home turf, local factions, the
Lunar's secret, and Truths earned in play. That file layers **on top of** this one; this file is the
shared Creation baseline.

## Hard content lines
- Hardcore play: honest dice, real consequences, no rescues. Defeat, Limit Break, and death are real.
- Respect the table's safety lines (Session Zero); Creation-as-Threat leans dark — don't push past a
  line a player has drawn. Peril Points OFF unless the player opts in.
