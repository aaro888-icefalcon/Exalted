## Step 3 — Inhabitants (group + sub-rolls)
First, **who's here** (`roll 1d12`): 1 newly-Exalted youth + a green cult · 2 First Age war-automata (no master) · 3 a raksha court slumming in the ruin · 4 hungry ghosts under a nephwrack · 5 the resident god + sanctum-keepers · 6 a beastfolk warband · 7 a Wyld Hunt expedition (Dragon-Blooded + mortals) · 8 a Guild salvage crew with hired muscle · 9 a sorcerer with bound demons · 10 a Lunar/Solar rival + retinue · 11 refugees squatting, unaware what they sleep atop · 12 nobody living — only the Hazard.

Then layer sub-rolls (each `roll 1d6`, read the band):
- **Why here:** 1–2 trapped/can't leave · 3–4 came for the same prize you did · 5–6 guarding it for someone.
- **Goal:** 1 survive · 2 loot and go · 3 hold the site · 4 free/wake what's bound · 5 study it · 6 use it as a base for a wider scheme.
- **Leadership:** 1 a tyrant · 2 a council that argues · 3 a charismatic believer · 4 a cold pragmatist · 5 leaderless / fractured · 6 secretly led by the Hazard itself.
- **Defenses:** 1 numbers · 2 a chokepoint they hold · 3 a turned automaton/ward · 4 a hostage or relic they'll threaten · 5 an alarm to summon worse · 6 a Charm-user who hits above their weight.
- **Internal Problem (a hook):** 1 a traitor · 2 starving/short on Essence · 3 a feud splitting them · 4 a wounded leader · 5 a captive they mistreat · 6 a secret that ruins them if told.
- **Recent event:** 1 a death · 2 a discovery · 3 a betrayal · 4 a failed ritual · 5 an arrival (yours, or another's) · 6 the Hazard worsened.
- **External stance** (toward the PC): 1 hostile on sight · 2 wary, will parley · 3 desperate for aid · 4 will trade · 5 try to use the PC · 6 indifferent until provoked.

## Step 4 — Six Keyed Locations
For each of 6 rooms, roll the sub-tables (`roll 1d8` unless noted). Skip what's dull.
- **Purpose:** 1 threshold/gate · 2 great hall · 3 workshop/forge · 4 archive/vault · 5 shrine/tomb · 6 quarters · 7 the engine/core · 8 a wound (breach, sinkhole, Wyld-pool).
- **Valuables here? (`roll 1d6`):** 1–3 none visible · 4 minor (coin, components) · 5 a Feature (hearthstone, lore, a key) · 6 the Reward (Step 5), guarded.
- **Mood:** 1 reverent · 2 ruined/sad · 3 tense · 4 wrong/uncanny · 5 serene (a trap) · 6 violent aftermath · 7 frozen mid-moment · 8 alive, watching.
- **Ingress Problem:** 1 sealed door (puzzle/phrase) · 2 collapse blocks it · 3 flooded/gassed · 4 guarded · 5 warded (Essence cost to pass) · 6 only reachable via another room.
- **Peril (`roll 1d6`):** 1 physical (trap, fall, fire) · 2 occupant (an inhabitant lairs here) · 3 magical (curse, hostile Charm-effect) · 4 the Hazard concentrated · 5 false floor over something worse · 6 none — let them breathe.
- **Feature (`roll 1d6`):** 1 a working device · 2 a vantage/shortcut · 3 a clue to the Reward's lock · 4 a defensible spot · 5 raw Essence to harvest · 6 a captive/witness who knows things.
- **Useful info:** 1 a name · 2 a map · 3 the command-phrase · 4 who looted it before · 5 the Hazard's weakness · 6 a Problem of a faction outside (a hook out the door).
