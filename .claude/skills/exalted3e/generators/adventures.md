# Adventures — Session-Hook Generator

A reason to leave the inn, in four rolls: **Situation + Draw + Threat + Challenge.** Roll with `scripts/ex_dice.py roll NdM`. The fastest hook of all is the shortcut at the bottom: **pull a faction Problem.**

## The frame: Draw vs. Threat
- **Draw** = what pulls the PC *in* (reward, duty, a person, a mystery). It answers "why bother?"
- **Threat** = what pushes *back* (the danger, the clock, the cost). It answers "why is it hard?"
- A good hook needs **both**. Draw with no Threat is a chore; Threat with no Draw is avoidable. If the PC can ignore it with no loss, the Draw is too weak; if there's no way to win, the Threat is too strong. Tune until the PC *wants* to engage and *can't* do it for free.

## Step 1 — Situation (`roll 1d12`) — the state of play
1 a settlement under a Problem (cross-ref `communities.md`) · 2 a court mid-intrigue (`courts.md`) · 3 a ruin newly opened (`ruins.md`) · 4 a faction on the move (army, caravan, Wyld Hunt) · 5 a cult rising or schisming (`cults.md`) · 6 a god/spirit out of balance · 7 a shadowland spreading · 8 a Wyld incursion / raksha raid · 9 a succession or a death of a power · 10 a Realm crackdown (tribute, Anathema-hunt) · 11 a natural disaster (flood, quake, blight) · 12 a rival Exalt's scheme bearing fruit.

## Step 2 — Draw (`roll 1d12`) — why the PC engages
| d12 | The pull |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reward: an artifact, a hearthstone, a demesne |
| 2 | A person in danger the PC cares about (or could) |
| 3 | A debt owed *to* the PC comes due |
| 4 | A mystery / a Truth half-glimpsed |
| 5 | Tribe / cult / charge the PC protects is threatened |
| 6 | A rival the PC wants to thwart is involved |
| 7 | A chance to grow a Project (build, rally, claim) |
| 8 | Knowledge: a First Age secret, a lost name |
| 9 | Reputation / a chance to be *seen* as a god |
| 10 | An oath or Intimacy demands action |
| 11 | A faction offers patronage for one job |
| 12 | Simple justice: a wrong the PC can't walk past |

## Step 3 — Threat (`roll 1d12`) — what pushes back
| d12 | The danger / cost |
|---|---|
| 1 | A rival Exalt (Dragon-Blood, Solar, Lunar, Abyssal) |
| 2 | The Wyld Hunt closing in |
| 3 | A behemoth / strange-beast (cross-ref `foes.md`) |
| 4 | A bound or angry god/elemental |
| 5 | A Deathlord's agent + the restless dead |
| 6 | Raksha / Fair Folk and their unreality |
| 7 | A Great House / the Realm garrison |
| 8 | The Guild and its money and muscle |
| 9 | A sorcerer with bound demons |
| 10 | A failing First Age Hazard (radiation, stasis, miasma) |
| 11 | A ticking clock (ritual, tribute deadline, spreading rot) |
| 12 | The PC's own past / a secret that could surface |

## Step 4 — Challenge (the obstacle shape)
Roll a goal-type + complication on `challenges.md` to give the hook its central scene — the thing the PC must actually *do* (kill, convince, find, undo-magic, rally…). That complication is the session's spine.

## Assembled example
*Situation: a shadowland spreading (7). Draw: a tribe the PC protects is threatened (5). Threat: a Deathlord's agent + the dead (5). Challenge: undo-magic / "unbinding frees what was bound."* → A nephwrack is unraveling the wards on an old battlefield to swell their army; the PC's people are next, but breaking the nephwrack's working risks loosing the very thing the wards have caged. Draw and Threat both present; the PC must act, and can't act cleanly.

## Shortcut — pull a faction Problem as the hook
Skip the rolls: open `setting/04_factions.md`, grab any faction's live `Problem:`, and that **is** the adventure. The Draw is "whoever clears this gains the faction's favor / weakens it"; the Threat is whatever defends the Problem from being solved. Clearing it edits the board (deletes the Problem, maybe adds a Feature). This is the agency loop — **Problems are hooks, and resolving them visibly moves Creation.** When a session ends, advance unaddressed Problems one step on their clocks so the world keeps turning whether the PC acts or not.
