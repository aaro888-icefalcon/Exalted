# Communities — Settlement Generator

A wandering Lunar (or any Exalt) walks into a village or town. Make it live in two rolls; the **Problem** is why the PC is needed. Roll with `scripts/ex_dice.py roll NdM`. Read as **Feature** (what they have) + **Problem** (the cry for a god).

## Step 1 — Nature (`roll 1d10`)
| d10 | Settlement | Feature seed | Likely tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farming village (rice, grain) | full granaries, a harvest festival | a shrine to a local god of the field |
| 2 | Herding camp / steppe clan | horses, warbeasts, mobility | a totem-spirit, animal omens |
| 3 | Mining town | jade, salt, or iron; a deep shaft | something woke in the lowest tunnel |
| 4 | River-port / trade-stop | a Guild factor, news, contraband | a god of the ford takes tolls |
| 5 | Wyld-edge frontier hamlet | hardy folk, strange crops | mutations, doors that open onto elsewhere |
| 6 | Refugee camp (war/famine fled) | desperate loyalty, hidden skills | the dead of the old home still follow them |
| 7 | Fishing/coastal village | boats, pearls, a sea-shrine | tribute owed to a thing in the water |
| 8 | Logging/forest village (or tree-city) | timber, hunters, a sacred grove | the forest's god is angry or absent |
| 9 | Satrapy town under Realm tribute | a tax-house, a small garrison | an Immaculate monk watches for Anathema |
| 10 | Pilgrim/shrine town | a holy site, donations, a cult | the patron god has gone silent |

## Step 2 — Leadership (`roll 1d8`)
1 a council of elders · 2 a hereditary headman/matriarch · 3 a Realm-appointed reeve (resented) · 4 the local god speaks through a priest · 5 a Guild factor who really runs things · 6 a war-leader (recent threat) · 7 no one — leaderless, frightened · 8 a charismatic newcomer (savior or con).

## Step 3 — The Feature (`roll 1d10`) — what they have worth protecting
1 a hearthstone/demesne nearby · 2 a relic or shrine of real power · 3 a master craft (a smith, a weaver of note) · 4 a strategic pass/ford/harbor · 5 unusual loyalty / a militia that fights above its weight · 6 a secret heir or hidden Exalt among them · 7 a stockpile (grain, jade, medicine) · 8 a bound or friendly local god · 9 knowledge (a map, a rite, a name) · 10 simply that they're *good people* worth saving.

## Step 4 — The Problem (`roll 1d12`) — the cry for a god (your hook)
| d12 | The Problem |
|---|---|
| 1 | A monster/behemoth/strange-beast preys on them (see HDNP-style threats) |
| 2 | Bandits, a warlord, or a Realm tax-collector bleeds them dry |
| 3 | Famine, blight, or a poisoned well |
| 4 | A shadowland is spreading at the edge of the fields; the dead walk |
| 5 | The local god has gone mad, silent, or greedy and demands too much |
| 6 | A raksha / Fair Folk steals dreams, children, or names |
| 7 | A feud or murder splits the village against itself |
| 8 | The Wyld is creeping closer; crops and folk are changing |
| 9 | A plague, or a curse laid by a slighted spirit |
| 10 | An Immaculate monk has come hunting Anathema — and someone here qualifies |
| 11 | A debt to the Guild / a Great House that will take people as payment |
| 12 | A buried First Age thing under the town is waking (cross-ref `ruins.md`) |

## Step 5 — Supernatural Tells / Omens (`roll 1d8`)
The wrongness the PC notices before anyone speaks: 1 livestock born malformed · 2 a shrine over-laden or stripped bare · 3 ghost-lights / cold spots after dark · 4 the river/sky the wrong color · 5 a circle of dead grass, perfectly round · 6 charms and wards on every door · 7 a song or rite everyone knows but won't explain · 8 a stranger already here, watching (another Exalt? a spy?).

## Step 6 — Stance Toward Exalted (`roll 1d8`)
| d8 | How they greet a revealed Exalt | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awe — they kneel, offer the best they have | a god has come; they ask for miracles |
| 2 | Desperate welcome | "thank the heavens, *do* something about the Problem" |
| 3 | Immaculate fear | Anathema! they hide children, send word to the monk |
| 4 | Wary bargaining | help is welcome — at a price, and watch your back |
| 5 | Old loyalty | a grandmother's tale says their kind once protected here |
| 6 | Greedy opportunism | how do *we* profit from having a god in town? |
| 7 | Hostile / armed | a prior Exalt wronged them; trust is gone |
| 8 | Oblivious | they don't recognize what the PC is — yet |

**Resolution promotes upward.** Clear the Problem and the settlement can become a faction Feature ("a loyal village," a demesne held) in `setting/04_factions.md`; ignore it and the Problem worsens on a clock. A village saved is the cheapest first brick of a Project (`rules/09_projects_and_dominion.md`).
