# Challenges — Obstacle Generator

Turns "the PC wants X" into a *complicated scene* instead of a yes. Pick the **goal-type** that matches the stated want, then roll its complication (`scripts/ex_dice.py roll 1d12`). The complication is a fresh **Problem**; what the PC already has is the **Feature** they'll lean on.

**Use it whenever** a request would otherwise just succeed. Stack a second complication for a major scene; lift one straight as a side-hook.

## Goal-types (10)
build · change-custom · clear-ruin · convince · find · kill · rally · resolve-conflict · sneak/steal · undo-magic.

---
### BUILD (a manse, a fort, a confederation, a Project)
1 the site is claimed/sacred · 2 the materials need a ruin-dive · 3 the only artisan is held hostage/indentured · 4 geomancy backfires without a rite · 5 a rival starts the same build faster · 6 the Guild owns the supply line · 7 a bound god demands tribute to allow it · 8 labor revolts mid-project · 9 the Realm taxes/forbids it · 10 it works — but draws a behemoth/elemental · 11 a hidden flaw will collapse it later · 12 finishing it requires a Mighty Deed (cross-ref Projects).

### CHANGE-CUSTOM (abolish a practice, install a new law/faith)
1 an Immaculate edict forbids it · 2 the elders' livelihood depends on the old way · 3 a god enforces the custom · 4 the change shames a powerful family · 5 it's load-bearing for a worse stability · 6 the young want it, the old will kill for tradition · 7 a martyr is needed first · 8 a rival Exalt champions the opposite · 9 it spreads too fast and warps · 10 it requires breaking an oath · 11 the Realm reads it as rebellion · 12 success creates a new, worse custom.

### CLEAR-RUIN (cross-ref `ruins.md` for the site itself)
1 the Hazard reactivates · 2 someone got there first · 3 the "monster" is a bound innocent · 4 clearing it frees something sealed · 5 a Wyld Hunt is en route · 6 the map is wrong · 7 the prize is cursed/keyed · 8 a patron wants it *intact* · 9 the path floods/collapses on a timer · 10 the inhabitants surrender — now they're dependents · 11 it's a trap baited by a rival · 12 the real ruin is below the ruin.

### CONVINCE (a court, a god, a holdout — uses Resolve/Guile)
1 they have high Resolve; need an Intimacy lever first · 2 a rival whispers the opposite · 3 they demand a deed as proof · 4 they're under a Charm/oath · 5 telling the truth ruins someone the PC likes · 6 the audience is hostile (Immaculate crowd) · 7 the decider is a puppet; find the hidden hand · 8 a translation/cultural gap · 9 they want a bribe the PC can't afford · 10 success offends a third party · 11 they agree — then betray · 12 they'll only listen mid-crisis.

### FIND (a person, a relic, a path, a truth)
1 it's hidden behind a riddle/cipher · 2 the only witness is dead/mad/ghost · 3 it's moving/being moved · 4 a rival hunts the same thing · 5 it's warded from scrying · 6 the trail runs through a court (cross-ref `courts.md`) · 7 it's in a shadowland/Wyld zone · 8 finding it alerts its guardian · 9 the "it" is a decoy · 10 the source wants payment in secrets · 11 it's been split into pieces · 12 it finds the PC first.

### KILL (a foe, a behemoth, a Deathlord's agent — cross-ref `foes.md`)
1 it has a defensive Charm that no-sells the first blow · 2 it's protected by hostages/a crowd · 3 killing it frees something worse · 4 it accelerates when crashed (flees/grows) · 5 it's the wrong target (mistaken identity) · 6 it has a Wyld Hunt / faction backing · 7 it can only be killed by a specific means · 8 it's already dying — and warns of the real threat · 9 reinforcements on a clock · 10 it surrenders / begs · 11 its death triggers a curse on the killer · 12 it returns (ghost, regen, body-double).

### RALLY (a militia, a tribe, a confederation — Magnitude play)
1 old feuds split the would-be allies · 2 they demand a champion's duel first · 3 a rival recruiter beat the PC there · 4 they're terrified of the Realm's reprisal · 5 a traitor in the ranks · 6 they need arms/food the PC must find · 7 a god must bless the muster · 8 they'll follow only after a visible miracle · 9 the leader is a coward/sellout · 10 numbers come, but green and unled · 11 success makes the PC a target faction · 12 they rally — for the *wrong* cause.

### RESOLVE-CONFLICT (a feud, a war, a succession)
1 both sides lie about the cause · 2 a third party profits from the strife · 3 the wronged party wants blood, not peace · 4 an old oath binds the fight · 5 a god/ghost feeds on the conflict · 6 the fair solution ruins a sympathetic figure · 7 the leaders are puppets · 8 peace requires a sacrifice · 9 the Realm wants it to continue · 10 a hidden grievance no one names · 11 settling it reignites a worse one · 12 only a marriage/hostage can seal it.

### SNEAK / STEAL (infiltrate, lift, slip past)
1 a bound god/elemental warden · 2 First Age wards trip on Essence use · 3 a witness who can't be silenced cleanly · 4 the prize is bait · 5 the layout shifts (Wyld/living-manse) · 6 a rival thief is inside already · 7 it's too heavy/large/alive to carry · 8 a Charm-user can sense intruders · 9 the route only opens on a schedule · 10 taking it triggers a curse/alarm-to-worse · 11 the owner is innocent/sympathetic · 12 success — but a clue points home to the PC.

### UNDO-MAGIC (break a curse, a ward, a binding, a working)
1 the caster must be found and faced · 2 unbinding frees what was bound · 3 it needs a rare reagent (a ruin-dive) · 4 the curse jumps to the breaker · 5 a counter-Charm is needed (sorcery/Essence) · 6 the ward is also load-bearing for a wall/dam · 7 a god owns the working and objects · 8 the victim resists being freed · 9 it's not magic — it's mundane (the diagnosis is the trap) · 10 partial undoing makes it worse · 11 the Immaculate Order wants the working studied, not destroyed · 12 undoing it reveals an uglier truth beneath.
