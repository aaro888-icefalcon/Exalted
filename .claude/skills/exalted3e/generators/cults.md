# Cults — Faith-as-Faction Generator

Builds god-cults, totem-cults, Immaculate cells, and Exalt-worship congregations. A cult is a **faction subtype** — roll it here, then register it in `setting/04_factions.md` (Magnitude/Cohesion/Trouble). Roll with `scripts/ex_dice.py roll NdM`. Read as **Features** + **Problems** (the Problems are what the PC can exploit or fix).

## Step 1 — Patron (`roll 1d10`)
| d10 | Worshipped power | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A god of a place (river, mountain, ford, harvest) | local, transactional, jealous of tribute |
| 2 | A god of a craft or trade | guild-like; bound to a profession |
| 3 | An animal totem (steppe/forest clan) | shamanic; omens, beast-spirits, taboos |
| 4 | The Immaculate Dragons (an Order cell) | doctrine, monks, Anathema-hunting |
| 5 | An Exalt as living god (the PC, or a rival) | new, fervent, fragile — built on a person |
| 6 | A dead hero / ancestor cult | graves, oaths, a ghost that may answer |
| 7 | A demon (Yozi-touched, secret) | hidden, transgressive, hunted if found |
| 8 | A Deathlord / the Neverborn (a death-cult) | shadowland-fed, nihilist, recruits the grieving |
| 9 | A behemoth or Wyld-thing held as divine | terrible, propitiated, not loved |
| 10 | A syncretic mix (roll twice; reconcile or schism) | tension built in |

## Step 2 — Holy-Law Severity ↔ the trade-off
Stricter law = more **Power** (devotion, miracles, obedience) and more **Problems** (brittleness, schism, enemies). Roll `1d6`:
| d6 | Severity | Power gained | Problem incurred |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loose / folk-faith | broad reach, easy growth | shallow loyalty; scatters under pressure |
| 2 | Customary | steady tithes, local trust | complacent; co-opted by the powerful |
| 3 | Devout | reliable miracles, a militia of believers | intolerant of outsiders; makes enemies |
| 4 | Strict | fierce obedience, real supernatural aid | schisms over doctrine; heretics breed |
| 5 | Zealous | fanatic devotees, willing martyrs | violent; the Realm/neighbors move against it |
| 6 | Absolute / sacrificial | the patron *answers directly* | demands blood/sacrifice; rots from within |

Higher severity raises the cult's effective Magnitude-as-threat but lowers Cohesion (one schism from splintering).

## Step 3 — Feature (`roll 1d8`) — what the cult can do
1 a real miracle on tap (heal, bless crops, ward) · 2 a militia of true believers · 3 a spy web of the pious (everyone confesses) · 4 a treasury of tithes / sacred relics · 5 the patron grants a Charm-like boon to clergy · 6 sanctuary network (safe houses across a region) · 7 legitimacy (the local lord needs their blessing) · 8 a holy site / demesne the patron empowers.

## Step 4 — Inherent Problems (`roll 1d8`, take 1-2) — the hooks
1 a schism brewing over doctrine/succession · 2 the patron is waning, mad, or absent (miracles failing) · 3 a charismatic heretic splitting the flock · 4 the Realm/Immaculate Order marks it for suppression · 5 it demands a sacrifice the faithful are starting to question · 6 a rival cult contests the same patron/territory · 7 corrupt clergy embezzle tithes / abuse the flock · 8 the patron's *true nature* is a secret that would shatter it (a "god" that's a demon, a hero that's a ghost).

## Step 5 — How It Grows (`roll 1d6`)
1 by miracle (a public wonder wins converts) · 2 by charity (feeds the poor; loyalty follows bread) · 3 by the sword (convert or be cast out) · 4 by patronage (a lord adopts it; top-down) · 5 by infiltration (quiet, cell by cell, families first) · 6 by crisis (every disaster swells the ranks). Each growth mode implies its own counter: starve the charity, expose the miracle, behead the patron, turn the lord.

## Step 6 — Faction Subtype (cross-ref `setting/04_factions.md`)
Map the cult onto a Goal archetype for its faction turn:
- **Theocrat** — an Immaculate cell or established temple (consolidates, enforces doctrine).
- **Predator** — a death-cult or demon-cult (feeds on a region, recruits the desperate).
- **Survivor** — a folk-faith under Realm pressure (hides, hoards loyalty).
- **Schemer** — a secret/syncretic cult working through other factions.
- **Conqueror** — a zealous expansionist faith (convert by sword).
- **Tyrant** — an Exalt-worship cult bound to one ruler's cult of personality.

**PC leverage.** A cult the PC founds is a faction **Feature** with a backlash **Problem** (Step 4) baked in. Resolving another cult's Problem (heal its mad patron, expose its embezzlers, settle its schism) either wins it as an ally or collapses it — both move the board.
