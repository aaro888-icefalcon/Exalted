# Courts — Power-Group & Intrigue Generator

For satrap courts, House seats, Lunar councils, Guild caravans, Yu-Shan god-courts — any room where power is contested. Roll with `scripts/ex_dice.py roll NdM`.

**Signature move: actor ≠ power source.** Every actor *holds* or *wants* a power source but is not identical to it. An **orphaned power source** — one no actor currently controls (a bound god whose binder died, a vacant office, a treasury with no heir) — is a ready-made hook: whoever grabs it wins. Look for the gap.

## Step 1 — Power Structure (`roll 1d6`)
| d6 | Structure | Who really decides |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Autocratic | one Dragon-Blood / satrap / god rules; the rest petition |
| 2 | Oligarchic | a handful of peers balance each other |
| 3 | Bureaucratic | offices outlast officeholders; the ledger is king |
| 4 | Factional | two-three blocs in open rivalry |
| 5 | Theocratic | an Immaculate (or cult) hierarchy overrules the secular |
| 6 | Anarchic / contested | the seat is *empty or disputed* — power is up for grabs |

## Step 2 — Mood (`roll 1d12`)
1 paranoid · 2 complacent · 3 grieving a recent death · 4 celebrating (a wedding, a victory) · 5 starving for coin · 6 braced for war · 7 festering with a buried scandal · 8 desperate for a savior · 9 smug and cruel · 10 fracturing openly · 11 in mourning for a vanished patron · 12 watching the PC the moment they enter.

## Step 3 — Major Actors (roll **3+**)
For each actor: a **Role**, a **Power Source**, and an **Agenda**.

**Role (`roll 1d10`):** 1 the seat-holder (satrap/matriarch/god) · 2 the heir/rival claimant · 3 the spymaster · 4 the treasurer/factor · 5 the priest/Immaculate · 6 the general/garrison-commander · 7 the favored advisor · 8 the outsider with leverage (Guild, foreign envoy) · 9 the indispensable functionary · 10 the power-behind-the-throne (hidden).

**Power Source (`roll 1d12`) — the lever they pull:**
| d12 | Source | If it goes orphan, the hook is… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legitimacy (birth, mandate, an Imperial writ) | a vacant claim everyone forges papers for |
| 2 | The treasury | unguarded gold; whoever takes it funds a coup |
| 3 | Immaculate sanction | a blessing withdrawn; heresy charges fly |
| 4 | A bound god / elemental | the binding lapses; the god is loose and owed |
| 5 | The Realm garrison / legion detachment | leaderless troops up for the highest bidder |
| 6 | Guild credit / a trade monopoly | a defaulted debt; the caravan reroutes |
| 7 | A spy network | masterless agents selling to anyone |
| 8 | Blackmail (held on a Dynast) | the file resurfaces with no owner |
| 9 | An indispensable office (water, tribute, the courts) | the seat empties; the function fails |
| 10 | Popular love (a hero, a feeder of the poor) | a leaderless mob looking for a champion |
| 11 | A sorcerer's working / a relic | an unattended power source anyone can claim |
| 12 | Marriage alliance / hostages | a broken match; both Houses feel the slight |

**Agenda (`roll 1d6`):** 1 hold what they have · 2 take a rival's power source · 3 install/depose the seat-holder · 4 cover a crime · 5 court the PC as a tool · 6 burn it all down rather than lose.

## Step 4 — The Central Conflict
Pick the two strongest actors (or roll the spread). State the **stakes** (`roll 1d8`): 1 succession · 2 the treasury/tribute · 3 a marriage · 4 a heresy/Immaculate ruling · 5 a war (start it / stop it) · 6 control of a bound god or relic · 7 exposure of a scandal · 8 the PC themselves (both want to recruit or remove them).

Assign **sides**: who backs whom, and **why** (each minor actor leans toward whoever protects *their* power source). Note one actor who is **secretly playing both sides** (`roll 1d6`: on 5–6, yes).

## Step 5 — Minor Actors (entry points — roll 2-3)
`roll 1d10`: 1 a slighted heir · 2 an indebted gambler · 3 a true believer · 4 a frightened servant who sees all · 5 a bitter veteran · 6 a Guild clerk with the real ledgers · 7 a discarded lover · 8 a captive/hostage · 9 a feral god of the household shrine · 10 a child who repeats secrets. Each is a **door**: cheap to turn, knows one true thing, wants one small thing.

## Step 6 — Defenses (if the PC attacks the court)
`roll 1d8`: 1 Dragon-Blooded house guard (combat) · 2 a bound god/elemental ward · 3 the garrison (a battle group) · 4 Immaculate retaliation (the Wyld Hunt hears) · 5 social: ruin the PC's name, not their body · 6 a poisoner / assassin on retainer · 7 they scatter and go to ground (no target) · 8 they have a hostage/leverage on someone the PC loves.

## Step 7 — Consequences if Destroyed
`roll 1d8`: 1 a power vacuum a worse faction fills · 2 the orphaned power source (Step 3) becomes a free prize · 3 famine/chaos hits the dependent community · 4 a rival House moves in · 5 the bound god is freed and vengeful · 6 the PC inherits the seat — and its Problems · 7 a martyr is made; the cause grows · 8 nothing changes; the bureaucracy reabsorbs the wound. Promote any survivor's Agenda into a faction `Problem:` in `setting/04_factions.md`.
