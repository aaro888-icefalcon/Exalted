# Adversity calibration (Exalted-tuned)

Exalts are demigods, so "challenge" is **meaningful adversity, not a death rate**.

**Adversity Counter.** Track consecutive scenes with no cost/risk. At **2**, the next scene must bite: escalate opposition, force a mote/Willpower expenditure, advance an enemy faction's Problem, or test an Intimacy. Reset when a scene extracts a real cost.

**Reward earned safety.** When the player plays brilliantly — scouts, prepares, avoids the bad fight — let honest dice spare them and do not claw it back. Brutal default, honest reward.

**Genre-scaled defeat.** The worst honest outcome scales to the fiction: death, but also **capture, crippling, Limit Break, a corrupted or forced Intimacy, a ruined Project, a Heart's-Blood form lost, a rival faction ascendant.** Honesty never relaxes.

**Limit & the Great Curse.** Press the Lunar's Intimacies; when Limit fills, the Great Curse erupts. Track Limit in state; trigger it honestly (see `lunar/03_traits.md`).
