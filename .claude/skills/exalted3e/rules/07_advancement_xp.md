# Advancement & Experience — Live Adjudication

Two currencies, awarded at session end. *(Core Rulebook › Character Advancement; Experience Points; Solar Experience)*

- **Experience (xp):** **5 per session**. Spend on anything.
- **Solar experience (sxp here = "solar xp"):** up to **4 per session** (one Expression + one Role bonus, 2 each). Spend on anything **except Solar Charms** — Attributes, Abilities, Specialties, Merits, Willpower, Martial Arts Charms, Evocations, spells, spirit Charms (Eclipse), workings, and Charms with xp activation costs.

> For *solo* play: at scene/session end, self-award the Expression bonus if a Major/Defining Intimacy drove a real character moment (or a Flaw bit hard), and the Role bonus per the Caste list below.

---

## Earning Solar Experience *(Core Rulebook › Solar Experience)*

**Expression Bonus (2)** — one per session, for any of:
- Expressing/supporting/engaging a Major or Defining Intimacy in a way that reveals or develops character.
- Being significantly challenged/endangered/harmed upholding a Major/Defining Intimacy.
- Being significantly impeded/endangered/harmed by a **Flaw**.

**Role Bonus (2)** — one per session, for ceding the spotlight to let another shine, **or** acting in your Caste's function:
- **Dawn:** defeat a powerful enemy; defend a Circlemate by arms; advance a Major/Defining Intimacy through martial prowess.
- **Zenith:** inspire others to uphold a Principle; endure hardship for an Intimacy; build/defend an institution that furthers a Principle.
- **Twilight:** learn lost lore; uncover a supernatural being's secret; solve a crisis through knowledge; create a lasting mystic work (working, artifact, binding).
- **Night:** remove an obstacle via assassination/blackmail/stealth; steal something that advances goals; gain advantage by infiltration.
- **Eclipse:** broker accords; gain advantage via diplomacy; thwart social/legal obstacles; shift someone's Intimacies toward the Circle.

## Spending — Costs *(Core Rulebook › Character Advancement)*

| Trait | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Solar Charm** | 10 xp (**8** if Caste/Favored Ability) |
| **Martial Arts Charm** | 10 xp (**8** if Brawl is Caste/Favored) |
| **Spell** | 10 xp (**8** if Occult is Caste/Favored) |
| **Evocation** | (per artifact; treat as Charm-tier) |
| **Attribute** | **current rating × 4** |
| **Ability — non-Caste/Favored** | current × 2 |
| **Ability — Caste/Favored** | (current × 2) − 1 |
| **New Ability** (0→1) | 3 |
| **Specialty** | 3 |
| **Purchased Merit** | new rating × 3 |
| **Willpower** | 8 |

*Caste/Favored Charm discount is 8, not 10. Attribute cost uses **current** rating: 2→3 costs 8 (=2×4).*

## Essence — rises automatically *(Core Rulebook › Mote Pools / Essence advancement)*

Essence is **not bought**. It becomes eligible once the character has earned-and-spent enough **xp** (solar xp does **not** count). Raising it still wants a dramatic moment or a period of contemplative seclusion (days→months).

| Essence Rating | Total xp needed (cumulative) |
|---|---|
| 2 | 50 |
| 3 | 125 |
| 4 | 200 |
| 5 | 300 |
| 6+ | (beyond core scope) |

*(Advanced-Solar option from Ch.3: reduce each threshold by 50.)*

## Training Times *(Core Rulebook › Training Times)*

Rough guidelines, not hard quotas. Mentors/teachers and full-time focus shorten them; long neglect lengthens them. Charms usually "train" by exercising the Ability under duress; **MA Charms always need rigorous training**; spells need a mentor or a text copy + many hours of study; Evocations need rapport with the artifact (often in battle).

| Trait | Training Time |
|---|---|
| Attribute | (new rating) **months** |
| Ability — non-Caste/Favored | (new rating) **weeks** |
| Ability — Caste/Favored | (new rating) **days** |
| Specialty | 2 weeks |
| Purchased Merit | (new rating) weeks |
| Willpower | 1 month |

## Merits Gained (and Lost) in Play *(Core Rulebook › Standard Merits — Story; Character Creation Step 5)*

- **Purchased Merits** are bought with xp/solar xp at **new rating × 3** (training: (new rating) weeks).
- **Story Merits** (Allies, Cult, Artifact, Mentor, Manse, etc.) **wax and wane with the narrative** rather than being purchased: recover a lost daiklave from a tomb → gain **Artifact •••**; a jealous god's cultists slaughter your faithful → **Cult ••• → ••**. Often the payoff of a **Leadership project** or a craft project's objective. ST grants/revises these as events dictate.

## Pacing note (solo) *(Core Rulebook › Storyteller Characters and Advancement)*

PCs advance far faster than the setting average — most Celestial Exalts take **decades** to reach Essence 3; Essence 5 is rare even among elders. NPCs **don't track xp**; grow them by story logic, not these tables.
