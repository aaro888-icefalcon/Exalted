# Sorcery & Sorcerous Workings — Live Adjudication

Sorcery shapes the world's Essence (not the caster's own pools). Two forms: **spells** (discrete, costed) and **sorcerous workings** (long projects). *(Core Rulebook › Sorcery; Spells and Sorcerous Workings)*

---

## The Three Circles *(Core Rulebook › The Three Circles)*

| Circle (Realm name) | Who can reach it |
|---|---|
| **Terrestrial** (Emerald) | Anyone: mortals, Dragon-Blooded, Liminals, all Exalted |
| **Celestial** (Sapphire) | Solars, Lunars, Sidereals |
| **Solar** (Adamant) | **Solars only** |

Solars learn the circle-Charms in order (Terrestrial → Celestial → Solar; first is the Terrestrial Circle Sorcery Charm). Abyssals use **necromancy** instead. Each Charm that initiates a circle grants a free **shaping ritual**.

## Sorcerous Motes & the Shape Sorcery Action *(Core Rulebook › Sorcerous Motes and the Shape Sorcery Action)*

Spells are paid in **sorcerous motes (sm)**, gathered fresh each casting — never from personal pools, never committed.

**Shape Sorcery action** (combat action, **not** flurryable):
1. Declare the spell.
2. Roll **(Intelligence + Occult)**; each success = **1 sorcerous mote** toward the cost.
3. When accumulated motes meet the cost → spell fires **immediately & reflexively**.
4. Below cost → keep shaping over later turns.

- **Decay:** any round you gather **0** motes, you lose **3** at end of round (down to 0 = spell lost).
- **Switching spells** mid-shape → current spell aborts, all gathered motes lost.
- **"Ritual" cost** = an extended shape over hours+; track *time*, not motes.
- **Willpower:** most spells cost ≥1 WP, paid up front. Successful cast **refunds 1 WP**. Lost/aborted/countered → WP gone.
- **Casting in Crash:** no WP refund, and **+3 sm** to every spell while crashed.
- **Control spell:** every sorcerer has ≥1 signature spell; many spells and rituals give it bonuses.

## Shaping Rituals *(Core Rulebook › Shaping Rituals; Multiple Shaping Rituals)*

Alternate ways to gain sorcerous motes — a sorcerer's unique path (a pact, a relic, a taboo). Solars gain more as they rise through circles; not limited to the original archetype. The five sample archetypes (each yields rituals + purchasable Merits):
- **Bargain with Mara** — drain motes from unrequited love-Ties toward you; bind souls.
- **Pact with an Ifrit Lord** — draw motes from nearby fire; pray for motes with burnt offerings.
- **Scarred by Nightmares** — a Defining Derangement; feed on others' emotional Intimacies & on Limit.
- **Soul-Perfecting Elixir** — brew alchemical draughts; asceticism yields motes after sleep.
- **Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes** — a 4-dot relic that stores committed motes and sharpens senses.

Mortal sorcerers buy a shaping ritual as a **5-dot Merit (needs Occult 3)**; first spell learned = control spell. *(Core Rulebook › Mortal Sorcerers and Shaping Rituals)*

## Interrupting Casting

**Countermagic** *(Core Rulebook › Countermagic)* — unravel a spell as it's shaped. (Intelligence + Occult), within **short range**, must be **initiated into that circle**. Every **2 successes drain 1 sm** (1-for-1 if you know the spell). Reduce a spell's motes to **0** → casting broken; the enemy also **can't shape sorcery next turn**. Combat action, not flurryable.

**Distortion** *(Core Rulebook › Countermagic — Distortion)* — twist/weaken an *already-cast* spell (can't undo it). Extended (Intelligence + Occult); difficulty by circle: **Terrestrial 1 / Celestial 3 / Solar 5**. Goal number & effect per spell text. Terminus 5 rolls. Short range, must be initiated; **−2** if you don't know the spell. Instant or independent-summon spells can't be distorted.

**Undoing a spell** — fully revoke a long-term spell via an **Ambition 3 working** of the same circle. *(Core Rulebook › Undoing a Spell)*

## Sorcerous Workings *(Core Rulebook › Sorcerous Workings)*

Permanent reshaping of the world. Define intent, then set three traits:

- **Ambition (1–3)** → **goal number** (separate scale per circle; see tables below).
- **Finesse (1, 3, or 5)** → **difficulty** of each roll. Higher Finesse = the *player* dictates how the effect manifests; lower = the *ST* does.
- **Means** → **terminus** (rolls allowed). Base terminus **5**; each Means **+1 roll** (stack).

Enact: extended **(Intelligence + Occult)**, base **interval 1 week**. Then pay XP to finalize.

**Means** (each +1 roll unless noted): complementary Ability (5, or 3+specialty; +2 if heavily Charm-backed), complementary known Spell, Extra Time (interval→month +1, →cycle +2, →year +3), Exotic Components (rare → +2), Cooperating sorcerer/spirit/occult-group (one source only), Sorcerous Infrastructure (First Age → +2).

**XP to finalize:** Ambition 1 = **2xp**, Ambition 2 = **4xp**, Ambition 3 = **8xp**. Casting a working **below** your mastery: **−2xp per circle of difference** (min 1xp). If a working is later made irrelevant, the XP is **refunded** at end of story.

**Botches don't auto-ruin** (unlike normal extended actions): on a successful working, each botch = one ST **complication**; on a failed working, each botch = one **disaster**. Dropping Finesse mid-project, or losing a Means, each counts as one botch. *(Core Rulebook › Sorcerous Workings and Botches; Losing Means)*

**Beyond the boundaries** (working a circle you haven't mastered): difficulty **+2 per circle above** you; every failed interval = a botch; interval defaults to 3 months (1 above) or 1 year (2 above); XP **+4 per unmastered circle**; Terrestrial sorcerers can't attempt Solar Ambition 3. *(Core Rulebook › Beyond the Boundaries)*

**Stopping an enemy working:** can't be countered/distorted once done — only an opposing working. Easier to sabotage Means or kill the caster. Spotting one in progress: diff 3 (Int+Occult) to notice, diff 5 to ID the effect (−1 if you're a sorcerer, −1 more if you saw the ritual). *(Core Rulebook › Undoing a Sorcerous Working)*

### Ambition → Goal Number by circle *(Core Rulebook › Sorcerous Workings — Ambition)*

| Circle | Amb 1 | Amb 2 | Amb 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrestrial | 5 | 10 | 20 |
| Celestial | 25 | 30 | 35 |
| Solar | 40 | 50 | 75 |

- **Terrestrial:** transform/enhance/weaken existing nature; bound or constrained supernatural forces. (e.g. ward a town vs. forest fires; grant mutations; bless a region's harvest.)
- **Celestial:** overt supernatural power on a village/district scale. (e.g. mind-link two people; alter a region's weather; reshape a willing demon; create a minion as strong as a 2nd Circle demon.)
- **Solar:** rewrite reality. (e.g. terraform a region; make a city fly; alter a region's afterlife; remake a being's very soul.)

**Hard limits** *(Core Rulebook › The Limits of Sorcery)*: immortality always has a catch; **no resurrection**; **no time travel**.

> **Worked example.** Ivory Eyes (Celestial) wants her workshop mobile & intelligent → ST rules **Ambition 3 Celestial = goal 35**. Means: Craft 5 + Craft Charms → +2 rolls, so **terminus 7**. She sets **Finesse 3** (diff 3 each roll) so she dictates the result. Over 7 weeks she hits 35 successes but botches once → ST adds one complication (the bound elemental falls obsessively in love with her). Pays **8xp**. *(Core Rulebook › A Sample Working)*

## Iconic Spells (name + gist) — full text in vault *(Core Rulebook › Terrestrial/Celestial/Solar Circle Spells)*

> Stat lines and complete rules live in `vault/Exalted 3e - Core Rulebook.md`. These are quick-reference gists only.

**Terrestrial Circle** (cost ~5–20sm, 1–2 WP):
- **Cirrus Skiff** — conjure a small flying cloud-boat.
- **Corrupted Words** *(Psyche)* — magically prevent a target from revealing a secret.
- **Death of Obsidian Butterflies** *(Decisive-only, Perilous)* — a storm of razor butterflies; area decisive attack.
- **Demon of the First Circle** *(Ritual, 2wp)* — summon & bind a 1st Circle demon (culminates at midnight).
- **Flight of the Brilliant Raptor** *(Decisive-only, Perilous)* — hurl a blazing essence-hawk as a decisive attack.
- **Infallible Messenger** — send a spoken message to anyone you know, anywhere.
- **Invulnerable Skin of Bronze** — +6 natural soak & Hardness 6; counts as medium armor.
- **Stormwind Rider** — ride a dust-devil mount up to ~100 mph through terrain.
- **Summon Elemental** *(Ritual)* — call & bind a single elemental.

**Celestial Circle** (cost ~15–30sm, 1–2 WP):
- **Cantata of Empty Voices** *(Perilous)* — wraith-choir: expanding environmental hazard (Damage 2L).
- **Demon of the Second Circle** *(Ritual)* — summon a 2nd Circle demon (new moon).
- **Impenetrable Veil of Night** — blanket an area in absolute magical darkness.
- **Incomparable Body Arsenal** — grow living essence-weapons from the body.
- **Magma Kraken** — erupt 10 lava tentacles out to long range to grapple/attack.
- **Travel Without Distance** — teleport to any seen location within (Essence×10) miles.

**Solar Circle** (cost ~25–40sm or Ritual, 2–4 WP):
- **Benediction of Archgenesis** *(Ritual)* — make (Essence×100) sq mi of barren land fertile.
- **Death Ray** *(Aggravated, Decisive-only, Perilous)* — line decisive attack of red Essence; melts mundane weapons.
- **Demon of the Third Circle** *(Ritual, 4wp)* — summon a 3rd Circle demon.
- **Rain of Doom** *(Aggravated)* — apocalyptic storm over a small city; called at sunset, climaxes at sunrise.
