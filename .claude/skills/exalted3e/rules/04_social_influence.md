# Social Influence — Live Adjudication

The core resolution: **influence roll** = (social Attribute: Charisma/Manipulation/Appearance + relevant Ability) vs. target's **Resolve**. Beat Resolve = target is moved. *(Core Rulebook › Social Influence)*

---

## Intimacies *(Core Rulebook › Intimacies; Step 6: Intimacies and Limit Trigger)*

An Intimacy = a relationship, belief, ideal, or goal. Two kinds:
- **Tie** — directed at a person/place/thing/group, with an emotion in parens: `My Wife (Love)`, `The Realm (Hatred)`.
- **Principle** — a belief or ideal: `My word is my bond`, `The Immaculate Philosophy`.

**Intensity** sets how hard it pushes Resolve. Use only the **single most relevant** Intimacy per roll.

| Intensity | Opposes influence (+Resolve) | Supports influence (−Resolve) |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | +2 | −1 |
| Major | +3 | −2 |
| Defining | +4 | −3 |

- **Minor** = matters in directly-relevant situations only. Won't override self-interest or a stronger Intimacy.
- **Major** = shapes behavior across life; will act against self-interest short of severe/life-altering harm.
- **Defining** = placed above all else; will lay down life for it.

Support and opposition **cancel**: Defining Tie supporting (−3) + Major Principle opposing (+3) → net 0 to Resolve. *(Core Rulebook › Intimacies)*

**Contradictory Intimacies are fine** (love + fear the same person). They each apply normally. *(Core Rulebook › Contradictory Intimacies)*

---

## Resolve & Guile (derive on the fly) *(Core Rulebook › Resolve; Guile)*

- **Resolve** = round_up((Wits + Integrity + specialty) / 2). Difficulty for instill, inspire, persuade, bargain, threaten. Only rolled when the target *resists*.
- **Guile** = round_up((Manipulation + Socialize + specialty) / 2). Difficulty for **read intentions**.
- Target unaware he's observed: **−2 Guile**.

**Appearance edge:** if attacker's Appearance > target's Resolve, attacker gets **+(difference) dice** on instill & persuade. Compare *before* Intimacy modifiers. (Group: compare to average Resolve, usually 2–3.) *(Core Rulebook › Appearance)*

## The Influence Actions *(Core Rulebook › Social Actions)*

**Read Intentions** — NOT an influence roll. (Perception + Socialize) vs. **Guile**. Success → learn one goal ("he's seducing you to get you alone") OR one Intimacy fitting a stated criterion ("does he love anyone?"). Not magic; ST gates what's deducible. Needed to learn what an `inspire` actually provoked.

**Instill** — change feelings/beliefs (create/strengthen/weaken Intimacies). ST may penalize implausible claims up to **−5**.
- Always free to create a new **Minor** Intimacy.
- Strengthen Minor→Major, or weaken Major→Minor: needs a *different* Minor+ Intimacy supporting it.
- Strengthen Major→Defining, or weaken Defining→Major: needs a *different* Major+ Intimacy supporting it.
- Strengthening requires evidence stronger than what set the current level.

**Persuade** — make the target *do* a task. Ceiling depends on the **supporting Intimacy** being exploited:
- No supporting Intimacy → only trivial, risk-free acts (begging a coin).
- **Minor** Intimacy → inconvenient/mildly dangerous tasks (mild injury, angry boss, modest loss); can exceed one scene if not life-disrupting.
- **Major** Intimacy → serious tasks risking extreme harm (join a militia; hand over poison despite prison) — balks only at near-certain death/ruin; long-term commitments OK.
- **Defining** Intimacy → almost anything: dedicate one's life, die in your name. Balks only at *absolutely unavoidable* death/utter ruin.

**Bargain** — like persuade but **ignores Intimacies**; instead offer a bribe/gift/favor the target judges worth the task's difficulty/danger. Weigh by target's wealth & status (apple sways a beggar, not a noble).

**Threaten (intimidate)** — like bargain but offers something the target *doesn't* want (bodily harm, ruin, blackmail). Works only if fear of refusing > fear of the task. Can double as instill of a fear-Tie toward you. **Always gives the target a negative Tie toward you** (context chosen by them) whether it succeeds or fails, and may weaken their positive Ties.

**Inspire** — incite an emotion (usually Performance) the target then acts on. On success the *target's player* chooses the form (new passion or acting on an aligned Intimacy). An inflamed passion counts as a **Major Intimacy** for Resolve/persuade while acted on. Inspirer doesn't know what he provoked — must **read intentions** to find out. **No group penalty** (it isn't tailored to a target).

## Resolving Resistance — Willpower & Decision Points *(Core Rulebook › Resisting Influence Rolls; Decision Points)*

If the influence **fails to beat Resolve** → target is simply unmoved.

If it **beats Resolve**, the target may still spend Willpower:

**Changing feelings** (create/destroy/weaken Intimacy, or an inspire) — spend **1 WP** to:
- Stop a new Intimacy forming, OR
- Stop a Major/Defining Intimacy being weakened, OR
- Reject a successful inspire.

**Making the target ACT** (persuade/bargain/threaten task) → a **Decision Point**:
1. Target picks an Intimacy and argues it justifies refusing *this* influence.
2. That Intimacy must be **≥ the intensity of the supporting Intimacy**, AND must **not** be the one used to boost Resolve (that one was already overcome).
3. If ST accepts the argument → spend **1 WP** to resist. Otherwise resistance is impossible.

> **Worked example.** Naya (Sidereal) persuades Son of Wolves to betray his Circle (serious task), exploiting his Major Principle of the Immaculate Faith. His Resolve is beaten. His Defining Tie of loyalty to his Circle is *unavailable* — he used it to raise Resolve. He cites a Major Principle "I can't abide a traitor" (Major ≥ Major, different Intimacy). ST agrees → 1 WP, he resists. *(Core Rulebook › Decision Points)*

**Wearing them down:** the initiate can retry with a *new* argument/Intimacy. Each Decision Point burns one valid Intimacy for the rest of the story. When the target runs out of valid Intimacies, beating Resolve finally convinces him.

## When Influence CAN'T Force Action (Unacceptable Influence) *(Core Rulebook › Unacceptable Influence)*

Target rejects outright, **no WP needed**, even if Resolve would've been beaten:
- Anything that would make him **kill himself** or do something he knows means certain death.
- Anything that would **completely abandon/end a Defining Intimacy** (you *can* first weaken Defining→Major, then try).
- Instill that strengthens/weakens an Intimacy without exploiting a strong-enough supporting Intimacy.
- Persuade not backed by a strong-enough Intimacy.
- A bargain with an inadequate incentive, or a threat that isn't actually threatening.
- Any **seduction violating the target's orientation**; PCs can never be put in a sexual situation without the player's OK (Red Rule). *(Core Rulebook › Seduction: The Red Rule)*

Only **Psyche-keyword** magic / mind control can override these. Influence is persuasion, never mind control. *(Core Rulebook › What Does Influence Mean?)*

## NPC Willpower (don't bog down) *(Core Rulebook › NPCs and Willpower)*

Minor mortals usually **don't** spend WP to resist — let them be swayed. Significant NPCs spend WP based on the intensity of the *opposing* Intimacy (high → likely resists). No opposing Intimacy → unlikely to resist.

---

## Complications *(Core Rulebook › Social Complications)*

- **Groups:** one roll vs. many = **−3 penalty**; resolve success **per target** (each has own Resolve).
- **Mass audience** of nameless mortals: treat as one entity, Resolve 1–2 (3–5 for ascetics). Skip WP; just rule what % is convinced by alignment with their Intimacies. Anyone with own stats resists individually. *(Core Rulebook › Playing to the Audience)*
- **Written** influence: Ability is always **Linguistics**; ST sets read time. *(Core Rulebook › Social Complications)*
- **Gestures/body language only:** target **+2 Resolve** (not for dance-inspire).
- **Overturning prior influence:** the already-persuaded target gets **+3 Resolve** vs. anything reversing it (stacks with Intimacy bonus); the new petitioner must spend **1 WP** before rolling. Lasts one story. *(Core Rulebook › Social Complications — Overturning Influence)*

---

## Social Actions in Combat *(Core Rulebook › Social Actions in Combat)*

All social actions work in combat, are **combat actions** (may be placed in a flurry), resisted normally — within common sense (a fast bribe yes, a contract negotiation no). **Surrender** = persuade or bargain ("my family will ransom me"); usually needs no Intimacy (Minor/Major if the enemy has reason to want you dead).

---

## Retry Conditions (after a failure) *(Core Rulebook › Retrying Social Actions)*

| Action | Retry only if… |
|---|---|
| Instill | present substantially greater evidence |
| Persuade | new argument on a different Intimacy ≥ strength, OR next story, OR after the supporting Intimacy strengthens |
| Bargain | offer substantially greater than before |
| Threaten | significantly escalate the threat |
| Inspire | wait until the scene ends |
| Read Intentions | not again on that target this scene |

---

## Quick scene loop
1. **Read intentions** to learn goals/Intimacies (vs. Guile).
2. Pick action; identify the supporting Intimacy and confirm the task ceiling allows it.
3. Compute Resolve (+ opposing Intimacy, − supporting, ± Appearance edge, ± complications).
4. Roll (Attribute + Ability) vs. Resolve.
5. If beaten: feeling-change → maybe 1 WP; forced action → Decision Point or unacceptable check.
